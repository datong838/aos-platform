## 1. Amazon Brand Registry

### Requirements

- **Trademark:** An active registered trademark (word mark or design mark
  containing text) in the country of the marketplace where you want to enroll.
  Pending trademarks are accepted in some marketplaces (US, EU, UK, JP, CA)
  through the IP Accelerator program.
- **Trademark type:** Must be a text-based mark or image-based mark that
  contains words, letters, or numbers. Pure design marks without text are not
  accepted.
- **Marketplace account:** Active Amazon seller or vendor account.
- **Product listing:** At least one product listed (or ready to list) under
  the brand.

### Application Process

1. **Go to** Amazon Brand Services (`brandservices.amazon.com`).
2. **Sign in** with your Amazon seller account credentials.
3. **Enter brand information:**
   - Brand name (must match the trademark exactly).
   - Trademark registration number.
   - Product categories where the brand is listed.
   - Countries where products are manufactured and distributed.
4. **Submit for verification.** Amazon sends a verification code to the
   trademark registration contact on file with the trademark office.
5. **Enter the verification code** within 30 days.
6. **Approval:** Typically 2-4 weeks after verification code submission.

### Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **A+ Content** | Enhanced product descriptions with rich media modules (images, comparison charts, video). Increases conversion 3-10%. |
| **Brand Analytics** | Search term reports, demographics, market basket analysis, repeat purchase behavior. |
| **Brand Store** | Multi-page storefront with custom URL. Free to create. |
| **Vine** | Invite trusted reviewers to review new products. Costs per-enrollment. |
| **Transparency** | Per-unit barcode program to prevent counterfeits from entering the supply chain. |
| **Project Zero** | Self-service counterfeit removal. Remove infringing listings without filing a report. |
| **Sponsored Brands** | Headline search ads featuring brand logo, custom headline, and multiple products. |
| **Manage Your Experiments** | A/B test A+ content, product titles, and images to optimize conversion. |
| **Brand Referral Bonus** | Earn a referral bonus (avg 10% of sales) when driving external traffic via Amazon Attribution. |
| **Virtual Bundles** | Create bundles from existing FBA inventory without repackaging. |
| **Subscribe & Save** | Priority access to subscription program for eligible products. |

### Tips

- File your trademark through Amazon IP Accelerator if you need brand
  registry access before the trademark is fully registered (costs more but
  saves 6-12 months of waiting).
- Enroll in every marketplace where you sell. Brand Registry enrollment is
  per-marketplace but uses the same trademark.
- After enrollment, immediately enable Transparency on high-risk ASINs
  to prevent hijacking.

## 2. Amazon Brand Store Setup

### Pages and Structure

A well-structured brand store typically includes:

| Page | Purpose | Key Elements |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Home** | First impression, brand overview | Hero banner, value prop, best sellers, category tiles |
| **Category 1-3** | Product browsing by category | Product grids, lifestyle imagery, sub-category navigation |
| **Best Sellers** | Social proof, high-converting products | Top-rated products with star ratings, "Most Popular" labels |
| **About Us** | Brand story and trust building | Origin story, mission, certifications, team photos |
| **New Arrivals** | Freshness, repeat visitor engagement | Latest launches, "Just Dropped" messaging |
| **Deals** (optional) | Seasonal/promotional | Coupon-eligible products, Lightning Deal callouts |

### Widgets and Modules

Amazon Brand Store builder provides these widget types:

- **Hero Image:** Full-width banner with optional text overlay. Use for
  seasonal campaigns and brand messaging.
- **Product Grid:** Display 4-8 products in a grid. Best for category pages.
- **Image with Text:** Lifestyle photo with descriptive text. Good for
  storytelling sections.
- **Shoppable Image:** Hotspot image where customers click products within a
  lifestyle scene.
- **Video:** Autoplaying brand video (up to 5 minutes). Place on home page
  above the fold.
- **Best Sellers:** Auto-populated from your catalog. Shows top-selling items.
- **Recommended Products:** Personalized recommendations based on browsing
  history.
- **Product Tile:** Single product highlight with image, title, price, and
  rating.

### A+ Content Modules

For product detail pages (not the store), A+ Content modules include:

- **Standard Comparison Chart:** Compare up to 5 of your own products.
  Drives cross-selling within your brand.
- **Standard Image and Text:** Left/right image with descriptive text.
- **Standard Four Image and Text Quadrant:** Four image/text pairs in a grid.
- **Standard Tech Specs:** Table format for technical specifications.
- **Standard Company Logo:** Brand logo with text description.
- **Premium A+ (Brand Story):** Carousel module linking to brand store.
  Available to Brand Registry members.

### A+ Content Optimization

1. **Lead with the primary customer benefit.** Do not lead with features.
2. **Use comparison charts** to show why your premium SKU is worth the
   upgrade over your standard SKU.
3. **"What's in the box" module** reduces "item not as described" returns
   by 10-15% on average.
4. **Alt text on every image** improves accessibility and contributes to
   search indexing.
5. **A/B test with Manage Your Experiments.** Test headline images, benefit
   ordering, and comparison chart layouts. Run tests for at least 8 weeks
   for statistical significance.
6. **Mobile preview every module.** Over 70% of Amazon traffic is mobile.
   Text-heavy modules that look good on desktop may be unreadable on mobile.

## 3. Shopee Mall

### Requirements

- **Business license:** Valid business registration in the seller's country
  of operation.
- **Trademark certificate:** Registered trademark for the brand.
- **Brand authorization:** If the seller is not the trademark owner, a brand
  authorization letter from the trademark holder is required.
- **Sales history:** Some markets require a minimum sales track record on
  Shopee (varies by market, typically 3+ months).
- **Performance metrics:** Low cancellation rate, low late shipment rate,
  adequate customer service response time.

### Application Process

1. Log in to Shopee Seller Centre.
2. Navigate to **Shop Settings > Shopee Mall Application**.
3. Upload required documents (business license, trademark certificate,
   authorization letter).
4. Submit for review.
5. **Review period:** 5-10 business days.
6. If approved, Mall badge is applied to the shop and all listings.

### Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Mall Badge** | Trust signal displayed on shop and product listings. Increases click-through rates. |
| **Priority Search Ranking** | Mall products rank higher in search results for relevant queries. |
| **Campaign Access** | Priority access to Shopee Mall-exclusive campaigns (brand days, mega sales). |
| **Buyer Trust** | 15-day "100% Authentic" return guarantee handled by Shopee. Reduces buyer friction. |
| **Brand Microsite** | Dedicated brand page within Shopee Mall. |
| **Marketing Tools** | Access to Mall-specific voucher tools and flash sale slots. |

### Tips

- Apply during non-peak periods. Review times increase significantly during
  mega sale preparation periods (9.9, 11.11, 12.12).
- Ensure all product images meet Mall quality standards (white background,
  high resolution, no watermarks) before applying.
- Some Shopee markets (e.g., Shopee MY, Shopee SG) have invitation-only Mall
  programs. Build a sales track record first, then request an invitation
  through your Shopee account manager.

## 4. TikTok Shop Brand Program

### Requirements

- **Trademark registration:** Valid trademark certificate in the market
  where you operate the TikTok Shop.
- **Business license:** Valid business registration.
- **Brand authorization:** Required if the seller is not the trademark
  owner. Must be notarized in some markets.
- **Product quality:** Products must meet TikTok Shop's product quality
  standards and restricted products policy.

### Application Process

1. Log in to TikTok Shop Seller Center.
2. Navigate to **Brand Program** section.
3. Upload trademark certificate, business license, and authorization
   documents.
4. Submit for review.
5. **Review period:** 3-7 business days.

### Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Brand Badge** | "Authorized Brand" badge displayed on product listings and shop page. |
| **Brand Tab** | Dedicated brand section within the shop profile. |
| **Campaign Priority** | Priority access to brand-focused campaigns and promotional events. |
| **Branded Content Tools** | Enhanced creator collaboration tools for brand content. |
| **Brand Analytics** | Access to brand-specific performance metrics and audience insights. |
| **Content Protection** | Priority handling of IP infringement reports. |

### Tips

- TikTok Shop is heavily content-driven. Brand registration alone will not
  drive sales. Pair registration with a creator/affiliate strategy and
  regular live streaming.
- Video content featuring the brand story performs 2-3x better than
  product-only content on TikTok Shop.
- Leverage the "Brand Mission" feature to recruit affiliates who promote
  your products for commission.

## 5. Lazada LazMall

### Requirements

- **Trademark certificate:** Registered trademark in the operating market
  or international registration covering the market.
- **Business registration:** Valid company registration documents.
- **Brand authorization:** If not the brand owner, an authorization letter
  or distribution agreement is required.
- **Product portfolio:** Minimum number of SKUs varies by market
  (typically 10+ SKUs).
- **Service metrics:** Must maintain Lazada's seller performance standards.

### Application Process

1. Log in to Lazada Seller Center.
2. Navigate to **LazMall Application** section.
3. Upload all required documents.
4. Submit for review.
5. **Review period:** 5-15 business days (varies by market and queue
   volume).

### Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **LazMall Badge** | Premium trust badge displayed on all listings. |
| **15-Day Returns** | LazMall return policy (handled by Lazada). Increases buyer confidence. |
| **Priority Customer Support** | Dedicated support channel for LazMall sellers. |
| **Campaign Access** | Priority in mega campaigns (6.6, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12). |
| **Brand Page** | Dedicated brand microsite within Lazada. |
| **Enhanced Listings** | Access to enriched content modules for product pages. |
| **CRM Tools** | Customer relationship management tools for repeat purchases. |

### Tips

- LazMall sellers must maintain a high chat response rate (>95%) and low
  cancellation rate (<2%). Monitor daily.
- Lazada heavily promotes LazMall products during mega campaigns. Ensure
  inventory is stocked 4-6 weeks before campaign dates.
- Some Lazada markets operate a tiered LazMall system (e.g., LazMall vs
  LazGlobal). Verify which tier you qualify for based on your business
  entity location.

## 6. Walmart Brand Portal

### Requirements

- **USPTO trademark:** Active registered trademark with the United States
  Patent and Trademark Office.
- **Walmart seller account:** Approved Walmart Marketplace seller account.
- **Product listings:** At least one active product listing on Walmart.com.

### Application Process

1. Navigate to the Walmart Brand Portal.
2. Sign in with your Walmart seller credentials.
3. Enter your brand name and USPTO trademark registration number.
4. Upload brand logo and select product categories.
5. Submit for review.
6. **Review period:** 2-4 weeks.

### Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Brand Shelf** | Curated brand landing page on Walmart.com. |
| **Enhanced Content** | Rich media product descriptions (similar to Amazon A+). |
| **IP Protection** | Access to Walmart's IP infringement reporting tools. |
| **Brand Analytics** | Brand-specific search and sales analytics. |
| **Sponsored Brands** | Brand-level advertising placements on Walmart.com. |

### Tips

- Walmart's brand program is less mature than Amazon's. Expect fewer
  features but less competition for brand visibility.
- Enhanced content (Walmart's equivalent of A+ Content) is provided through
  third-party syndication services (e.g., Salsify, Syndigo). Factor in the
  additional cost.
- Walmart.com is growing rapidly as a marketplace. Early brand registrants
  benefit from lower competition and higher organic visibility.

## 8. Timeline and Cost Estimates

### Brand Registry Application Timeline

| Platform | Pre-requisite Time | Application Review | Total Time to Active |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Amazon Brand Registry | Trademark: 8-18 months (or IP Accelerator: 2-4 weeks) | 2-4 weeks | 9-19 months (or 3-5 weeks via IP Accelerator) |
| Shopee Mall | Trademark: 8-18 months + sales history: 3 months | 5-10 business days | 11-21 months |
| TikTok Shop Brand | Trademark: 8-18 months | 3-7 business days | 8-18 months |
| LazMall | Trademark: 8-18 months | 5-15 business days | 9-19 months |
| Walmart Brand Portal | USPTO trademark: 8-12 months | 2-4 weeks | 9-13 months |

### Cost Estimates

| Cost Item | Estimated Range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| **Trademark filing (single country)** | $300-$3,000 | Varies by country. China: $300-$800. US: $750-$1,500. EU: $1,000-$2,000. |
| **Trademark filing (Madrid Protocol, 3 countries)** | $3,000-$8,000 | Includes WIPO base fee + country designations. |
| **Amazon IP Accelerator** | $2,000-$5,000 | Expedited trademark + immediate brand registry access. |
| **Brand Store design (professional)** | $500-$3,000 | One-time cost. DIY is free but time-intensive. |
| **A+ Content creation (per ASIN)** | $100-$500 | Includes design and copywriting. DIY is free. |
| **Brand photography (product line)** | $500-$2,000 | Lifestyle + product shots for 5-10 SKUs. |
| **Transparency enrollment** | $0.01-$0.05/unit | Per-unit barcode cost. Volume discounts available. |
| **Vine enrollment** | $200/parent ASIN | Amazon Vine enrollment fee. |
| **Brand Registry application** | Free | All platforms offer free brand registry enrollment. |

### Recommended Budget by Stage

| Stage | Monthly Budget | Focus |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| **Pre-brand (validation)** | $0 | No brand spend. Focus on product-market fit. |
| **Trademark filing** | One-time $1,000-$3,000 | File in primary market + China. |
| **Brand registry + basic content** | $500-$1,500 (one-time) | A+ content for top 3-5 ASINs, basic store. |
| **Brand growth** | 3-5% of revenue | Ongoing content, Sponsored Brands, store updates. |
| **Brand defense** | $100-$500/month | Transparency, monitoring tools, IP enforcement. |
