# Dispute Resolution Across Platforms

Comprehensive guide to handling disputes, claims, and chargebacks across major cross-border
e-commerce platforms. The goal is to resolve disputes efficiently, minimize financial loss,

## Amazon A-to-Z Claim Defense

The Amazon A-to-Z Guarantee protects buyers who are dissatisfied with a purchase. When a
buyer files a claim, it directly impacts your Order Defect Rate (ODR). Keeping ODR below
1% is critical to maintaining selling privileges.

### Common A-to-Z Claim Reasons

| Reason | Description | Defense Difficulty |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Item not received | Buyer claims they never received the package | Medium - depends on tracking evidence |
| Not as described | Buyer claims the item differs from the listing | Hard - subjective; listing accuracy matters |
| Defective item | Buyer says the product is damaged or non-functional | Medium - depends on defect rate data |
| Return not processed | Buyer returned the item but did not receive a refund | Easy - show return processing records |
| Late delivery | Item arrived after the estimated delivery window | Hard - carrier issues are difficult to defend |

### Evidence to Collect and Submit

For every A-to-Z claim, gather the following before responding:

1. **Order details**: Order ID, purchase date, ship date, delivery date.
2. **Tracking information**: Carrier name, tracking number, delivery confirmation
   (signature if available). Screenshot the tracking page showing delivery status.
3. **Delivery proof**: Photo of delivery (if available from carrier), GPS coordinates
   of delivery scan, signature confirmation.
4. **Communication history**: All buyer-seller messages. Screenshot the full thread
   showing your attempts to resolve the issue before the claim.
5. **Listing evidence**: Screenshot of the listing at the time of purchase showing
   accurate description, images, and specifications.
6. **Product evidence**: Inspection photos, quality control records, packaging photos
   if available.
7. **Refund/return records**: If a refund was issued or return authorized, provide
   transaction records.

### Response Template

```
Dear Amazon A-to-Z Claims Team,

Thank you for the opportunity to respond to this claim regarding
Order [Order ID].

SUMMARY OF FACTS:
- Order placed: [date]
- Order shipped: [date] via [carrier], tracking: [number]
- Delivery confirmed: [date] at [time] per carrier records

SELLER ACTIONS TAKEN:
[Describe what you did to resolve the issue before the claim was filed.
Include dates of messages, refund offers, etc.]

EVIDENCE PROVIDED:
1. [Tracking confirmation screenshot]
2. [Buyer-seller message history]
3. [Additional evidence as applicable]

RESOLUTION REQUESTED:
Based on the evidence above, we respectfully request that this claim
be denied / we have already issued a full refund on [date].

Thank you for reviewing this matter.
```

### Appeal Process

If Amazon rules against you:

1. You have 30 calendar days to appeal from the date of the decision.
2. Go to Seller Central > Performance > A-to-Z Guarantee Claims.
3. Find the claim and click "Appeal."
4. Provide new evidence or clarification not included in the original response.
5. Appeals are reviewed by a different team than the original decision.
6. You get one appeal per claim. Make it count.

### Win Rate Tips

- **Respond within 48 hours.** Late responses almost always result in a ruling
  against the seller.
- **Always attempt resolution before the claim.** Message the buyer proactively.
  Amazon favors sellers who demonstrated good-faith resolution attempts.
- **Use FBA when possible.** FBA orders where Amazon handles fulfillment shift
  A-to-Z liability to Amazon, not the seller.
- **Ship with tracking and signature confirmation** for orders above $25. Without
  delivery confirmation, "item not received" claims are nearly impossible to defend.
- **Keep your listing accurate.** "Not as described" claims are hardest to fight.
  Ensure images, dimensions, materials, and features are precisely represented.
- **Issue proactive refunds for legitimate complaints.** If the buyer has a valid
  complaint, refund before they file a claim. A refund costs the same either way,
  but a claim hurts your ODR.
- **Historical win rates by claim type**:
  - Item not received (with tracking proof): ~60-70% win rate
  - Item not received (without tracking): ~10% win rate
  - Not as described: ~30-40% win rate
  - Defective (low overall defect rate): ~40-50% win rate

## Amazon Buyer Messages

Respond to all buyer messages promptly and professionally. Amazon tracks your message
response time as part of seller performance metrics.

### Response Time Requirements

- **Target**: respond within 4 hours during business hours.
- **Maximum**: 24 hours. Messages unanswered after 24 hours count against your
  response rate metric.
- **Weekend/holiday coverage**: set up auto-responders or ensure staff coverage.

### Response Templates by Scenario

**Order status inquiry**:
```
Hello [Buyer Name],

Thank you for reaching out about your order [Order ID].

Your order was shipped on [date] via [carrier]. Here is your
tracking number: [tracking number]. You can track your package at
[carrier tracking URL].

The estimated delivery date is [date]. If you have not received
your package by [date + 2 days], please let us know and we will
look into it immediately.

Best regards,
[Brand Name] Support Team
```

**Product question (pre-purchase)**:
```
Hello [Buyer Name],

Thank you for your interest in our [product name]!

To answer your question: [direct, factual answer].

[If applicable: additional helpful context or suggestion.]

Please let us know if you have any other questions. We are happy
to help!

Best regards,
[Brand Name] Support Team
```

**Complaint / issue report**:
```
Hello [Buyer Name],

Thank you for letting us know about this issue with your [product
name]. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

[Specific acknowledgment of the reported issue.]

We would like to make this right. Here are your options:
1. Full refund -- we will process this immediately.
2. Free replacement -- we will ship a new unit right away.

Please let us know which you prefer, and we will take care of it.

Best regards,
[Brand Name] Support Team
```

### Refund Decision Framework

| Situation | Product Value < $15 | Product Value $15-50 | Product Value > $50 |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|---------------------|
| Defective / damaged | Full refund, no return needed | Full refund or replacement, no return needed | Full refund upon return, or replacement shipped first |
| Not as described | Full refund, no return needed | Full refund, return optional | Full refund upon return |
| Buyer remorse | Full refund, no return needed | Partial (30-50%) refund to keep, or full refund with return | Full refund upon return |
| Missing parts | Ship missing parts or full refund | Ship missing parts or full replacement | Ship missing parts; full replacement if unavailable |

### Communication Rules

- Never use threatening or confrontational language.
- Never suggest the buyer is lying or exaggerating.
- Never ask the buyer to contact Amazon instead of resolving directly.
- Always offer at least two resolution options.
- If you cannot resolve the issue, state what you can do (not what you cannot).
- Save all message threads. They are critical evidence if a claim is filed.

## Shopee Dispute Process

Shopee's dispute system follows a structured flow: buyer initiates, seller responds,
and Shopee mediates if needed.

### Dispute Reasons on Shopee

| Reason | Description | Seller Response Strategy |
|--------|-------------|------------------------|
| Did not receive order | Package not delivered within guarantee period | Provide tracking proof; extend guarantee if in transit |
| Incomplete / wrong product | Received wrong item, missing items, or incomplete set | Offer replacement or refund; provide packing photos if available |
| Faulty product | Product is defective or not working | Request photo evidence; offer refund or replacement |
| Product not as described | Product differs from listing | Compare listing to buyer's photos; update listing if inaccurate |
| Physical damage | Product arrived damaged | Request photos; check carrier handling; offer replacement |

### Seller Response Process

1. **Buyer opens a return/refund request**: You receive a notification in Shopee Seller
   Centre and the Seller app.
2. **Response window**: You have **2-3 days** (varies by market) to respond. If you
   do not respond, Shopee auto-approves the buyer's request.
3. **Your options**:
   - **Accept**: Agree to the return/refund. Process it promptly.
   - **Dispute**: If you disagree, provide evidence and explanation.
4. **If disputed**: Shopee reviews both sides and makes a decision within 5-7 business
   days.

### Response Template (Shopee Dispute)

```
Dear Shopee Support Team,

Regarding dispute for Order [Order Number]:

The buyer has requested a [return/refund] citing [stated reason].

OUR POSITION:
[State your position clearly and factually.]

EVIDENCE:
1. [Tracking information showing delivery]
2. [Product listing screenshot showing accurate description]
3. [Packing/shipping photos if available]
4. [Communication history with buyer]

We have attempted to resolve this with the buyer by [describe
your resolution attempts].

We respectfully request that Shopee review the evidence and
[your requested outcome].

Thank you.
```

### Shopee-Specific Tips

- **Chat response rate matters.** Shopee's algorithm factors your chat response rate
  into shop scoring. Maintain above 90%.
- **Shopee Guarantee period.** Buyers can only open disputes during the guarantee
  period (typically 7-15 days after delivery, varies by market).
- **Photo evidence.** Shopee heavily weighs photo evidence in dispute resolution.
  Ask buyers to provide photos of issues; provide your own packing/product photos.
- **Partial refunds.** Shopee supports partial refunds through the dispute process.
  Propose partial refunds for minor issues to avoid full loss.
- **Return shipping.** In most Shopee markets, return shipping is paid by the buyer
  unless the product is defective or the seller shipped the wrong item.

## TikTok Shop Dispute Handling

TikTok Shop is a newer platform with evolving dispute processes. The core framework
follows a similar pattern to other marketplaces.

### Dispute Categories

| Category | Common Issues | Key Evidence |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Delivery | Not received, late delivery | Tracking proof, carrier records |
| Product quality | Defective, broken, not working | Product inspection records, quality certificates |
| Not as described | Differs from listing or video | Listing screenshots, product photos, video content |
| Missing items | Incomplete order or accessories | Packing records, weight verification |

### TikTok Shop Dispute Flow

1. **Buyer initiates after-sale request** through the TikTok Shop order page.
2. **Seller reviews and responds** within the response window (typically 48 hours).
3. **If seller rejects**: buyer can escalate to TikTok Shop mediation.
4. **TikTok Shop reviews** evidence from both sides and issues a decision.
5. **Appeal**: limited appeal options available within 7 days of decision.

### TikTok Shop-Specific Considerations

- **Experience Score.** TikTok Shop uses an "Experience Score" that factors in return
  rates, dispute rates, and response times. A low score reduces visibility and can
  restrict selling privileges.
- **Video evidence.** Because TikTok is a video platform, buyers may post unboxing
  or complaint videos. Monitor tagged content and respond professionally. Never
  engage in public arguments on video content.
- **Creator/affiliate orders.** Disputes on orders generated through creator affiliates
  may involve the creator as a third party. Handle these carefully as creators can
  amplify issues publicly.
- **Return window.** TikTok Shop's return policy varies by market and category. Check
  current policies for your specific marketplace.

## PayPal Claim and Chargeback Defense

PayPal claims occur when buyers escalate disputes through PayPal's Resolution Center.
These are common for Shopify, independent site, or cross-platform transactions.

### PayPal Dispute Stages

| Stage | Description | Seller Action | Timeframe |
|-------|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| Inquiry | Buyer opens a dispute to communicate with seller | Respond with resolution offer | 20 days to resolve |
| Claim | Buyer or PayPal escalates to a formal claim | Submit evidence package | 10 days to respond |
| Chargeback | Buyer disputes through their bank/card issuer via PayPal | Submit evidence to PayPal for bank review | 10-20 days to respond |

### Evidence Package for PayPal Claims

Compile and submit the following through PayPal's Resolution Center:

1. **Proof of delivery**: Tracking number, carrier name, delivery confirmation.
   For items over $750 USD, signature confirmation is required for Seller Protection.
2. **Proof of item description accuracy**: Screenshots of the listing page, product
   photos, and specifications as they appeared at the time of sale.
3. **Communication records**: Messages between you and the buyer showing resolution
   attempts.
4. **Shipping records**: Shipping label, packing slip, weight records.
5. **Transaction details**: PayPal transaction ID, amount, date.

### PayPal Seller Protection Eligibility

PayPal Seller Protection covers eligible transactions against:
- Unauthorized transactions (buyer claims they did not authorize the payment).
- Item Not Received (INR) claims.

**Requirements for eligibility**:
- Ship to the address on the PayPal transaction details page.
- Respond to PayPal's documentation requests within the deadline.
- Provide valid proof of shipment (INR) or proof of delivery (unauthorized transaction).
- The transaction must be for a physical, tangible good.

### PayPal Response Template

```
Dear PayPal Resolution Team,

Case ID: [Case Number]
Transaction ID: [Transaction ID]

SUMMARY:
The buyer has filed a [dispute/claim/chargeback] claiming [stated reason].

EVIDENCE:
1. Proof of shipment: [Carrier], tracking [number], shipped [date]
2. Proof of delivery: Delivered [date] at [time] per carrier records
3. Item description: [Link to listing or screenshot]
4. Communication: [Summary of buyer contact and resolution attempts]

RESOLUTION:
[State your position and requested outcome.]

I have attached supporting documentation for your review.

Thank you for your assistance.
```

## Credit Card Chargeback Prevention and Response

Credit card chargebacks occur when a buyer disputes a charge directly with their card
issuer. These are more costly than platform disputes because they incur chargeback fees
($15-25 per occurrence) and affect your merchant account standing.

### Chargeback Reason Codes

| Code Category | Common Reasons | Prevention Strategy |
|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Fraud | Unauthorized use of card | Use fraud detection tools, AVS, CVV verification |
| Consumer disputes | Not as described, not received | Accurate listings, tracking, delivery confirmation |
| Processing errors | Duplicate charge, wrong amount | Verify charges before processing |
| Authorization | No authorization obtained | Ensure proper payment processing flow |

### Prevention Strategies

1. **Clear billing descriptor.** Ensure your business name on the credit card
   statement matches your store name. Unrecognized charges are the #1 trigger
   for "friendly fraud" chargebacks.

2. **Shipping confirmation with tracking.** Send tracking information immediately
   after shipment. Use carriers that provide delivery confirmation.

3. **Clear return policy.** Display your return policy prominently on the site,
   in order confirmation emails, and on packing slips. Make it easy for customers
   to contact you for returns rather than filing a chargeback.

4. **Responsive customer service.** Many chargebacks happen because the buyer
   could not reach the seller. Respond to inquiries within 24 hours. Provide
   multiple contact methods.

5. **Accurate product descriptions.** Ensure images, dimensions, materials, and
   features precisely represent the product. "Not as described" chargebacks are
   common and difficult to fight if the listing is inaccurate.

6. **Fraud detection tools.** Use your payment processor's fraud tools (Stripe
   Radar, PayPal fraud filters, etc.) to flag suspicious transactions before
   fulfillment.

### Chargeback Response Process

1. **Receive chargeback notification** from your payment processor.
2. **Review the reason code** to understand the specific claim.
3. **Gather evidence** (same evidence types as PayPal claims above).
4. **Submit representment** (your rebuttal) within the processor's deadline
   (typically 7-21 days depending on the card network).
5. **Wait for decision.** The issuing bank reviews evidence and decides (30-75 days).
6. **Accept or escalate.** If you lose, you may have pre-arbitration or arbitration
   options, but these incur additional fees ($250-500).

### Chargeback Evidence Checklist

| Evidence Type | Description | Required For |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| Transaction receipt | Payment confirmation with amount, date, card details | All chargebacks |
| AVS/CVV match | Address Verification and CVV match results | Fraud claims |
| Delivery confirmation | Tracking showing delivered to billing/shipping address | Not received claims |
| Customer communication | Email/chat logs showing buyer acknowledged receipt or satisfaction | All disputes |
| Product description | Listing page screenshot or archived version | Not as described |
| Return policy | Copy of policy as displayed at time of purchase | Consumer disputes |
| Signed delivery | Signature confirmation for high-value items | Fraud and not received |
| IP/device data | Buyer's IP address, device fingerprint matching shipping address region | Fraud claims |

## Return Reason Analysis

Systematically analyzing return reasons separates operational problems from buyer behavior
patterns and reveals the highest-impact improvement opportunities.

### Return Reason Categories

| Category | Examples | Root Cause Area | Action Owner |
|----------|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| Product defect | Broken, non-functional, missing parts | Manufacturing / QC | Supplier + QC team |
| Damage in transit | Crushed, scratched, wet | Packaging / logistics | Packaging + shipping team |
| Not as described | Wrong size, color, material, features | Listing accuracy | Listing team |
| Buyer remorse | Changed mind, found cheaper, no longer needed | Normal behavior | None (accept baseline) |
| Wrong item sent | Received different product | Warehouse / fulfillment | Operations team |
| Size/fit issue | Too small, too large | Listing + sizing info | Listing team |
| Arrived too late | Needed by a date, arrived after | Shipping speed | Logistics team |
| Compatibility | Does not work with buyer's device/setup | Listing + product info | Listing team |

### Analysis Process

1. **Collect data.** Export return reasons from each platform's seller dashboard monthly.
2. **Categorize.** Assign each return to one of the categories above.
3. **Calculate rates.** Return rate = returns / total units sold, by category and SKU.
4. **Identify patterns.** Sort by frequency and cost impact.
5. **Prioritize.** Focus on the top 3 return reasons by volume or cost.
6. **Act.** Assign action items to the responsible team with deadlines.
7. **Track.** Monitor return rate trends monthly to verify improvements.

### Return Rate Benchmarks

| Category | Acceptable | Concerning | Critical |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Overall return rate | < 3% | 3-5% | > 5% |
| Product defect | < 1% | 1-2% | > 2% |
| Not as described | < 1% | 1-2% | > 2% |
| Damage in transit | < 0.5% | 0.5-1% | > 1% |

### Identifying Product Issues vs Buyer Behavior

Some returns are inherent to e-commerce (buyer remorse, impulse purchases). Others signal
fixable problems. Distinguish between them:

**Product or operational issue** (fixable):
- Multiple returns citing the same defect or issue.
- Return reason matches a known product limitation.
- Return rate for a specific SKU is significantly higher than category average.
- Returns increase after a supplier batch change.

**Buyer behavior** (baseline, not fixable):
- Return reasons are diverse with no pattern.
- Return rate is in line with category averages.
- Returns correlate with promotional periods (impulse buyers).
- Buyer has a history of frequent returns across sellers.

## When to Accept Loss vs When to Fight

Not every dispute is worth fighting. Use this cost-benefit framework to decide.

### Decision Matrix

| Factor | Accept the Loss | Fight the Dispute |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Claim amount** | < $20 | > $50 |
| **Evidence strength** | Weak (no tracking, listing ambiguous) | Strong (tracking, delivery proof, clear listing) |
| **Buyer history** | First-time buyer, legitimate complaint | Repeat offender, suspicious pattern |
| **Account health impact** | ODR/metrics still healthy | Close to threshold, every claim matters |
| **Time cost** | Staff time to fight > claim value | Claim value justifies time investment |
| **Precedent** | Isolated incident | Pattern that will recur if not addressed |
| **Public visibility** | Private dispute only | Public review attached; others watching |

### Cost of Fighting a Dispute

Calculate the true cost before deciding:

```
Fight cost = Staff time (hours x hourly rate)
           + Evidence gathering time
           + Potential appeal fees (chargebacks)
           + Risk of negative review escalation
           + Opportunity cost (time not spent on revenue-generating activities)

Accept cost = Refund/claim amount
            + Return shipping (if applicable)
            + Impact on ODR/metrics (quantify if near threshold)
            + Product loss (if not returned)
```

**Rule of thumb**:
- Under $20: almost always cheaper to accept and refund.
- $20-50: fight only if evidence is strong and you have a clear case.
- Over $50: fight if you have any reasonable evidence. The dollar value justifies
  the time investment.
- Any amount near ODR/metric threshold: fight to protect account health.

### When to Always Fight

- Obvious fraud or abuse (buyer claims not received but tracking shows delivery).
- Serial return abuse (buyer has pattern of free-product schemes).
- Competitor sabotage (fake claims, coordinated attacks).
- Claims that would push you over a metric threshold (ODR > 1%).

### When to Always Accept

- Legitimate product defect (your fault; fix the root cause).
- Legitimate shipping failure (carrier lost the package; claim from carrier instead).
- Small amount where evidence is ambiguous.
- Customer who has been a repeat buyer (retain the relationship).

## Escalation to Platform Support

When standard dispute processes fail or when you face systemic issues, escalate to
platform support teams directly.

### Amazon Seller Support

**How to contact**:
1. Seller Central > Help (bottom left) > Contact Us.
2. Select the relevant category (Account, Orders, Products, etc.).
3. Choose Email, Phone, or Chat.

**What to prepare**:
- Specific case IDs, order IDs, or ASIN numbers.
- Clear description of the issue in 2-3 sentences.
- Evidence files ready to attach or reference.
- Your desired outcome stated explicitly.

**Escalation path**:
1. First contact: Seller Support associate (Level 1).
2. If unresolved: request escalation to a "Captive Team" or specialist.
3. If still unresolved: email jeff@amazon.com (Executive Seller Relations).
   This is a real escalation path but should be used sparingly and only for
   significant account-level issues.
4. Brand Registry members: use the Brand Support line for faster, more
   knowledgeable assistance.

**Tips**:
- Be concise and factual. Long, emotional messages reduce effectiveness.
- Reference specific policies or guidelines that support your case.
- If you get an unhelpful response, open a new case and try again. Different
  associates have different levels of authority and knowledge.
- Keep a log of all case IDs and outcomes for reference.

### Shopee Seller Support

**How to contact**:
1. Shopee Seller Centre > Help Centre > Contact Us.
2. Use the chat function (fastest) or submit a ticket.
3. For urgent issues: use the Seller app's support chat.

**Escalation path**:
1. First contact: Seller Centre chat support.
2. If unresolved: request escalation to a "Senior Agent" or "Dispute Team."
3. If still unresolved: contact your Shopee account manager (if assigned).
4. For systematic issues: email the Shopee seller operations team for your market.

### TikTok Shop Seller Support

**How to contact**:
1. TikTok Seller Center > Help Center > Contact Us.
2. Use live chat or submit a support ticket.

**Escalation path**:
1. First contact: Seller Center support.
2. If unresolved: request escalation or contact your account manager.
3. For policy-level disputes: use the appeal function within the specific
   violation or dispute notice.

### PayPal Support

**How to contact**:
1. PayPal Resolution Center > Open dispute/case details > Contact PayPal.
2. Phone support: call the merchant support line for your country.
3. Message Center in your PayPal business account.

**Escalation path**:
1. First contact: Resolution Center / phone support.
2. If unresolved: request escalation to a "Claims Specialist."
3. For systematic issues: contact your PayPal account representative (if assigned).

### General Escalation Best Practices

- **Document everything.** Save case IDs, names of agents, dates, and outcomes.
- **Be persistent but polite.** If the first agent cannot help, try again.
  Different agents have different levels of authority.
- **Reference policy.** Cite specific platform policies or terms of service that
  support your position.
- **Quantify impact.** State the financial impact and account health impact clearly.
  Platforms prioritize cases with measurable business impact.
- **Follow up.** If a case is open for more than 5 business days without progress,
  follow up. Do not assume silence means the issue is being worked on.
- **Know when to stop.** If you have exhausted all escalation paths and the decision
  stands, accept it, document the lesson, and adjust operations to prevent recurrence.
