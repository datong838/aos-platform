## Review Monitoring Setup

Consistent monitoring ensures no review goes unnoticed and response time targets are met.

### Daily Monitoring Checklist

| Task | Frequency | Tool / Location |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Check new reviews on Amazon | Daily | Seller Central > Customer Reviews |
| Check new reviews on Shopee | Daily | Shopee Seller Centre > Shop Rating |
| Check new reviews on TikTok Shop | Daily | TikTok Seller Center > Reviews |
| Check buyer messages | Every 4 hours | Platform seller messaging dashboard |
| Review negative review alerts | Immediately | Email/app notification |
| Weekly review summary | Weekly | Compiled from all platforms |

### Alert Configuration

Set up alerts so that critical reviews are flagged immediately:

**Amazon**:
- Enable email notifications for new reviews in Seller Central under
  Notification Preferences.
- Use third-party monitoring tools (e.g., FeedbackWhiz, SageMailer, Jungle Scout
  Alerts) for real-time alerts on 1-3 star reviews.
- Set up Brand Analytics alerts if enrolled in Brand Registry.

**Shopee**:
- Enable push notifications in the Shopee Seller app.
- Check the Shop Rating section daily for new reviews.
- Monitor the Chat Response Rate metric (directly affects shop score).

**TikTok Shop**:
- Enable seller app notifications for reviews and messages.
- Monitor the Experience Score dashboard for review trends.
- Check video comment sections for product feedback from ad/content viewers.

### Monitoring Tools Comparison

| Tool | Platforms | Key Features | Price Range |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| FeedbackWhiz | Amazon | Review alerts, email automation, analytics | $20-80/month |
| SageMailer | Amazon | Review requests, alerts, templates | $25-100/month |
| Jungle Scout | Amazon | Review tracking, keyword monitoring, alerts | $49-129/month |
| Helium 10 (Review Insights) | Amazon | Sentiment analysis, review trends, alerts | $39-249/month |
| Manual monitoring | All platforms | Free but time-intensive | Staff time only |

### Monitoring SOP

1. **Morning check (9 AM local marketplace time)**: Review all new reviews from the
   past 24 hours. Prioritize 1-2 star reviews for immediate response.
2. **Midday check (1 PM)**: Respond to any remaining reviews. Check buyer messages.
3. **Evening check (6 PM)**: Final sweep for new reviews and messages. Ensure all
   negative reviews from today have received a response.
4. **Weekly review (Monday AM)**: Compile review data into tracking spreadsheet.
   Identify trends. Flag recurring issues for product/supplier team.

## Positive Review Response Templates

Vary responses to avoid appearing robotic. Reference specific details from each review.

### Template 1: Simple Thank You

```
Thank you so much for your feedback! We are glad you are enjoying
the [product name]. Your satisfaction is what drives us. If you ever
need anything, we are just a message away.
```

**Best for**: Short, general positive reviews without specific details.

### Template 2: Feature Acknowledgment

```
We really appreciate you taking the time to share your experience!
It is great to hear that the [specific feature mentioned] has been
working well for you. That is exactly what we designed it for. Thank
you for choosing [brand name]!
```

**Best for**: Reviews that call out a specific product feature.

### Template 3: Use-Case Validation

```
Thank you for sharing how you use the [product name] for [specific
use case]. It is wonderful to hear it fits perfectly into your
routine. We love learning how our customers use our products --
feedback like yours helps us keep improving.
```

**Best for**: Reviews describing a specific use case or scenario.

### Template 4: Quality Recognition

```
Your kind words about the quality of our [product name] mean a lot
to our team. We work closely with our production partners to maintain
high standards, and feedback like yours tells us we are on the right
track. Thank you!
```

**Best for**: Reviews praising build quality, materials, or durability.

### Template 5: Enthusiastic Buyer

```
Wow, thank you for such a wonderful review! We are thrilled that the
[product name] exceeded your expectations. It is customers like you
who make what we do worthwhile. We hope you enjoy it for a long time
to come!
```

**Best for**: Highly enthusiastic 5-star reviews with detailed praise.

### Response Rules

- Never respond with identical text to two consecutive reviews.
- Reference at least one specific detail from the review.
- Keep responses under 80 words.
- Do not include links, promotional language, or requests for additional purchases.
- Respond within 24 hours, ideally within 12 hours.
- Use the reviewer's name if the platform displays it.

## Negative Review Response Templates

Organize responses by issue category. Always move resolution to a private channel.

### Quality Issue Response

```
We sincerely apologize for the quality issue you experienced with
your [product name]. This does not reflect the standard we strive
for, and we take this feedback very seriously. We would like to make
it right -- please contact our support team at [email/message link]
so we can arrange a replacement or full refund. We have also flagged
this with our quality control team to prevent it from happening again.
```

**Follow-up actions**:
- Log the specific defect in the quality tracker (date, ASIN/SKU, defect type, batch if known).
- If 3+ reviews mention the same defect: escalate to supplier with photos and request
  corrective action report.
- If 5+ reviews mention the same defect: consider pausing sales until the root cause
  is identified and resolved.

### Shipping / Delivery Issue Response

```
We are very sorry about the shipping issue with your order. Delays
and delivery problems are incredibly frustrating, and we completely
understand your disappointment. Please reach out to us at [email/
message link] with your order details, and we will resolve this as
quickly as possible. We are also following up with our shipping
partner to address this.
```

**Follow-up actions**:
- Check tracking details to confirm the issue.
- If the package is lost: reship or refund immediately.
- If the package is delayed: provide updated tracking and expected delivery date.
- Log the issue by carrier and shipping route. If a carrier consistently causes
  problems on a route, evaluate alternatives.

### Expectations Mismatch Response

```
Thank you for your honest feedback about the [product name]. We are
sorry it did not meet your expectations regarding [specific concern].
We have taken note of this and will review our product description to
ensure it accurately represents the product. In the meantime, please
contact us at [email/message link] -- we would like to find a
solution that works for you.
```

**Follow-up actions**:
- Review the listing against the complaint. Is the listing accurate?
- If the listing is misleading: update title, bullets, images, or description
  immediately.
- If the listing is accurate but the product does not meet common expectations:
  consider product improvements or adding clarifying information.

### Damage in Transit Response

```
We are sorry to hear that your [product name] arrived damaged. That
is not the experience we want for our customers. Please contact us at
[email/message link] with a photo of the damage, and we will send a
replacement right away at no cost to you. We are also reviewing our
packaging to prevent this in future shipments.
```

**Follow-up actions**:
- Request photos of the damage (for carrier claim and packaging improvement).
- File a carrier damage claim if applicable.
- Review packaging: is the product adequately protected for the shipping route?
- If damage reports exceed 2% of orders: redesign packaging immediately.

### Response Rules for Negative Reviews

- Respond within 24 hours. Ideally within 4 hours for 1-star reviews.
- Never argue, deflect blame, or imply the customer is wrong.
- Never offer compensation in the public response (handle privately).
- Never share order details, tracking numbers, or personal information publicly.
- Always provide a clear path to private resolution.
- After resolving privately, do NOT ask the customer to update or remove the review.
  Some customers will do so voluntarily -- that is a bonus, not an expectation.

## Requesting Reviews Compliantly

Building review velocity through compliant methods is critical. Platform policies strictly
prohibit incentivized or manipulated reviews.

### Amazon: Request a Review Button

The "Request a Review" button in Seller Central is the safest and most compliant method.

**How to use**:
1. Go to Seller Central > Orders > Manage Orders.
2. Find the order (must be 5-30 days after delivery).
3. Click "Request a Review" on the order detail page.
4. Amazon sends a standardized email requesting both a product review and seller feedback.

**Automation**:
- Use third-party tools (FeedbackWhiz, Jungle Scout, SageMailer) to automate the
  "Request a Review" click for all eligible orders.
- Amazon's own API supports automated review requests for Brand Registered sellers.

**Timing best practices**:
- Send 5-7 days after delivery for consumable/simple products.
- Send 10-14 days after delivery for products requiring setup or extended use.
- Avoid weekends and holidays (lower open rates).

**Rules**:
- You can only send one review request per order.
- The button is unavailable after 30 days post-delivery.
- You cannot customize the message (Amazon controls the template).

### Shopee: Auto-Review Reminders

Shopee has a built-in auto-review reminder system.

**How it works**:
- Shopee automatically sends a notification to buyers reminding them to leave a review.
- Reviews on Shopee auto-complete with 5 stars if the buyer does not review within
  the platform's deadline (varies by market, typically 7-15 days).

**Seller actions**:
- Ensure the product experience is positive (packaging, quality, delivery speed).
- Include a thank-you card in the package (see Insert Card section).
- Use Shopee's chat feature to send a polite thank-you message after delivery
  (do not explicitly request a review in the chat).

### General Review Request Best Practices

- Focus on product quality first. The best review strategy is a great product.
- Time your requests when buyer satisfaction is highest (shortly after delivery,
  after they have had time to use the product).
- Never condition any benefit on leaving a review.
- Never ask buyers to contact you before leaving a review (this is review manipulation).
- Accept negative reviews gracefully and use them as improvement data.

## Amazon Vine Program

Amazon Vine is an invitation-only program where Amazon's most trusted reviewers receive
free products in exchange for honest reviews.

### Eligibility

- Product must be enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry.
- Product must have fewer than 30 reviews.
- Product must be in "New" condition with available FBA inventory.
- Products with adult content, heavy/bulky items, or certain restricted categories
  may not be eligible.

### Setup Process

1. Go to Seller Central > Advertising > Vine.
2. Select the ASIN to enroll.
3. Set the number of units to provide (up to 30 units per parent ASIN).
4. Amazon selects Vine Voices (reviewers) and ships the product to them.
5. Reviewers have 30 days to submit a review after receiving the product.

### Costs

- Enrollment fee: $200 per parent ASIN (as of 2025; check current pricing).
- Product cost: you bear the cost of the units shipped to reviewers (COGS + FBA fees).
- Total estimated cost per review: $15-50 depending on product value and enrollment fee.

### ROI Calculation

```
Total Vine cost = Enrollment fee + (Units provided x COGS per unit)
Cost per review = Total Vine cost / Number of reviews received
Break-even = Additional revenue from review-driven conversion lift

Example:
  Enrollment fee: $200
  Units provided: 15 at $8 COGS each = $120
  Total cost: $320
  Reviews received: 12 (typical ~80% response rate)
  Cost per review: $26.67

  If each review increases daily sales by 0.5 units at $25 margin:
  Payback period: $320 / (0.5 x $25) = ~26 days
```

### Vine Best Practices

- Enroll products at launch, before running PPC campaigns. Reviews improve ad
  conversion and reduce wasted ad spend.
- Start with 10-15 units rather than the full 30. Evaluate review quality before
  committing more.
- Ensure the product is fully ready (no known defects, listing optimized). Vine
  reviewers are thorough and will note issues.
- Vine reviews are marked with a "Vine Customer Review of Free Product" badge. These
  reviews are generally trusted by shoppers despite the badge.
- You cannot influence the content or rating of Vine reviews. Expect honest
  assessments.

### Vine Limitations

- Reviews may not all be positive -- this is by design and expected.
- You cannot select which reviewers receive the product.
- Enrollment cannot be reversed once units are shipped.
- Not all categories are eligible.

## Dealing with Fake or Competitor Negative Reviews

Fake reviews from competitors or malicious actors are an unfortunate reality. Platform
reporting mechanisms exist but require proper evidence.

### Identifying Fake Reviews

Signs that a review may be fake or competitor-driven:

- The reviewer has no verified purchase badge.
- The review content is vague, generic, or does not describe the actual product.
- Multiple negative reviews appear in a short timeframe with similar language.
- The reviewer's profile shows a pattern of reviewing only competitor products positively.
- The review mentions a competitor product by name.
- The review describes issues that do not match the product (wrong color, size,
  features that do not exist).
- The review was posted before the product could have been received (based on
  order dates).

### Amazon Reporting Process

1. **Report the review**:
   - Go to the review on the product page.
   - Click "Report abuse" below the review.
   - Select the appropriate reason.

2. **Contact Seller Support**:
   - Open a case in Seller Central > Help > Contact Us.
   - Category: Products and Inventory > Product Reviews.
   - Provide the ASIN, review ID, and specific reasons you believe the review
     violates policy.

3. **Brand Registry Report** (if enrolled):
   - Use the "Report a Violation" tool in Brand Registry.
   - Select "Review abuse" as the violation type.
   - Provide evidence.

4. **Email Amazon directly**:
   - community-help@amazon.com (general review issues).
   - Provide: ASIN, review link, reviewer profile link, specific policy violation,
     and any evidence of manipulation.

**Success rate**: Amazon removes clearly fake reviews in approximately 30-50% of cases.
Removal can take 1-4 weeks. Persistence and clear evidence improve outcomes.

### Shopee Reporting Process

1. Go to the review in Shopee Seller Centre.
2. Click "Report" on the specific review.
3. Select the reason (spam, irrelevant, offensive, or fake).
4. Provide supporting evidence.
5. Shopee typically responds within 3-7 business days.

### TikTok Shop Reporting Process

1. Navigate to the review in TikTok Seller Center.
2. Use the "Appeal" or "Report" function on the review.
3. Provide evidence that the review violates platform policy.
4. TikTok typically reviews within 5-10 business days.

### Proactive Defense

- Maintain a high volume of genuine positive reviews (dilutes fake negatives).
- Document patterns (screenshots, dates, reviewer profiles) for escalation.
- If you suspect coordinated competitor attacks, compile evidence and escalate to
  platform account management or legal team.
- Never retaliate by posting fake negative reviews on competitor listings. This
  violates platform policy and can result in account suspension.

## Review Analytics

Systematic analysis of review data reveals product issues, listing gaps, and
competitive opportunities.

### Sentiment Tracking

Track review sentiment over time using these categories:

| Sentiment | Rating | Action |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Very positive | 5 stars | Extract praise keywords for listing optimization |
| Positive | 4 stars | Note minor concerns for product improvement |
| Neutral | 3 stars | Identify specific dissatisfaction drivers |
| Negative | 2 stars | Root-cause analysis; consider listing updates |
| Very negative | 1 star | Immediate response; escalate if pattern emerges |

### Keyword Extraction from Reviews

Mine review text for recurring themes:

1. **Positive keywords**: Words and phrases that appear frequently in 4-5 star reviews.
   Use these in listing copy, PPC keywords, and marketing materials.
   - Example: If customers frequently say "easy to assemble," make this a bullet
     point lead.

2. **Negative keywords**: Words and phrases in 1-3 star reviews indicating problems.
   - Example: If "flimsy" appears in 5+ reviews, investigate material quality.

3. **Feature requests**: Suggestions or wishes expressed in reviews.
   - Example: "I wish it came in blue" signals a color variation opportunity.

### Review Tracking Spreadsheet

Maintain a central tracking sheet with these columns:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date | Review date |
| Platform | Amazon, Shopee, TikTok, etc. |
| ASIN / SKU | Product identifier |
| Rating | 1-5 stars |
| Sentiment Category | Quality, shipping, expectations, damage, positive |
| Key Quote | Relevant excerpt from the review |
| Response Status | Pending, responded, resolved |
| Root Cause | Product defect, listing issue, shipping, buyer error |
| Action Taken | Refund, replacement, listing update, supplier notice |
| Follow-up Needed | Yes/No and details |

### Monthly Review Report

Compile a monthly summary including:
- Total reviews received (by platform and product).
- Average rating trend (vs. previous month).
- Top 3 positive themes.
- Top 3 negative themes.
- Return rate correlation with review complaints.
- Action items for product, listing, and packaging teams.

## Product Insert Card Best Practices

Insert cards are physical cards placed inside product packaging. When done correctly,
they build brand loyalty and encourage reviews. When done incorrectly, they violate
platform policy and risk account suspension.

### Compliant Insert Card Examples

**Example 1: Thank You Card**
```
Thank you for your purchase!

We hope you love your [product name]. Our small team puts a lot of
care into every product, and your support means the world to us.

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out:
support@yourbrand.com

We would love to hear your honest feedback!
Scan the QR code to visit our product page.

[QR code linking to product page]
```

**Example 2: Brand Story Card**
```
Welcome to the [Brand Name] family!

[1-2 sentences about your brand story or mission]

Need help? Contact us anytime:
support@yourbrand.com

Your feedback helps us improve. We welcome your honest review!
```

**Example 3: Care Instructions + Review Prompt**
```
Care Instructions for Your [Product Name]:
- [Instruction 1]
- [Instruction 2]
- [Instruction 3]

Questions? Reach us at support@yourbrand.com

Enjoying your purchase? We would appreciate your honest feedback
on [platform name].
```

### Non-Compliant Insert Card Examples (DO NOT USE)

**Violation 1: Incentivized review**
```
WRONG: "Leave a 5-star review and receive a $5 Amazon gift card!
Email us your review screenshot to claim."
```

**Violation 2: Review gating**
```
WRONG: "If you love our product, please leave a review on Amazon!
If you have any issues, please contact us FIRST at
support@brand.com before leaving a review."
```

**Violation 3: Specific rating request**
```
WRONG: "We hope you will give us 5 stars! Your 5-star review
helps our small business grow."
```

**Violation 4: External review redirect**
```
WRONG: "Please leave your review on our website:
www.brand.com/reviews (instead of on Amazon)"
```

### Insert Card Design Tips

- Use high-quality cardstock (minimum 300 GSM).
- Include brand colors and logo for recognition.
- Keep text minimal and readable (font size 10+ pt).
- QR codes should link to the product page, not directly to the "leave a review" page.
- Include a support email or messaging channel for issue resolution.
- Print double-sided to maximize space without adding bulk.
- Consider including a brief product use tip or care instruction for added value.
- Size: business card (3.5 x 2 in) or half postcard (5.5 x 4.25 in) are standard.
