## How to Use This Reference

Each market profile follows the same structure:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Population | Total population (approximate) |
| E-commerce Penetration | % of population that shops online |
| E-commerce Market Size | Estimated annual e-commerce GMV |
| Avg Order Value (AOV) | Typical basket size in USD |
| Top Platforms | Dominant marketplaces for cross-border sellers |
| Logistics Score | 1-5 rating of fulfillment infrastructure |
| Regulatory Notes | Key compliance requirements |
| Key Opportunities | Why to consider this market |
| Key Risks | Why to be cautious |

Cross-reference each profile with the 5-Factor Scoring Framework in
`SKILL.md` to generate a composite market score for your specific category.

## North America

### United States (US)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Population | ~335 million |
| E-commerce Penetration | ~80% |
| E-commerce Market Size | ~$1.1 trillion |
| Avg Order Value | $80-$120 |
| Top Platforms | Amazon (dominant), Walmart Marketplace, TikTok Shop, Shopify (DTC), Temu, eBay |
| Logistics Score | **5** -- FBA, extensive 3PL network, 2-day delivery standard |
| Payment | Credit/debit cards dominant, Apple Pay/Google Pay growing, Buy Now Pay Later popular |

**Regulatory Notes:**
- FCC certification required for electronic devices
- FDA registration for food, cosmetics, supplements, medical devices
- CPSIA compliance for children's products (lead, phthalates testing)
- Prop 65 warnings for products sold in California
- Multi-state sales tax nexus triggered by FBA inventory placement
- Section 301 tariffs on China-origin goods (varies 7.5-25%+ by HS code)
- De minimis threshold: $800 (packages under $800 enter duty-free)

**Key Opportunities:**
- Largest single-country e-commerce market
- Highest consumer spending power
- FBA infrastructure enables fast, reliable delivery
- TikTok Shop growing rapidly for discovery-driven categories
- English-language market reduces content creation barriers

**Key Risks:**
- Extremely competitive; high CPC ($0.80-$3.00+ for most categories)
- Established sellers with thousands of reviews create high moats
- FBA fees increasing year-over-year (storage + fulfillment)
- Complex tax obligations across 45+ states with sales tax
- High return rates (15-30% in apparel, 5-15% in general merchandise)
- Section 301 tariffs significantly increase landed cost from China

### Canada (CA)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Population | ~40 million |
| E-commerce Penetration | ~75% |
| E-commerce Market Size | ~$65 billion |
| Avg Order Value | $70-$100 CAD |
| Top Platforms | Amazon.ca (dominant), Walmart.ca, Shopify (DTC), eBay.ca |
| Logistics Score | **4** -- Amazon FBA.ca available, 3PL options in major cities |
| Payment | Credit/debit cards, Interac e-Transfer |

**Regulatory Notes:**
- GST/HST registration required (5-15% depending on province)
- Bilingual labeling required (English + French) for consumer products
- Canadian Standards Association (CSA) for electrical/electronic products
- Health Canada regulations for food, cosmetics, NHPs
- De minimis threshold: $20 CAD (very low; most shipments attract duty)

**Key Opportunities:**
- English-speaking market, easy expansion from US
- Lower competition than US Amazon in most categories
- Can share FBA inventory with NARF (North America Remote Fulfillment)
- Culturally similar to US, reducing localization cost

**Key Risks:**
- Small market (1/10th of US) limits revenue ceiling
- Very low de minimis ($20 CAD) means almost all imports incur duty
- Bilingual labeling adds cost and complexity
- Winter shipping challenges in northern regions
- High shipping costs for direct-from-China fulfillment

### Mexico (MX)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Population | ~130 million |
| E-commerce Penetration | ~55% |
| E-commerce Market Size | ~$60 billion |
| Avg Order Value | $30-$60 |
| Top Platforms | Amazon.com.mx, Mercado Libre (dominant in LATAM), Walmart.com.mx |
| Logistics Score | **3** -- FBA.mx available but limited; last-mile inconsistent outside major metros |
| Payment | Credit/debit cards, OXXO (cash-based payment), bank transfers, BNPL growing |

**Regulatory Notes:**
- NOM (Normas Oficiales Mexicanas) standards for electronics, textiles, toys
- RFC (tax ID) required for selling; CFDI invoicing mandatory
- Import duties vary widely (0-35%); USMCA may reduce duty for NA-origin goods
- IVA (VAT) at 16%
- Spanish-language product listings and customer service required

**Key Opportunities:**
- Large, young population with growing e-commerce adoption
- Less competition than US for many categories
- USMCA benefits for goods shipped from US/CA warehouses
- Growing middle class with increasing disposable income
- Amazon MX and Mercado Libre both investing heavily in infrastructure

**Key Risks:**
- Lower AOV limits margin per unit
- Cash-on-delivery (OXXO) payments create fulfillment complexity
- Last-mile delivery unreliable outside Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey
- NOM certification can take 2-4 months and cost $3,000-$8,000
- Currency volatility (MXN) affects pricing stability
- Security concerns for high-value goods in some regions

### Germany (DE)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Population | ~84 million |
| E-commerce Penetration | ~78% |
| E-commerce Market Size | ~$130 billion |
| Avg Order Value | $60-$90 EUR |
| Top Platforms | Amazon.de (dominant), eBay.de, Otto, Kaufland |
| Logistics Score | **5** -- Amazon FBA EU hub, pan-European fulfillment, excellent 3PL |
| Payment | PayPal (dominant), bank transfer (SEPA), credit cards, Klarna, invoice payment |

**Regulatory Notes:**
- CE marking required for applicable categories
- WEEE registration (Stiftung EAR) for electronics
- VerpackG (packaging law): register with LUCID, contract dual system
- EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) expanding scope
- German VAT registration (19% standard rate) + OSS optional for EU-wide
- REACH/SVHC chemical compliance
- German-language listings and customer service expected

**Key Opportunities:**
- Largest economy in Europe, strong consumer spending
- Amazon DE is the gateway to pan-European FBA
- German consumers value quality; premium pricing possible
- Lower advertising cost than US Amazon
- Pan-European FBA enables serving FR, IT, ES, NL, PL from one stock pool

**Key Risks:**
- Complex regulatory stack (WEEE + VerpackG + EPR + CE + REACH)
- German consumers are demanding on quality and expect easy returns
- Return rates among highest in Europe (especially apparel, 40%+)
- Invoice payment culture means delayed cash collection
- Compliance cost can be $5,000-$15,000+ for initial setup
- German language required for all communications

- BRL currency volatility affects pricing
- Complex tax system (arguably most complex globally)
- Consumer protection laws (CDC) strongly favor buyers

- Coupang's Rocket Delivery sets high logistics standard
- Korean wave (K-culture) creates export and import opportunities
- High mobile commerce penetration

**Key Risks:**
- KC certification costly and mandatory for many categories
- Korean language required for all consumer-facing content
- Coupang dominates; difficult to compete outside their ecosystem
- Fast fashion cycles; trends change rapidly
- Local entity or agent often required
- Intense price competition online
- Consumer expectations for delivery speed extremely high

## Oceania

### Australia (AU)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Population | ~26 million |
| E-commerce Penetration | ~75% |
| E-commerce Market Size | ~$50 billion |
| Avg Order Value | $60-$90 AUD |
| Top Platforms | Amazon.com.au, eBay.com.au, Kogan, Catch.com.au |
| Logistics Score | **3** -- Amazon FBA.au available but limited capacity; distance from suppliers is a challenge |
| Payment | Credit/debit cards, PayPal, Afterpay/Zip (BNPL very popular), bank transfer |

**Regulatory Notes:**
- Australian standards (AS/NZS) for electrical, children's products
- RCM (Regulatory Compliance Mark) for electrical and EMC
- TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration) for health products
- ACCC (consumer law) strong consumer protection enforcement
- GST at 10% on all imported goods (no de minimis since 2018)
- English-language market
- Biosecurity restrictions (strict quarantine on plant/animal products)

**Key Opportunities:**
- English-speaking market with high spending power
- Amazon.com.au still growing, less competitive than US/UK
- BNPL adoption among highest globally, enabling higher-value purchases
- Geographic isolation means less casual competition
- Consumers open to international brands and products

**Key Risks:**
- Small population relative to shipping distance from China
- High shipping costs and long transit times from most origins
- GST on all imports (no de minimis threshold)
- Amazon.com.au FBA still building capacity
- Strict biosecurity rules can block/delay shipments
- RCM certification required for electronics
- Distance from origin makes returns very expensive
