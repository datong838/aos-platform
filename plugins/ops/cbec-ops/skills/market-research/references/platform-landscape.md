## Amazon

### Overview

The world's largest cross-border e-commerce ecosystem. Amazon operates
country-specific marketplaces with a unified seller platform (Seller Central)
and the most mature fulfillment-by-marketplace (FBA) infrastructure.

### Markets Served

| Marketplace | Launch Year | Cross-Border Seller Access | FBA Available |
|-------------|-------------|---------------------------|---------------|
| Amazon.com (US) | 1994 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.ca (Canada) | 2002 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.com.mx (Mexico) | 2015 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.co.uk (UK) | 1998 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.de (Germany) | 1998 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.fr (France) | 2000 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.it (Italy) | 2010 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.es (Spain) | 2011 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.nl (Netherlands) | 2020 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.pl (Poland) | 2021 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.se (Sweden) | 2020 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.be (Belgium) | 2022 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.co.jp (Japan) | 2000 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.com.au (Australia) | 2017 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.sg (Singapore) | 2019 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.ae (UAE) | 2019 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.sa (Saudi Arabia) | 2020 | Open | Yes |
| Amazon.in (India) | 2013 | Restricted (local entity) | Yes |
| Amazon.com.br (Brazil) | 2012 | Limited | Yes |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Referral Fee (Commission) | 6-45% (most categories 8-15%) | Category-dependent; media higher |
| FBA Fulfillment Fee | $3.00-$8.00+ per unit (US) | Based on size and weight tier |
| FBA Storage (monthly) | $0.78-$2.40/cu ft | Seasonal surge Oct-Dec |
| FBA Aged Inventory Surcharge | $0.50-$6.90/cu ft | Applies to inventory >180 days |
| Professional Seller Plan | $39.99/month (US) | Per marketplace; some included in pan-EU |
| Advertising (Sponsored Products) | CPC $0.30-$3.00+ | Highly category-dependent |

**Pan-European FBA:** Single inventory pool in one EU country (typically DE),
Amazon distributes to other EU FBAs. Reduces per-market storage cost but
triggers VAT registration in destination countries (unless using OSS).

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) | Ship to Amazon warehouse; Amazon picks, packs, ships | Most sellers; Buy Box advantage |
| FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant) | Seller handles fulfillment via own warehouse or 3PL | High-margin, oversized, or slow-moving items |
| SFP (Seller Fulfilled Prime) | Seller ships but displays Prime badge | Established sellers with fast shipping capability |
| NARF (North America Remote Fulfillment) | US FBA inventory serves CA and MX | Easy CA/MX expansion without separate FBA |
| Pan-European FBA | One EU FBA inventory serves all EU marketplaces | EU-wide sellers |
| Amazon Global Logistics (AGL) | Amazon's freight forwarding from China to FBA | China-based sellers |
| Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) | Use FBA for non-Amazon orders (Shopify, etc.) | Sellers on multiple channels |

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC (US) |
|---------|-------------|------------------|
| Sponsored Products | Keyword-targeted product ads in search results | $0.50-$2.50 |
| Sponsored Brands | Banner ads with brand logo and multiple products | $0.75-$3.00 |
| Sponsored Display | Retargeting and audience-based display ads | $0.30-$1.50 |
| Sponsored Brands Video | Video ads in search results | $0.50-$2.00 |
| Amazon DSP | Programmatic display, on and off Amazon | CPM-based; $15K+ minimum (managed) |

**Key metrics:** ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sale) = Ad Spend / Ad Revenue.
Target ACoS varies by strategy: 15-25% for profitability, 30-50% for launch,
50-100%+ for ranking push.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Capital requirement:** $5,000-$30,000+ for US launch (inventory + FBA fees + advertising)
- **Brand Registry:** Requires registered trademark; unlocks A+ Content, Brand Analytics, Vine
- **Category ungating:** Some categories require approval (beauty, grocery, automotive, etc.)
- **Compliance:** Product-specific certifications (FCC, UL, CE, etc.) must be in place before listing
- **Tax:** VAT registration for EU markets; sales tax nexus for US states

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Home & Kitchen | High search volume, moderate competition outside top niches |
| Beauty & Personal Care | High margin, repeat purchase, brand-buildable |
| Sports & Outdoors | Large category, seasonal peaks, diverse niches |
| Pet Supplies | Loyal customer base, high repeat rate |
| Office Products | Steady demand, B2B opportunities |
| Toys & Games (seasonal) | Massive Q4 peak, but requires CPSIA compliance |
| Health & Household | Growing category, but FDA/supplement rules strict |

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- China-based sellers: Use Amazon Global Logistics or freight forwarder to FBA warehouse. Account verification increasingly strict (video call, utility bills, business license).
- Currency: Amazon pays in seller's local currency or marketplace currency; Amazon Currency Converter takes 1-1.5%. Use Payoneer or WorldFirst for better rates.
- Review velocity: Critical on Amazon. Use Vine (Brand Registry), Request a Review button, and product inserts (within ToS) to build reviews. Never buy reviews -- permanent suspension risk.
- Account health: Amazon's performance metrics (ODR < 1%, late shipment < 4%, pre-fulfillment cancel < 2.5%) are strictly enforced. FBA eliminates most shipping-related risks.

## Shopee

### Overview

Southeast Asia's largest e-commerce platform by order volume. Shopee operates
a mobile-first marketplace with heavy gamification, vouchers, and live streaming
features. Owned by Sea Limited (Singapore).

### Markets Served

| Market | Cross-Border Seller Access | Local Warehouse / Fulfillment |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Singapore | Open (SIP cross-border program) | SLS (Shopee Logistics Service) |
| Malaysia | Open (cross-border) | SLS, local warehouse options |
| Thailand | Open (cross-border) | SLS |
| Philippines | Open (cross-border) | SLS |
| Indonesia | Restricted (prefer local entity) | SLS |
| Vietnam | Open (cross-border) | SLS |
| Taiwan | Open (cross-border) | SLS |
| Brazil | Open (cross-border) | Limited |
| Mexico | Open (cross-border) | Limited |
| Colombia | Open (cross-border) | Limited |
| Chile | Open (cross-border) | Limited |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Commission Rate | 2-6% (varies by market and category) | Generally lower than Amazon |
| Transaction Fee | 2-3% | Payment processing |
| Service Fee | 2-6.5% | Market-dependent |
| Shipping Subsidy | Platform often subsidizes | Shopee's competitive tool; reduces seller cost |
| Advertising (Shopee Ads) | CPC $0.03-$0.30 | Much lower than Amazon CPC |
| Coins Cashback / Vouchers | Platform-funded or co-funded | Heavily promoted during campaigns |

**Total take rate:** Typically 8-15% all-in (commission + transaction + service fees). Lower than Amazon's effective take rate in most cases.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| SLS (Shopee Logistics Service) | Shopee's integrated logistics; seller ships to local sorting center | Most sellers in SEA |
| Cross-border fulfillment | Ship from China/HK to Shopee's cross-border warehouse | China-based sellers |
| Self-ship (3PL) | Seller arranges own logistics | Sellers with existing local fulfillment |
| Shopee-managed warehouse | Emerging option in select markets | High-volume sellers |

**Key consideration:** Shopee does NOT have an FBA-equivalent on par with Amazon. Fulfillment is primarily seller-managed with Shopee-coordinated last-mile delivery. Cross-border sellers typically ship from China warehouses to Shopee's sorting centers.

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Shopee Ads (Search) | Keyword-targeted in-app search ads | $0.03-$0.20 |
| Shopee Ads (Discovery) | Algorithm-recommended product ads | $0.02-$0.15 |
| Shopee Live | Live streaming with product links | Organic (no CPC); requires time investment |
| Flash Deals / Campaign Slots | Discounted slots during mega-campaigns (9.9, 11.11, 12.12) | Fee-based entry or performance threshold |

**Key metrics:** Shopee advertising is significantly cheaper than Amazon but has
lower conversion rates. Focus on campaign periods (monthly pay-day sales + mega
sales) for maximum ROI.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Capital requirement:** $1,000-$5,000 for initial inventory (lower AOV = less capital per SKU)
- **Registration:** Business license required; China-based sellers use SIP (Shopee International Platform)
- **Language:** Each market requires localized listings (Malay, Thai, Vietnamese, Bahasa, etc.)
- **Campaign participation:** Mega-campaigns (9.9, 11.11, 12.12) drive majority of sales; must qualify
- **Price competition:** Shopee is price-driven; sellers must compete on vouchers and pricing

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Fashion & Accessories | Highest volume category on Shopee across all markets |
| Beauty & Personal Care | High repeat rate, live streaming effective |
| Home & Living | Growing rapidly, diverse niches |
| Electronics Accessories | Phone cases, chargers, cables -- high volume, low AOV |
| Baby & Kids | Large segment in SEA's young population |
| Health & Wellness | Growing category, especially post-pandemic |

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- China sellers dominate cross-border: Shopee International Platform (SIP) based in Shenzhen is the primary cross-border seller hub.
- Mega-campaigns are critical: 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12, and monthly payday sales can drive 50-70% of monthly revenue. Plan inventory and advertising around these dates.
- Gamification: Shopee Coins, Shopee Live, Flash Deals, and voucher stacking are core to the platform experience. Sellers who ignore these features underperform.
- Low AOV strategy: Success on Shopee requires volume. Bundle products, offer free shipping thresholds, and optimize for repeat purchases.
- Chat response time: Shopee ranks sellers partly on chat response speed. Set up auto-replies and monitor chat actively.

## Lazada

### Overview

Southeast Asia's second-largest e-commerce platform, owned by Alibaba Group.
Lazada operates in six core SEA markets with a focus on branded retail and
mall-style shopping experience. Benefits from Alibaba's technology and supply
chain infrastructure.

### Markets Served

| Market | Cross-Border Seller Access | Local Warehouse / Fulfillment |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Singapore | Open | Lazada Fulfillment Center |
| Malaysia | Open | Lazada Fulfillment Center |
| Thailand | Open | Lazada Fulfillment Center |
| Philippines | Open | Lazada Fulfillment Center |
| Indonesia | Open (Lazada Global Shipping) | Lazada Fulfillment Center |
| Vietnam | Open | Lazada Fulfillment Center |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Commission Rate | 1-8% (varies by category) | Generally competitive with Shopee |
| Payment Fee | 1-2% | Payment processing |
| Shipping Fee | Varies by market | Lazada often subsidizes |
| Advertising (Lazada Ads) | CPC $0.03-$0.25 | Similar to Shopee range |
| LazMall Fee | Higher commission but brand badge | For verified brands/flagship stores |

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Lazada Fulfillment (FBL) | Ship to Lazada warehouse; similar to FBA concept | LazMall sellers, high-volume products |
| Seller-managed (3PL) | Seller arranges delivery via logistics partners | Most cross-border sellers |
| Lazada Global Shipping (LGS) | Ship from China to Lazada cross-border warehouse | China-based cross-border sellers |
| Dropship | Limited support; seller relies on supplier fulfillment | Testing phase or low-commitment entry |

**FBL vs Shopee:** Lazada's FBL is more developed than Shopee's fulfillment options, drawing from Alibaba's Cainiao logistics expertise. FBL sellers get preferential listing visibility.

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Sponsored Search | Keyword-targeted search results ads | $0.03-$0.20 |
| Sponsored Discovery | Algorithm-based product recommendations | $0.02-$0.15 |
| Sponsored Affiliates | Performance-based affiliate network | Commission-based (5-15%) |
| LazLive (Live Streaming) | Live shopping with in-stream purchase | Organic; requires content investment |
| Campaign Banners | Mega-campaign featured placements | Fee-based or invitation |

### Barriers to Entry

- **Capital requirement:** $1,000-$5,000 for initial inventory
- **Registration:** Business license; Alibaba ecosystem familiarity helpful
- **LazMall:** Higher entry bar (brand verification, higher fees) but better visibility and trust
- **Language:** Localized listings required per market
- **Competition with Shopee:** Lazada has less traffic in some markets; dual-platform strategy often necessary

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Electronics & Gadgets | Alibaba supply chain advantage, branded products |
| Fashion (mid-range) | LazMall brand focus supports premium fashion |
| Home & Living | Growing category, strong during mega-campaigns |
| Beauty | Competing with Shopee for beauty market share |
| FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) | LazMart grocery vertical in some markets |
| Appliances | Higher-ticket items perform well on LazMall |

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- Alibaba ecosystem advantage: Sellers familiar with 1688/Alibaba.com find Lazada's seller tools and logistics concepts familiar. AliExpress cross-border knowledge transfers well.
- LazMall vs Marketplace: LazMall (branded store) gets higher visibility and trust but requires brand verification and higher commitment. Start on marketplace, upgrade to LazMall when qualified.
- Dual-platform strategy: Most successful SEA sellers operate on both Shopee AND Lazada. Category performance differs by platform and market.
- Campaign calendar: Similar to Shopee (monthly pay-day, 6.6, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12) but Lazada also runs Birthday Sale (March) and mid-year mega sale.
- Lazada University: Free seller education platform; useful for onboarding and best practice learning.

## TikTok Shop

### Overview

TikTok's integrated e-commerce platform enabling in-app purchases through short
videos, live streams, and a dedicated product marketplace. Rapidly growing,
leveraging TikTok's massive user base and content-driven discovery model.

### Markets Served

| Market | Cross-Border Seller Access | Fulfillment Options |
|--------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| United States | Open (rapidly expanding) | Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT), seller-managed |
| United Kingdom | Open | Seller-managed, 3PL |
| Indonesia | Local entity required (merged with Tokopedia) | Tokopedia logistics |
| Thailand | Open (cross-border) | TikTok logistics, seller-managed |
| Vietnam | Open (cross-border) | TikTok logistics, seller-managed |
| Malaysia | Open (cross-border) | TikTok logistics, seller-managed |
| Philippines | Open (cross-border) | TikTok logistics, seller-managed |
| Singapore | Open (cross-border) | TikTok logistics, seller-managed |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Commission Rate | 2-8% (market and category dependent) | US: currently promotional rates for seller acquisition |
| Transaction Fee | 1-2% | Payment processing |
| FBT Fulfillment Fee (US) | Comparable to FBA | Size and weight tier based |
| Advertising (TikTok Ads) | CPC $0.10-$1.00+ | Video ad creative required |
| Affiliate Commission | 5-20% | Creator-driven sales; seller sets commission rate |
| Shipping Subsidy | Platform subsidized in some markets | Part of growth strategy |

**Promotional rates:** TikTok Shop is aggressively subsidizing fees to attract sellers (as of early 2026). Expect fee increases as the platform matures. Do not build P&L models assuming current promotional rates are permanent.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| FBT (Fulfilled by TikTok) -- US | Ship to TikTok warehouse; TikTok handles fulfillment | US sellers; similar to FBA |
| Seller-managed (3PL) | Seller arranges shipping; TikTok coordinates tracking | Most SEA cross-border sellers |
| Cross-border shipping | Ship from China/origin to buyer | Budget-conscious sellers; longer delivery times |
| Local warehouse (TikTok logistics) | Emerging in SEA markets | High-volume sellers in specific markets |

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| In-Feed Ads | Video ads in For You Page | $0.20-$1.00 |
| Spark Ads | Boost organic posts as ads | $0.10-$0.80 |
| Product Shopping Ads | Catalog-based product ads | $0.15-$0.75 |
| LIVE Shopping Ads | Promote live stream shopping events | Varies |
| Affiliate Program | Creators promote products for commission | Commission-based (no CPC) |

**Content-first platform:** Unlike Amazon (search-intent) or Shopee (browse-intent), TikTok Shop is discovery-intent. Success requires compelling video content. Products that demo well or have visual appeal thrive. Products that require detailed specs comparison struggle.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Content creation:** Must produce short-form video content; sellers without video capability are disadvantaged
- **Capital requirement:** $2,000-$15,000 for US (inventory + content creation + advertising); $500-$3,000 for SEA
- **Creator network:** Building relationships with TikTok creators for affiliate sales is critical and time-consuming
- **Registration:** Business license required; US requires SSN or EIN
- **Speed of change:** Platform policies, fees, and features change rapidly; requires constant adaptation
- **Category restrictions:** Some categories (supplements, medical devices) face stricter moderation

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Beauty & Skincare | Demo-friendly, impulse purchase, creator-driven |
| Fashion & Accessories | Visual, trend-driven, works well in short video |
| Kitchen Gadgets | "Satisfying" demo videos go viral, high impulse buy |
| Phone Accessories | Low price point, high volume, easy to demo |
| Fitness & Wellness | Aspirational content, strong creator community |
| Home Organization | Before/after content performs well |
| Novelty/Gift Items | Shareability drives organic views |

**Categories that struggle on TikTok Shop:** B2B products, complex electronics requiring detailed specs, commodity items with no visual differentiation, high-consideration purchases (furniture, appliances).

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- Content is king: Invest in video content creation. Budget $500-$2,000/month for content production (in-house or agency). A/B test hooks (first 3 seconds), music, and CTAs.
- Affiliate program is essential: Top TikTok Shop sellers generate 40-70% of sales through affiliate creators. Set competitive commission rates (15-25% for launch, 10-15% at scale) and provide free samples to creators.
- Live shopping: Particularly strong in SEA markets (Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia). US live shopping is growing but not yet dominant. Live sessions of 2-4 hours can drive significant daily sales.
- Platform volatility: TikTok Shop has faced regulatory challenges (Indonesia ban leading to Tokopedia merger, US TikTok ban discussions). Factor political/regulatory risk into market selection.
- Speed advantage: The platform is still early. Sellers who establish now build review/rating moats before competition intensifies. First-mover advantage is real but time-limited.

## Mercado Libre

### Overview

Latin America's largest e-commerce platform by GMV and market capitalization.
Founded in Argentina (1999), operates in 18 LATAM countries. Integrated
ecosystem includes marketplace, payments (Mercado Pago), logistics (Mercado
Envios), lending, and advertising.

### Markets Served

| Market | Relative Size | Cross-Border Seller Access | Fulfillment |
|--------|---------------|---------------------------|-------------|
| Brazil | Largest (~60% of ML GMV) | Open (CBT program) | Mercado Envios Full |
| Mexico | Second largest | Open (CBT program) | Mercado Envios Full |
| Argentina | Third largest | Open | Mercado Envios |
| Colombia | Growing | Open | Mercado Envios |
| Chile | Growing | Open | Mercado Envios |
| Peru | Emerging | Open | Mercado Envios |
| Uruguay | Small | Open | Limited logistics |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Commission Rate | 11-19% (varies by category and listing type) | Classic listings lower; Premium listings higher but better visibility |
| Mercado Envios Fee | Included in commission or separate | Varies by product size/weight and origin-destination |
| Advertising (Product Ads) | CPC ~$0.05-$0.30 | Lower than Amazon US but conversion also lower |
| Mercado Pago Fee | 2-5% | Payment processing; installment fees vary |
| Cross-Border Shipping | Varies | ML's CBT program manages customs/logistics for cross-border |

**Total take rate:** 15-25% all-in for most sellers, depending on listing type and whether using Mercado Envios Full.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Mercado Envios Full (Fulfillment) | Ship to ML warehouse; ML handles fulfillment | Sellers wanting FBA-equivalent |
| Mercado Envios (Flex/Standard) | ML coordinates logistics; seller prepares shipment | Moderate-volume sellers |
| Cross-Border Trade (CBT) | Ship from China/US; ML handles import and last-mile | Cross-border sellers entering LATAM |
| Seller-managed | Seller arranges own logistics | Sellers with local presence |

**Mercado Envios Full** is the closest equivalent to Amazon FBA in Latin America. Products fulfilled by ML get preferential listing placement and buyer trust. Strongly recommended for serious sellers.

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Product Ads | Sponsored product listings in search results | $0.05-$0.30 |
| Display Ads | Banner ads across ML ecosystem | CPM-based |
| Mercado Ads (off-platform) | Retargeting ads served outside ML | CPM-based |
| Mercado Shops | Branded storefront within ML | No direct ad cost; branding play |

**Advertising maturity:** Less sophisticated than Amazon Ads. Keyword targeting
is more limited, and analytics tools are developing. However, competition for
ad placements is also lower, making ROI attractive for early adopters.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Language:** Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (all others); bilingual content needed for multi-market
- **Capital requirement:** $2,000-$10,000 for initial inventory and logistics setup
- **Registration:** Tax ID required per market (CPF/CNPJ for Brazil, RFC for Mexico)
- **CBT program:** Cross-border sellers can use ML's CBT program, but local fulfillment gets better visibility
- **Import taxes (Brazil):** 60%+ effective import duty makes DTC pricing from China challenging
- **Payment:** Installment culture (parcelamento) means sellers may wait for funds

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Electronics & Accessories | Top-selling category across all LATAM markets |
| Automotive Parts | Unique strength of ML vs global competitors |
| Home & Garden | Growing rapidly, diverse price points |
| Fashion | Large category, but returns are a challenge |
| Tools & Hardware | Strong B2C and B2B demand |
| Beauty & Personal Care | Growing, especially in Brazil |
| Sports & Fitness | Growing middle class driving demand |

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- Brazil requires local strategy: The 60% import tax makes cross-border DTC from China uncompetitive for most products. Consider local warehousing (via Mercado Envios Full) or finding a Brazilian distributor/importer.
- Mexico is the easier LATAM entry: Lower import taxes, USMCA benefits for US-warehoused goods, and strong Mercado Libre infrastructure.
- Installment payments: Brazilian and Mexican consumers routinely buy in 3-12x installments (sem juros = interest-free). Factor cash flow delays into financial planning.
- Reputation system: ML's reputation scores (MercadoLider tiers) heavily influence visibility. Build order volume and maintain high satisfaction to unlock MercadoLider status.
- Mercado Pago ecosystem: Payments, lending, and buyer protection integrated. Sellers with good Mercado Pago standing get financing offers for inventory.

## Noon

### Overview

The leading homegrown e-commerce platform in the Middle East, founded in 2016
and headquartered in Riyadh. Noon operates in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt
with a focus on local relevance and competitive pricing against Amazon.ae.

### Markets Served

| Market | Cross-Border Seller Access | Fulfillment |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------|
| UAE | Open (seller registration) | Noon Fulfillment (FBN) |
| Saudi Arabia | Open (seller registration) | Noon Fulfillment (FBN) |
| Egypt | Open (seller registration) | Noon Fulfillment (FBN) |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Commission Rate | 5-25% (category dependent) | Electronics lower; fashion higher |
| Fulfillment Fee (FBN) | Varies by size/weight | Competitive with Amazon FBA.ae |
| Advertising (Noon Ads) | CPC $0.05-$0.40 | Less mature than Amazon Ads |
| Payment Processing | Included in commission | No separate fee |
| Registration | Free | No monthly subscription fee |

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| FBN (Fulfilled by Noon) | Ship to Noon warehouse; Noon handles fulfillment | Recommended for all sellers; visibility boost |
| Seller-managed | Seller arranges own delivery | Sellers with local 3PL presence |
| Express Delivery (Noon Now) | Same-day/next-day in select cities | FMCG, grocery, convenience items |

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Sponsored Products | Keyword-targeted search ads | $0.05-$0.30 |
| Homepage Banners | Featured placement | CPM/fixed price |
| Campaign Participation | Yellow Friday, Noon Minutes, Mega Sales | Fee-based or invitation |

**Advertising maturity:** Still developing compared to Amazon. Lower CPC and
less competition for ad placements. Early movers benefit from cheap traffic.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Trade license required for UAE sellers; Saudi commercial registration for SA
- **Capital requirement:** $3,000-$10,000 for initial inventory + FBN setup
- **Language:** Arabic product content preferred; English accepted but Arabic performs better
- **Certifications:** ESMA/SASO compliance for applicable products
- **Halal:** Required for food, cosmetics, personal care products
- **Competition with Amazon:** Noon competes primarily on price; margin pressure in overlapping categories

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Electronics & Gadgets | Strong demand; price-competitive positioning |
| Fashion & Accessories | Growing category, especially modest fashion |
| Beauty & Perfume | Cultural significance; high demand for fragrance |
| Home & Kitchen | Growing household spending |
| Grocery (Noon Minutes) | Rapid delivery grocery vertical |
| Baby & Kids | Young regional demographic |

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- Dual-platform strategy: Most successful MENA sellers operate on both Amazon.ae/Amazon.sa AND Noon. Category performance and pricing dynamics differ between platforms.
- Arabic content advantage: Sellers with Arabic content capabilities outperform English-only listings. Invest in Arabic copywriting and customer service.
- Noon competes on price: Noon positions against Amazon as the value alternative. Expect price matching pressure and frequent promotional campaigns.
- Ramadan is peak season: Ramadan + Eid al-Fitr is the biggest sales period in MENA (comparable to Q4 in the West). Plan inventory 2-3 months ahead.
- COD considerations: Cash-on-delivery is declining but still ~25-35% of orders. Budget for COD rejection rates (10-15%).

## Coupang

### Overview

South Korea's dominant e-commerce platform, often called "Korea's Amazon."
Listed on NYSE (2021). Known for Rocket Delivery (next-day and same-day)
powered by a massive owned logistics network of 100+ fulfillment centers.

### Markets Served

| Market | Cross-Border Seller Access | Fulfillment |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------|
| South Korea | Open (Global Marketplace program) | Rocket Delivery (Coupang Fulfillment), seller-managed |
| Taiwan | Expanding (Coupang acquired) | Developing |

### Seller Fees Overview

| Fee Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Commission Rate | 5-15% (category dependent) | Competitive with Amazon |
| Rocket Delivery Fulfillment | Varies by size/weight | Premium service; seller pays fulfillment fee |
| Advertising (Coupang Ads) | CPC $0.05-$0.50 | Growing ad platform |
| Registration | Free basic plan | Rocket Growth program for cross-border sellers |
| Payment Processing | Included | No separate fee |

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Rocket Delivery | Coupang's flagship: next-day/same-day delivery from Coupang FCs | Recommended for all sellers; dramatically better conversion |
| Rocket Growth | Cross-border program: ship from China to Coupang's Korean warehouse | China-based sellers entering Korea |
| Seller-managed | Seller handles fulfillment | Sellers with Korean warehouse/3PL |
| Rocket Jikgu (Direct Import) | Coupang-operated cross-border for curated brands | Invited brands only |

**Rocket Delivery dominance:** Products on Rocket Delivery convert dramatically better than non-Rocket products. For serious sellers, Rocket Delivery/Rocket Growth participation is essentially mandatory.

### Advertising Ecosystem

| Ad Type | Description | Typical CPC |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Sponsored Products | Search keyword targeting | $0.05-$0.40 |
| Brand Ads | Brand-level search visibility | $0.10-$0.50 |
| Display Ads | Homepage and category page placements | CPM-based |
| Rocket WOW Deals | Flash sale participation | Performance or fee-based |

**Advertising maturity:** Developing rapidly. Currently less competitive than
Amazon Ads, making ROI favorable. Expect increasing sophistication and costs
as the ad platform matures.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Korean language:** All listings, customer service, and communications must be in Korean. Non-negotiable.
- **KC certification:** Required for electronics, children's products, and many consumer goods. Cost: $2,000-$10,000+; timeline: 4-12 weeks.
- **Capital requirement:** $5,000-$20,000 for initial inventory + Rocket Growth setup
- **Local address/agent:** Korean business registration or local agent typically required
- **KFDA:** Food and cosmetics require KFDA approval (costly and time-consuming)
- **Consumer expectations:** Korean consumers expect fast delivery (same-day/next-day) and flawless product quality

### Best Product Categories

| Strong Categories | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| Beauty & Skincare | Korea is a global beauty hub; high demand for both domestic and imported products |
| Food & Grocery (Rocket Fresh) | Coupang's grocery vertical; fresh and packaged food |
| Fashion | Trend-driven market, fast turnover |
| Electronics & Gadgets | High consumer spending on tech |
| Baby & Kids | Low birth rate but high spending per child |
| Health Supplements | Growing health-conscious consumer base |
| Pet Supplies | Rapidly growing pet ownership |

### Cross-Border Seller Notes

- Rocket Growth is the entry path: China-based sellers ship to Coupang's Korean warehouses via the Rocket Growth program. This gets products into the Rocket Delivery ecosystem without a Korean entity.
- KC certification is non-negotiable: Do not attempt to list regulated products without proper KC certification. Coupang enforces strictly, and Korean customs will seize non-compliant products.
- Korean localization quality matters: Machine-translated listings perform poorly. Invest in native Korean copywriting. Korean consumers are sensitive to language quality.
- Trend cycles are fast: Korean consumer trends change rapidly (weeks, not months). Monitor Korean social media (Naver, KakaoTalk, Instagram Korea) and adapt listings/products accordingly.
- Delivery speed expectations: Korean consumers consider 2-day delivery slow. Rocket Delivery (next-day/same-day) is the baseline expectation. Non-Rocket products must compete on price.

### Step 4: Validate with Unit Economics

Before committing to a platform, calculate unit economics using this template:

```
Revenue per unit (selling price)          ___________
- Platform commission                     ___________
- Payment processing fee                  ___________
- Fulfillment fee (FBA/FBN/etc)          ___________
- Shipping to warehouse                   ___________
- Import duty + taxes                     ___________
- Product cost (COGS)                     ___________
- Advertising cost per unit (est.)        ___________
- Returns allowance (% of revenue)        ___________
= Net profit per unit                     ___________
  Net margin %                            ___________
```

**Minimum viable margin targets:**
- Amazon US/EU: >15% net margin after ads (to sustain)
- Shopee/Lazada SEA: >10% net margin (lower AOV, higher volume)
- TikTok Shop: >20% gross margin before ads (ad spend varies wildly)
- Mercado Libre: >15% net margin (account for installment delays)
- Noon: >15% net margin (account for COD rejections)
- Coupang: >15% net margin (premium positioning possible)

If unit economics do not work at realistic advertising costs, the platform-market
combination is not viable for that product. Revisit product selection or
market choice before proceeding.
