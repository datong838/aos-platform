## Amazon (US / EU / JP)

### Markets Served

- **Amazon US:** United States, with FBA export reaching Canada and Mexico (NARF).
- **Amazon EU:** Germany, UK, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Sweden, Poland,
  Belgium. Pan-EU FBA enables a single inventory pool to serve all EU markets.
- **Amazon JP:** Japan. Standalone marketplace with separate inventory.

Additional Amazon marketplaces exist (AU, IN, SG, AE, SA, BR, TR, EG) but are
smaller and not covered individually here.

### Monthly Active Buyers

- US: approximately 310 million monthly visitors, approximately 200 million Prime
  members globally.
- EU: approximately 150 million monthly visitors across all EU storefronts.
- JP: approximately 55 million monthly visitors.

### Seller Fees

- **Referral fee (commission):** 8-20% depending on category. Most categories fall
  in the 12-15% range. Media categories are lower; fashion and jewelry are higher.
- **FBA fulfillment fee:** varies by product size and weight. Small standard items
  approximately $3.00-$5.50 per unit in the US. Large items significantly higher.
- **Monthly subscription:** $39.99/month (Professional plan, US). Required for
  serious sellers.
- **Storage fees:** monthly storage plus aged inventory surcharges for stock over
  180 days.
- **Effective total cost:** 25-40% of selling price when combining referral fee, FBA
  fees, and advertising.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon) | Amazon stores, picks, packs, ships. Prime eligible. | Most sellers; highest conversion rate |
| FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant) | Seller handles all fulfillment | Large/heavy items; low-volume testing |
| SFP (Seller Fulfilled Prime) | Seller ships but earns Prime badge | High-volume sellers with own warehouse |
| Pan-EU FBA | Single inventory serves all EU marketplaces | EU multi-market sellers |
| EFN (European Fulfillment Network) | Store in one EU country, ship to others on demand | Low-volume EU expansion |
| Multi-Country Inventory | Distribute stock across EU warehouses | High-volume EU sellers needing fast delivery |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: High.** The most developed advertising platform among all marketplaces.

- **Sponsored Products:** PPC keyword and product targeting. The primary ad type.
  Average CPC $0.80-$3.00+ (US), lower in EU/JP.
- **Sponsored Brands:** Banner ads with brand logo and multiple products. Requires
  Brand Registry.
- **Sponsored Display:** Retargeting and audience targeting on and off Amazon.
- **Sponsored TV / Streaming TV:** Video ads on Prime Video and other streaming
  properties.
- **Amazon DSP:** Programmatic display and video across Amazon's ad network.
- **Brand Analytics:** Detailed search query data, market basket analysis, customer
  demographics.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires business entity documentation, valid credit card, tax
  ID. US requires EIN or SSN. EU requires VAT registration in at least one EU
  country.
- **Brand Registry:** Recommended for brand protection and A+ Content access.
  Requires registered trademark.
- **Category approvals:** Some categories gated (beauty, grocery, automotive parts).
  Requires applications with invoices or compliance documentation.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. Well-documented process, but VAT for EU and category
  gating can delay launch by 2-8 weeks.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** Every 14 days. Funds available after a 14-day hold from the
  order date.
- **Currency:** Pays in local marketplace currency. Currency conversion available via
  Amazon Currency Converter for Sellers (ACCS) or third-party services (Payoneer,
  WorldFirst).
- **Reserve:** Amazon may hold a reserve for new sellers (typically 7-14 days of
  sales) which releases as performance history builds.

### Best Product Categories

Consumer electronics accessories, home and kitchen, beauty and personal care, pet
supplies, sports and outdoors, tools and home improvement, toys, baby products.

### Competitive Landscape

Highly competitive in most categories. Top categories have thousands of active
sellers. Winning requires strong product differentiation, optimized listings,
professional images, and consistent advertising investment. Review accumulation
creates a significant moat for established sellers.

### Growth Trajectory

**Mature.** US marketplace is the largest globally. Growth continues at 8-12%
annually but the rate of new seller entry exceeds overall growth, increasing
competition year over year. EU and JP continue to grow faster in percentage terms.

## Shopee

### Markets Served

Southeast Asia (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam,
Taiwan), Brazil, Mexico, Poland, Chile, Colombia. Each market has a separate
localized storefront.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 350 million monthly active users across all markets. Indonesia, Brazil,
and Thailand are the largest individual markets.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 2-10% depending on market and seller tier. New sellers often get
  promotional 0% commission for the first 3-6 months.
- **Transaction fee:** 2-3% payment processing fee on top of commission.
- **Shipping subsidy:** Shopee subsidizes shipping in many markets; seller contributes
  a portion.
- **Effective total cost:** 8-18% of selling price including commission, transaction
  fee, and shipping contribution.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| SLS (Shopee Logistics Service) | Shopee's integrated logistics network with pickup and delivery | Most sellers; primary option |
| SPX (Shopee Express) | Shopee's own last-mile delivery (select markets) | Faster delivery in covered areas |
| 3PL partners | Third-party logistics integrated with Shopee | Cross-border sellers with regional warehouses |
| Self-ship | Seller arranges own shipping (limited availability) | Large or special items |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium.** Functional but less sophisticated than Amazon.

- **Shopee Ads (keyword search ads):** CPC-based ads in search results. Primary ad
  type. CPCs generally $0.05-$0.50 (significantly lower than Amazon).
- **Discovery Ads:** Product recommendations in browse feeds.
- **Shopee Live:** Live streaming commerce integrated directly into the platform.
  Strong in SEA markets.
- **Affiliate Marketing:** Shopee's affiliate program connects sellers with
  influencers and publishers.
- **Vouchers and flash deals:** Platform-sponsored promotional events that sellers
  can join (usually with margin contribution).

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Relatively easy. Individual sellers can register with ID. Cross-
  border sellers register through Shopee's Cross-Border Seller Center.
- **Category restrictions:** Fewer gated categories than Amazon.
- **Difficulty:** Low. The easiest major platform to start selling on. The challenge
  is not entry but profitability due to price sensitivity.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** 2-5 days after delivery confirmation (varies by market).
  Faster than most platforms.
- **Currency:** Local currency per market. Payoneer and other services handle
  conversion for cross-border sellers.
- **Note:** Cash-on-delivery (COD) is dominant in Indonesia and Philippines (70%+),
  which introduces rejection risk (15-30% of COD orders may be refused).

### Best Product Categories

Fashion and accessories, beauty and personal care, home and living, consumer
electronics accessories, phone accessories, health and wellness, baby products.
Low-to-mid price point items ($3-$30) perform best.

### Competitive Landscape

Extremely price-competitive. Southeast Asian consumers are highly price-sensitive,
and Shopee's platform design encourages price comparison. Sellers compete primarily
on price, shipping speed, and promotional activity. Brand loyalty is lower than on
Amazon. High seller volume but lower average revenue per seller.

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing.** SEA e-commerce penetration continues to increase. Shopee is the leading
platform in most SEA markets. Brazil and LATAM markets represent significant growth
opportunities. Key risk: parent company Sea Limited cycles through profitability
focus and growth investment, which affects seller subsidies and fee structures.

## Lazada

### Markets Served

Southeast Asia: Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam.
Backed by Alibaba Group.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 160 million monthly active users across SEA markets.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 1-8% depending on category and seller tier. LazMall (brand mall)
  sellers typically pay higher commission but gain trust badges and premium placement.
- **Payment fee:** 1-2% transaction processing.
- **Effective total cost:** 6-15% of selling price.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| LGS (Lazada Global Shipping) | Cross-border fulfillment with China warehouses | China-based cross-border sellers |
| Lazada Fulfillment | Lazada warehouse fulfillment (select markets) | Local inventory sellers wanting faster delivery |
| 3PL | Third-party logistics partners | Sellers with established regional logistics |
| Self-ship | Seller-managed shipping | Niche or oversized items |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium.** Alibaba's ad technology applied to SEA.

- **Sponsored Search:** Keyword-based CPC ads.
- **Sponsored Discovery:** Product recommendations in feeds.
- **Sponsored Affiliate:** Affiliate marketing network.
- **LazLive:** Live streaming commerce.
- **Campaign participation:** Mega sales events (9.9, 11.11, 12.12) are critical for
  visibility and volume.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires business registration or individual ID. LazMall requires
  brand authorization and business license.
- **Cross-border:** Relatively straightforward for China-based sellers through LGS.
- **Difficulty:** Low-Medium. Slightly more structured than Shopee, especially for
  LazMall tier.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** 7-14 days after delivery confirmation.
- **Currency:** Local currency per market.

### Best Product Categories

Electronics, fashion, home and living, baby, beauty. Lazada skews slightly more
premium than Shopee, with LazMall attracting brand-conscious buyers.

### Competitive Landscape

Competitive but less price-driven than Shopee. LazMall provides a premium tier that
rewards brand investment. Alibaba's backing brings infrastructure advantages but
also operational complexity (frequent platform changes, shifting seller priorities).

### Growth Trajectory

**Mature (SEA).** Lazada is the second-largest marketplace in most SEA markets
behind Shopee. Growth continues but market share has been gradually ceding to Shopee
and TikTok Shop in several markets. Alibaba's continued investment signals long-term
commitment.

## TikTok Shop

### Markets Served

United States, United Kingdom, Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam,
Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore), LATAM markets (expanding). Global expansion
ongoing.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Over 200 million monthly active TikTok Shop users (across all markets). The user
base leverages TikTok's 1.5+ billion monthly active users, with shop functionality
reaching a growing percentage.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 2-8% depending on category and market. US typically 5-8%. SEA
  markets as low as 2-4%. New seller promotional rates may apply.
- **Transaction fee:** 1-2% payment processing.
- **Effective total cost:** 5-15% of selling price (lower than Amazon but creator
  commissions can add 10-30% per affiliate sale).

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| FBT (Fulfilled by TikTok) | TikTok warehouses handle fulfillment (US, expanding) | US sellers wanting fast shipping |
| 3PL | Third-party logistics partners integrated with TikTok | Established sellers with logistics partnerships |
| Self-ship | Seller handles all fulfillment | Low-volume or testing phase |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium-Low.** Rapidly developing but still behind Amazon and Meta.

- **Product Shopping Ads:** In-feed ads linking directly to product pages.
- **Live Shopping Ads:** Ads promoting live selling sessions.
- **Video Shopping Ads:** Shoppable short-form video content.
- **Creator Marketplace / Affiliate:** Sellers set commission rates; creators produce
  content driving sales. This is the dominant sales channel for many TikTok Shop
  sellers.
- **Shop Ads (Spark Ads):** Boosted organic content from creators.
- **Note:** Creator/affiliate-driven sales often outperform self-managed ads. Budget
  allocation should account for both.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires business license and ID. US requires SSN or EIN.
  Process is generally straightforward.
- **Content requirement:** Unlike search-based marketplaces, TikTok Shop requires
  video and/or live content to drive sales. Sellers without content creation
  capability (in-house or via creators) will struggle.
- **Difficulty:** Low-Medium for registration. High for success without content/
  creator strategy.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** 15 days from delivery confirmation (US). Varies by market.
  Dispute hold windows can extend this.
- **Currency:** Local currency per market.

### Best Product Categories

Beauty and personal care, fashion and accessories, home and kitchen gadgets, health
and wellness, novelty and trending items, items under $50 with visual/demonstrable
appeal. Products that photograph or demonstrate well in video format have a strong
advantage.

### Competitive Landscape

Early-stage competition in most categories. First-mover advantage is real but
temporary. The platform rewards content quality and creator relationships over
traditional listing optimization. Sellers with strong video content or creator
networks have a significant edge.

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing rapidly.** TikTok Shop is the fastest-growing major e-commerce platform
globally. US GMV grew substantially year-over-year. SEA markets (especially
Indonesia) are already significant. Major risk: regulatory uncertainty in the US
and potential bans/divestitures could disrupt operations.

## Walmart Marketplace

### Markets Served

United States. Walmart also operates international marketplaces (Mexico, Canada) but
the US marketplace is the primary opportunity for cross-border sellers.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 120 million monthly unique visitors to Walmart.com. Growing online
presence backed by 4,700+ physical stores in the US.

### Seller Fees

- **Referral fee (commission):** 6-20% depending on category. Most categories fall
  in the 8-15% range. Slightly lower than Amazon in many categories.
- **WFS fulfillment fee:** Comparable to Amazon FBA. Small standard items
  approximately $3.00-$5.00 per unit.
- **No monthly subscription fee.** (Unlike Amazon's $39.99/month.)
- **Effective total cost:** 20-35% of selling price with WFS and advertising.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| WFS (Walmart Fulfillment Services) | Walmart warehouses handle fulfillment; W+ tag eligible | Sellers wanting Walmart+ visibility and fast shipping |
| 3PL / self-ship | Seller manages fulfillment; must meet delivery SLAs | Established sellers with US fulfillment infrastructure |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium.** Developing rapidly but still behind Amazon.

- **Sponsored Products:** CPC keyword-based ads. Primary ad type.
- **Sponsored Brands:** Brand awareness ads (limited rollout).
- **Display Ads:** On-site display targeting via Walmart Connect.
- **Walmart DSP:** Programmatic advertising leveraging Walmart's first-party shopper
  data (powerful but requires minimum spend).
- **CPCs:** Generally 30-50% lower than Amazon for equivalent categories, reflecting
  lower competition.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Application-based. Requires US business tax ID (EIN), US
  warehouse or fulfillment address, track record of marketplace or e-commerce sales.
  Cross-border sellers typically need a US entity or registered agent.
- **Approval process:** Walmart reviews applications and approves selectively. Not
  open registration like Amazon or Shopee.
- **Difficulty:** Medium-High. The approval process and US entity requirement create
  real barriers for international sellers.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** Every 14 days via ACH to a US bank account. Payoneer
  accepted as a virtual US bank alternative.
- **Currency:** USD only.

### Best Product Categories

Home and furniture, electronics, toys, grocery and consumables, health and wellness,
auto parts. Categories that align with Walmart's core brick-and-mortar strengths
perform best online.

### Competitive Landscape

Significantly less competitive than Amazon. Fewer active sellers (approximately
150,000 vs. Amazon's 2 million+). Lower review counts needed to compete on page 1.
Early-mover advantage still available in many categories. The trade-off is lower
overall traffic volume.

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing.** Walmart is aggressively investing in marketplace growth, and e-commerce
is a strategic priority. Walmart+ membership growing. Online grocery driving traffic
that cross-pollinates with marketplace purchases. Walmart's physical store network
provides unique fulfillment advantages (ship from store, in-store pickup).

## Mercado Libre

### Markets Served

Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, plus 13 additional Latin American
countries. Brazil and Mexico are the dominant markets representing approximately 75%
of GMV.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 90 million unique active buyers. The largest e-commerce platform in
Latin America by a significant margin.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 11-22% depending on category and listing type. Higher than most
  platforms. "Classico" (basic) listings are cheaper but get less visibility.
  "Premium" listings get top placement but cost more.
- **Mercado Pago fee:** 4-5% for payment processing (integrated).
- **Effective total cost:** 18-30% of selling price.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Mercado Envios Full | Mercado Libre warehouse fulfillment (similar to FBA) | High-volume sellers in BR/MX |
| Mercado Envios Flex | Seller ships but uses Mercado Libre's logistics network | Sellers with own warehouse |
| Mercado Envios Places | Buyer picks up at collection point | Reducing last-mile costs |
| Cross-border (CBT) | Ship directly from China/origin to Latin American buyers | Low-volume cross-border testing |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium.** Functional PPC system, less sophisticated targeting than Amazon.

- **Product Ads:** CPC-based sponsored product listings in search results.
- **Display Ads:** Banner ads on category and product pages.
- **Mercado Shops:** Branded storefronts within the marketplace.
- **Promotional tools:** Flash deals, coupons, and participation in platform-wide
  sales events.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires local tax ID (CPF/CNPJ in Brazil, RFC in Mexico) or
  cross-border registration through Mercado Libre's CBT program.
- **Cross-border complexity:** Brazil has notoriously high import taxes (up to 60%
  on imported goods) and complex customs processes. Mexico is more accessible.
- **Language:** Listings must be in Portuguese (Brazil) or Spanish (other markets).
  Translation quality matters significantly.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. Registration is manageable, but Brazilian tax/import
  complexity is a real barrier.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** 14-21 days. New sellers may face longer holds.
- **Currency:** Local currency (BRL, MXN, ARS, etc.). Mercado Pago handles
  conversion.

### Best Product Categories

Consumer electronics, auto parts and accessories, home and garden, fashion, beauty,
sports. Price sensitivity is high but brand awareness is growing.

### Competitive Landscape

Less competitive than Amazon in most categories. Chinese cross-border sellers are
present but face logistical and tax challenges (especially in Brazil). Local sellers
have shipping speed and tax advantages. Winning requires competitive pricing with
fast delivery via Envios Full.

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing.** Latin American e-commerce penetration is still relatively low (10-15%),
providing long runway. Mercado Libre's integrated ecosystem (payments, logistics,
credit) creates a strong moat. Key risks: currency volatility (especially Argentine
peso), political/regulatory instability in some markets.

## Noon

### Markets Served

United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Egypt. The leading homegrown marketplace in the
Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 30 million monthly active users across UAE, KSA, and Egypt.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 5-27% depending on category. Electronics lower, fashion higher.
- **Noon Fulfillment (FBN) fees:** Comparable to regional 3PL rates. Includes pick,
  pack, ship.
- **Effective total cost:** 12-35% of selling price.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| FBN (Fulfilled by Noon) | Noon warehouse fulfillment in UAE/KSA | Sellers wanting fast delivery and Noon Express badge |
| Self-ship / 3PL | Seller manages fulfillment | Niche sellers, oversized items |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Low-Medium.** Basic but developing.

- **Sponsored Products:** CPC-based ads in search results.
- **Banners and display:** Category page placements.
- **Noon campaigns:** Participation in platform-wide sales events (Yellow Friday,
  seasonal sales).
- **Limited targeting sophistication.** Ad tools are simpler than Amazon or Shopee.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires trade license (UAE or KSA), or cross-border seller
  agreement. Product compliance requirements vary by category.
- **Halal certification:** Required for food, cosmetics, and personal care products.
- **Import regulations:** ESMA (UAE) and SASO (KSA) product standards apply to many
  categories.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. The regulatory and certification landscape is complex for
  certain categories.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** 14-30 days depending on seller tier and track record.
- **Currency:** AED (UAE), SAR (KSA), EGP (Egypt).

### Best Product Categories

Electronics and accessories, beauty and personal care, fashion, home and kitchen,
baby products. Premium and branded items perform well given high per-capita spending
in UAE and KSA.

### Competitive Landscape

Moderate competition. Fewer sellers than Amazon or Shopee. Amazon (via Souq.ae
acquisition) is the primary competitor in the region. Noon differentiates with local
focus and Arabic-first experience. Chinese cross-border sellers are active but face
certification and logistics barriers.

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing.** MENA e-commerce is underpenetrated relative to income levels. UAE and
KSA are investing heavily in digital commerce infrastructure. Saudi Vision 2030
is driving e-commerce adoption. Egypt represents a large but lower-spending market.

## Coupang

### Markets Served

South Korea. The dominant domestic e-commerce platform (often called "the Amazon of
South Korea").

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 22 million active customers. In a country of approximately 52 million
people, this represents deep market penetration.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 10-15% depending on category.
- **Fulfillment fees:** Coupang's Rocket Delivery logistics cost varies by product
  size and weight.
- **Effective total cost:** 18-30% of selling price.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Rocket Delivery (FLC - Fulfillment and Logistics by Coupang) | Coupang's own fulfillment centers; next-day or same-day delivery | Most sellers; required for Rocket badge |
| 3PL / self-ship | Seller manages logistics | Cross-border or niche sellers |
| Rocket Global | Cross-border shipping with Coupang logistics support | International sellers without Korean warehousing |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium.** Functional PPC with growing capabilities.

- **Sponsored Products (Rocket Growth Ads):** CPC-based keyword ads.
- **Brand Ads:** Display ads for brand awareness.
- **Coupang Media Solutions:** Programmatic display across Coupang properties.
- **CPCs:** Generally moderate, competitive for high-demand categories.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires Korean business registration number (BRN) or
  cross-border partnership. International sellers typically need a Korean entity or
  partner.
- **Language:** All listings must be in Korean. Quality translation is essential.
- **Logistics:** Rocket Delivery integration is practically required for competitive
  visibility.
- **Difficulty:** Medium-High. Korean entity requirement and language barrier create
  significant friction for non-Korean sellers.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** Net monthly (approximately 30 days).
- **Currency:** KRW (Korean Won).

### Best Product Categories

Fresh food and grocery, beauty and cosmetics (K-beauty is a major export but also
strong domestic demand), fashion, home and living, electronics, health supplements.

### Competitive Landscape

Highly competitive domestically. Korean consumers are sophisticated, brand-aware, and
expect next-day delivery. Cross-border sellers face competition from established
Korean domestic sellers with superior logistics speed. Winning requires strong
product differentiation and willingness to use Coupang's fulfillment system.

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing.** Coupang continues to grow revenue and expand beyond e-commerce into food
delivery (Coupang Eats), video streaming (Coupang Play), and fintech. The Rocket
Delivery ecosystem creates strong buyer loyalty and repeat purchase behavior.

## Ozon

### Markets Served

Russia and CIS (Commonwealth of Independent States) countries. The leading online
marketplace in Russia.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 50 million monthly active buyers in Russia and CIS markets.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 3-15% depending on category. Consumer electronics lower, fashion
  and beauty higher.
- **Fulfillment fee:** Varies by product dimensions and fulfillment method.
- **Logistics fee:** Per-order shipping charge handled by Ozon logistics.
- **Effective total cost:** 12-25% of selling price.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Ozon Fulfillment (FBO) | Ozon warehouse storage and fulfillment | Sellers wanting full logistics outsourcing |
| FBS (Fulfilled by Seller, Ozon shipping) | Seller stores inventory, Ozon handles delivery | Sellers with own warehouse near Ozon hubs |
| Cross-border | Ship directly from China or other origin country | Testing market; willing to accept longer delivery times |
| 3PL | Third-party logistics with Ozon integration | Established regional logistics operations |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Medium.** Developed ad platform with decent targeting.

- **Sponsored Products:** CPC-based ads in search and category pages.
- **Brand Shelf:** Branded product groups in search results.
- **Banner Ads:** Display ads on category and main pages.
- **External Performance Marketing:** Drive traffic from external sources via Ozon
  tracking links.
- **Ozon Premium:** Enhanced product placement for premium sellers.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** Requires Russian legal entity or cross-border seller
  registration. Cross-border sellers can register from some countries but face
  sanctions-related restrictions.
- **Sanctions and payments:** International payment processing is complicated by
  Western sanctions on Russia. Cross-border sellers may face challenges receiving
  payments.
- **Language:** Listings must be in Russian.
- **Difficulty:** Medium-High. Sanctions-related payment and banking complications
  are the primary barrier for Western sellers. Chinese sellers face fewer issues.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** 14-30 days depending on fulfillment method.
- **Currency:** RUB (Russian Ruble). Currency volatility is a significant risk.

### Best Product Categories

Electronics and accessories, fashion, home and garden, beauty, auto parts, children's
products, sports equipment.

### Competitive Landscape

Moderate competition. Western brands have largely exited Russia, creating
opportunities for Chinese and other Asian sellers. Domestic Russian sellers are
active but product selection is narrower than in Western markets. Ozon competes
with Wildberries (the other major Russian marketplace).

### Growth Trajectory

**Growing.** Russian e-commerce penetration is increasing rapidly. The exit of
Western brands has created category gaps that new sellers can fill. Key risks:
geopolitical sanctions, currency instability, regulatory changes, payment processing
challenges for international sellers.

## Allegro

### Markets Served

Poland (primary market, dominant position) and Czech Republic (expanding). The
largest e-commerce platform in Central and Eastern Europe.

### Monthly Active Buyers

Approximately 25 million registered buyers, primarily in Poland (population
approximately 38 million). Very high domestic penetration.

### Seller Fees

- **Commission:** 5-13% depending on category.
- **Listing fee:** Small per-listing fees (unlike Amazon's subscription model).
- **Allegro One Fulfillment fees:** Per-unit fulfillment cost comparable to regional
  3PL.
- **Effective total cost:** 10-20% of selling price.

### Fulfillment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Allegro One Fulfillment | Allegro warehouse fulfillment with fast delivery | Sellers wanting the Allegro Smart! badge |
| Self-ship / courier | Seller arranges shipping via InPost, DHL, DPD, etc. | Most sellers currently |
| InPost lockers | Delivery to automated parcel lockers (dominant last-mile in Poland) | Cost-effective, convenient for buyers |

### Advertising Ecosystem

**Maturity: Low-Medium.** Basic but effective for the market.

- **Sponsored Offers:** CPC-based product ads in search results.
- **Display Ads:** Banner ads on category pages.
- **Allegro Ads platform:** Self-serve advertising dashboard.
- **Campaign participation:** Seasonal events and promotional campaigns.

### Barriers to Entry

- **Registration:** EU-based business or cross-border seller registration. Polish
  entity not strictly required but helpful. VAT registration in Poland recommended.
- **Language:** Listings must be in Polish. Quality translation is critical; Polish
  consumers are particular about language quality.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. Straightforward registration, but Polish-language
  requirement and EU VAT compliance create friction for non-EU sellers.

### Payment Terms

- **Payout frequency:** Every 14 days via bank transfer.
- **Currency:** PLN (Polish Zloty). EUR accepted for cross-border sellers.

### Best Product Categories

Electronics and accessories, fashion, home and garden, automotive parts, sports and
outdoors, children's products, beauty. Wide category coverage similar to Amazon.

### Competitive Landscape

Moderate competition. Allegro dominates Polish e-commerce with approximately 40%
market share. Amazon entered Poland in 2021 but has not yet significantly eroded
Allegro's position. Local sellers have trust advantages. Chinese cross-border sellers
are present but face language and logistics barriers.

### Growth Trajectory

**Mature (Poland), Growing (CEE expansion).** Allegro has saturated the Polish market
but is expanding to Czech Republic and potentially other CEE countries. The platform
is investing in fulfillment infrastructure and advertising tools. Key opportunity:
established dominance in a large, growing European market with less competition than
Western EU marketplaces.
