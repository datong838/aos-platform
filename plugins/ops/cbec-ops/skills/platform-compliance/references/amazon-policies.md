## Selling Policies Overview

### Amazon Seller Code of Conduct

Amazon's Seller Code of Conduct establishes the baseline expectations for all marketplace
participants. Key principles:

- **Act fairly**: Do not engage in practices that harm other sellers, buyers, or the
  marketplace.
- **Be truthful**: All information provided to Amazon and buyers must be accurate.
- **Provide a good buyer experience**: Meet or exceed buyer expectations on product
  quality, shipping speed, and customer service.
- **Do not abuse Amazon's systems**: Do not manipulate search rankings, reviews, or any
  other marketplace mechanism.
- **Follow the law**: Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in every jurisdiction
  where you sell.

### Seller Performance Standards

Amazon expects sellers to maintain the following minimum performance levels:

| Metric | Target | Maximum Allowed |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Order Defect Rate (ODR) | < 0.5% | 1% |
| Late Shipment Rate | < 2% | 4% |
| Pre-Fulfillment Cancel Rate | < 1% | 2.5% |
| Valid Tracking Rate | > 98% | 95% minimum |
| Customer Response Time | < 12 hours | 24 hours |

Exceeding the maximum threshold on any metric triggers a warning. Sustained violations
lead to listing deactivation or account suspension.

## Product Listing Policies

### Restricted Products

Amazon maintains a list of products that are prohibited outright or restricted to approved
sellers. Categories include but are not limited to:

**Prohibited**:
- Illegal substances and drug paraphernalia.
- Weapons and ammunition (with limited exceptions).
- Surveillance equipment marketed for illegal use.
- Products that promote hate or violence.
- Recalled products.

**Restricted (require approval)**:
- Dietary supplements.
- Topical products (cosmetics, skincare).
- Children's products (toys, clothing).
- Electronics with wireless capabilities.
- Hazardous materials.
- Pesticides and pest control devices.
- Alcohol, tobacco, and related products.

Before listing any product, verify it is not on Amazon's restricted products list and that
you have any required category approvals.

### Condition Guidelines

Products must be listed in the correct condition:

- **New**: Factory sealed, unused, undamaged, with all original packaging and accessories.
- **Renewed (Refurbished)**: Inspected and tested to work like new, with minimal cosmetic
  imperfections. Requires enrollment in Amazon Renewed program.
- **Used**: Must accurately describe the condition (Like New, Very Good, Good, Acceptable)
  with honest disclosure of any defects.

Listing a used product as new is a serious violation that frequently leads to account
suspension.

### Intellectual Property

- Do not use trademarked brand names, logos, or copyrighted images without authorization.
- Do not create listings on branded ASINs unless you are selling genuine products from that
  brand.
- Register your own brand through Amazon Brand Registry to gain additional protections.
- Respond to IP complaints within 3 business days; repeated complaints without resolution
  lead to listing and account-level actions.

### Listing Content Rules

- Titles must not exceed 200 characters (some categories cap at 80).
- No promotional language in titles (e.g., "SALE," "FREE SHIPPING," "BEST SELLER").
- No HTML, excessive punctuation, or ALL CAPS in listing content.
- Product descriptions must accurately represent the item.
- Images must follow Amazon's image requirements (see product-listing skill references).
- Backend search terms: 250 bytes maximum, no competitor brand names or ASINs.

## Review Manipulation

### What Is Prohibited

Amazon strictly prohibits any attempt to influence, manipulate, or fabricate customer
reviews. Specific prohibitions include:

- Offering compensation (money, discounts, free products, gift cards) in exchange for
  reviews.
- Using review services, Facebook groups, or any third-party review exchange networks.
- Inserting product inserts that ask for positive reviews, offer incentives for reviews,
  or direct customers to leave reviews only if they are satisfied.
- Asking family, friends, or employees to leave reviews.
- Reviewing your own products from any account.
- Selectively requesting reviews only from customers you believe had a positive experience.
- Voting on reviews (helpful/unhelpful) to manipulate visibility.
- Reporting legitimate negative reviews as abuse to get them removed.

### What Is Allowed

- Using Amazon's "Request a Review" button in Seller Central (the standard automated
  review request).
- Including a neutral product insert that thanks the customer and provides contact
  information for support (no review solicitation).
- Enrolling in Amazon Vine for new products (Amazon's official early reviewer program).
- Responding to customer reviews through the official comment feature.

### Consequences of Review Manipulation

- First offense: warning or listing-level review suppression.
- Repeat offenses: permanent removal of all product reviews, listing deactivation, or
  account suspension.
- Severe cases (review farms, large-scale manipulation): immediate permanent account ban
  with forfeiture of pending disbursements.
- Legal action: Amazon has pursued civil litigation against sellers engaged in systematic
  review fraud.

## Communication Policies

### Buyer-Seller Messaging Rules

All communication with buyers must go through Amazon's buyer-seller messaging system
unless the buyer initiates contact through another channel.

**Permitted messages**:
- Responding to buyer inquiries about products or orders.
- Proactive messages necessary to complete an order (shipping delays, product
  clarifications, substitution requests).
- Messages resolving returns, refunds, or customer service issues.

**Prohibited messages**:
- Marketing or promotional content of any kind.
- Requests for positive reviews or seller feedback.
- Links to external websites (including your own store).
- Requests for the buyer's email address, phone number, or other contact information.
- Attachments not related to the specific order.
- Messages after the order is complete unless the buyer contacts you first.

**Communication style rules**:
- Respond within 24 hours (target: 12 hours).
- Use professional, neutral language.
- Do not ask buyers to remove or change negative feedback or reviews.
- Do not threaten or pressure buyers in any way.
- Include order ID in all order-related messages.

## Account Health Dashboard

### Key Metrics Explained

#### Order Defect Rate (ODR)

The ODR is the most critical metric for account health. It includes:

- **A-to-Z Guarantee claims** (not withdrawn by the buyer).
- **Chargebacks** (credit card disputes).
- **Negative feedback** (1-2 star seller feedback).

**Threshold**: Must remain below 1%. Amazon recommends below 0.5%.

**Calculation period**: Rolling 60-day window.

**Impact of breaching**: Warning at first breach; repeated breaches lead to suspension
of selling privileges.

#### Late Shipment Rate

Percentage of orders confirmed shipped after the expected ship date.

**Threshold**: Must remain below 4%.

**How to manage**:
- Set realistic handling times.
- Upload tracking information before the ship-by date.
- Use FBA to eliminate merchant-fulfilled shipping risks.

#### Pre-Fulfillment Cancel Rate

Percentage of orders cancelled by the seller before shipment.

**Threshold**: Must remain below 2.5%.

**How to manage**:
- Maintain accurate inventory counts.
- Sync inventory across channels in near real-time.
- Do not list products you cannot fulfill within the stated handling time.

#### Valid Tracking Rate

Percentage of packages with valid tracking information uploaded.

**Threshold**: Must remain above 95% (recommended: 98%+).

**Requirements**:
- Tracking must be from a supported carrier.
- Tracking must show at least one carrier scan event.
- Tracking must be uploaded before the ship-by date.

### Account Health Rating (AHR)

Amazon's AHR is a composite score (displayed as Healthy, At Risk, or Critical) that
factors in:

- Policy violations (product policy, IP complaints, listing violations).
- Performance metrics (ODR, late shipment, cancellation rates).
- Buyer feedback patterns.

A "Critical" or "At Risk" rating requires immediate action. Check the Account Health
page daily when your rating is not "Healthy."

## Common Violations and Consequences

### Listing Deactivation

**Triggers**:
- Product does not match the ASIN detail page description.
- Listing contains prohibited claims (health, safety, or performance claims without
  substantiation).
- Images violate Amazon's image requirements.
- Product is in a restricted category without approval.
- Price is flagged as significantly above or below fair market value (pricing error
  detection).

**Resolution**: Fix the listing issue and reactivate through the "Fix your products" page
in Seller Central. No formal appeal required for most listing-level issues.

### ASIN Suspension

**Triggers**:
- IP complaint from a rights owner.
- Product authenticity complaint from a buyer.
- Product safety complaint.
- Used-sold-as-new complaint.
- Multiple listing-level violations on the same ASIN.

**Resolution**: Submit a Plan of Action (POA) through the ASIN-level appeal process.
Include invoices proving authenticity, corrective actions taken, and preventive measures.

### Account Suspension

**Triggers**:
- ODR exceeds 1% for a sustained period.
- Multiple ASIN-level suspensions within a short timeframe.
- Review manipulation detected.
- Multiple IP complaints without resolution.
- Selling counterfeit products.
- Operating multiple accounts without approval.
- Serious safety violations.

**Resolution**: Submit a comprehensive Plan of Action through the account-level appeal
process. Follow the Appeal Letter Framework in the main SKILL.md document.

## Appeal Letter Templates

### IP Complaint Appeal

Structure for responding to intellectual property complaints:

```
Subject: Plan of Action - ASIN [ASIN] - IP Complaint [Complaint ID]

1. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
We acknowledge the intellectual property complaint received on [date]
regarding ASIN [ASIN] ([product name]).

2. ROOT CAUSE
[Choose the applicable scenario]:
- We sourced this product from [supplier name], believing it to be
  genuine. Our verification process failed to detect [specific issue].
- The listing content inadvertently used [trademarked term/copyrighted
  image] in [title/images/description].
- The product design overlaps with [rights holder]'s protected design,
  which our product development review did not catch.

3. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS TAKEN
- Removed the affected listing immediately on [date].
- Pulled all remaining inventory of this product on [date].
- Contacted the rights owner at [contact method] on [date] to request
  a retraction (if applicable).
- Refunded all customers who purchased this product.

4. PREVENTIVE MEASURES
- Implemented a mandatory IP clearance check for all new products
  before listing. This check includes [specific steps].
- Added [brand/trademark] to our restricted sourcing list.
- All team members completed IP compliance training on [date].
- Scheduled quarterly audits of active listings for IP compliance.
```

### Inauthentic Product Appeal

```
Subject: Plan of Action - ASIN [ASIN] - Inauthentic/Product Condition

1. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
We acknowledge the inauthentic product complaint regarding ASIN [ASIN].

2. ROOT CAUSE
- We sourced this product from [supplier name/distributor name].
  Our invoice verification process did not sufficiently validate
  [specific gap: authorized distribution chain / product batch /
  packaging authenticity].

3. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS TAKEN
- Removed the listing on [date].
- Disposed of / returned all remaining inventory of this product
  on [date].
- Provided full refunds to all affected customers.
- Terminated our relationship with this supplier for this product.

4. PREVENTIVE MEASURES
- Updated our supplier vetting process to require [specific new
  requirements: authorized distributor letters, brand authorization
  certificates, supply chain documentation].
- Implemented incoming inspection checklist that includes [specific
  authenticity checks].
- All new product batches now undergo [specific verification process]
  before being shipped to Amazon fulfillment centers.
- Assigned [role/person] as compliance officer responsible for
  ongoing supplier and product authenticity verification.

ATTACHED: Invoices from authorized supplier, product authenticity
certificates, photos of inspection process.
```

### Safety Complaint Appeal

```
Subject: Plan of Action - ASIN [ASIN] - Product Safety

1. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
We acknowledge the safety concern raised regarding ASIN [ASIN].
Customer safety is our highest priority.

2. ROOT CAUSE
- The safety concern relates to [specific issue].
- Our pre-listing safety review did not identify this risk because
  [specific gap in process].

3. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS TAKEN
- Immediately removed the listing on [date].
- Initiated a voluntary recall of all units sold, contacting
  affected customers on [date].
- Removed all inventory from FBA fulfillment centers on [date].
- Commissioned independent third-party testing from [lab name,
  accreditation] on [date] to evaluate the specific safety concern.

4. PREVENTIVE MEASURES
- All products in this category now require third-party safety
  testing before listing (test report attached).
- Added [specific safety check] to our incoming quality inspection.
- Updated product specifications to address the identified safety
  issue with [specific design/material change].
- Implemented ongoing safety monitoring: review all customer feedback
  daily for safety-related keywords.

ATTACHED: Third-party test report, updated product safety
certificates, corrective action documentation.
```

### Review Manipulation Appeal

```
Subject: Plan of Action - Review Policy Violation

1. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
We acknowledge the review policy violation identified on our account.
We understand that Amazon prohibits any form of review manipulation.

2. ROOT CAUSE
- [Scenario A]: We included product insert cards that offered
  [specific incentive] in exchange for reviews. We did not fully
  understand that this violated Amazon's review policies.
- [Scenario B]: A team member / third-party service engaged in
  [specific prohibited activity] without proper authorization or
  understanding of Amazon's policies.
- [Scenario C]: We used Amazon's "Request a Review" feature
  appropriately, but a third-party service we contracted
  independently engaged in [prohibited activity] without our
  knowledge or consent.

3. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS TAKEN
- Immediately ceased all review solicitation activities beyond
  Amazon's built-in "Request a Review" button.
- Removed all product insert cards from inventory on [date].
- Terminated relationship with [third-party service] on [date].
- Audited all active listings to remove any review-related
  incentive language.

4. PREVENTIVE MEASURES
- Established a written company policy prohibiting any review
  manipulation or incentivized review activity.
- All team members signed acknowledgment of review policy on [date].
- Only Amazon's official "Request a Review" button will be used
  for review solicitation.
- Product inserts now contain only: thank you message, care
  instructions, and customer support contact (no review requests).
- Quarterly compliance audits include review practices review.
```

## Restricted Categories and Ungating

### How Ungating Works

Some Amazon categories require approval before you can list products. The approval process
(commonly called "ungating") typically requires:

1. **Application submission** through Seller Central.
2. **Invoices** from authorized suppliers (usually 3+ invoices from different dates).
3. **Product images** showing the product, packaging, and labels.
4. **Compliance documentation** (safety certificates, test reports) for regulated
   categories.
5. **Brand authorization** letters for gated brands within open categories.

### Common Restricted Categories

| Category | Typical Requirements | Difficulty |
|----------|---------------------|------------|
| Grocery & Gourmet Food | FDA compliance, invoices, product images | Medium |
| Health & Personal Care | FDA compliance, COA, invoices | Medium-High |
| Beauty | Invoices, product images, ingredients list | Medium |
| Toys & Games | CPC, CPSIA testing, invoices | Medium |
| Jewelry | Invoices, hallmark documentation | Medium |
| Watches | Brand authorization, invoices | High |
| Clothing & Accessories | Invoices, brand authorization | Low-Medium |
| Automotive | Invoices, fitment data | Medium |
| Sports Collectibles | LOA, invoices, authenticity proof | High |
| Fine Art | Provenance documentation | High |

### Ungating Tips

- Use invoices from reputable, verifiable wholesalers or distributors.
- Invoices must show your legal business name and address matching your Amazon account.
- Minimum quantity on invoices is typically 10+ units.
- Invoices must be dated within the last 365 days.
- Do not use invoices from retail purchases (Best Buy, Walmart, etc.).
- Some categories may require a professional selling plan and positive account history.
- If rejected, address the specific reason before reapplying; do not resubmit the same
  application unchanged.

## Product Safety and Compliance

### United States Regulatory Requirements

Cross-border sellers shipping to the US must comply with federal safety regulations. Key
agencies and requirements:

#### CPSC (Consumer Product Safety Commission)

Applies to: consumer products, especially children's products.

- **Children's products** (designed for children 12 and under) require:
  - Third-party testing by a CPSC-accepted laboratory.
  - Children's Product Certificate (CPC).
  - CPSIA compliance (lead content, phthalates).
  - Tracking labels on the product and packaging.
  - ASTM F963 compliance for toys.
- **General consumer products** require:
  - General Certificate of Conformity (GCC).
  - Compliance with applicable CPSC regulations.

#### FDA (Food and Drug Administration)

Applies to: food, dietary supplements, cosmetics, medical devices, and over-the-counter
drugs.

- **Food products**: facility registration, FDA-compliant labeling, ingredient listing.
- **Dietary supplements**: GMP compliance, proper labeling, no drug claims.
- **Cosmetics**: ingredient labeling, no prohibited ingredients, no drug claims.
- **OTC drugs**: drug facts labeling, NDC number, GMP compliance.

#### FCC (Federal Communications Commission)

Applies to: electronic devices that emit radio frequency energy.

- Devices must be tested and certified by an FCC-accredited lab.
- FCC ID or Declaration of Conformity must be obtained before sale.
- FCC label must be physically present on the device.
- Covers: Wi-Fi devices, Bluetooth devices, RF transmitters, computing devices.

#### Prop 65 (California)

- Products sold in California (or to California residents) containing listed chemicals
  must carry a Prop 65 warning.
- Warning must be on the product, packaging, or listing.
- Failure to warn can result in lawsuits from private enforcers (bounty hunters).
- Common categories affected: electronics, jewelry, food, supplements, household goods.

### Documentation to Maintain

For every product, keep on file:

- Test reports from accredited laboratories.
- Certificates of Conformity (CPC for children's products, GCC for general products).
- FCC certification documents (for electronics).
- FDA registration and compliance records (for applicable categories).
- Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for products containing chemicals.
- Insurance certificates (product liability insurance is strongly recommended).

## Recent Policy Changes

Amazon updates its policies frequently. Notable areas of recent change include:

### Evolving Areas to Monitor

- **Product claims enforcement**: Amazon is increasing scrutiny on unsubstantiated health,
  safety, and environmental claims. "Eco-friendly," "antibacterial," and similar claims
  now require supporting documentation.
- **Supply chain documentation**: Increased requirements for supply chain transparency,
  particularly for products in categories susceptible to counterfeiting.
- **Sustainability claims**: New requirements around "Climate Pledge Friendly" and
  sustainability certifications. Misleading green claims face enforcement.
- **AI-generated content**: Policies around AI-generated listing content, images, and
  reviews are actively being developed. Monitor Seller Central announcements.
- **Cross-border compliance**: Tighter enforcement of import documentation, customs
  compliance, and product marking requirements for international sellers.
- **Data privacy**: Updated requirements around handling buyer personal information,
  aligned with evolving global privacy regulations.

### How to Stay Updated

- Check **Seller Central News** dashboard daily.
- Subscribe to **Amazon Seller Forums** for your marketplace.
- Monitor the **Policy Compliance** section of Account Health weekly.
- Review **Program Policy** pages quarterly for changes to baseline rules.
- Follow industry publications and seller communities for early warnings about
  enforcement waves.

Always verify policy information directly in Seller Central, as this reference document
may not reflect the most recent changes.
