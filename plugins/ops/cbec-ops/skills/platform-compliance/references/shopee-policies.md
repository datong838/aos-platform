## Seller Code of Conduct

### Core Principles

Shopee expects sellers to maintain the following standards:

- **Honest and accurate listings**: Products must match their descriptions, images, and
  stated condition.
- **Timely order fulfillment**: Orders must be shipped within the platform's required
  timeframe.
- **Responsive communication**: Sellers must respond to buyer inquiries promptly.
- **Fair pricing**: No price gouging, bait-and-switch tactics, or misleading discount
  structures.
- **Legal compliance**: All products must comply with local laws and regulations in the
  market where they are sold.
- **No marketplace manipulation**: Sellers must not manipulate ratings, reviews, search
  rankings, or sales metrics through artificial means.

### Account Integrity

- One seller account per individual or business entity (unless approved for multiple
  accounts by Shopee).
- Account information must be accurate and current.
- Do not share account credentials or sell/transfer accounts.
- Business registration and identity verification documents must be valid.

## Penalty Points System

Shopee uses a penalty points system to enforce policy compliance. Points accumulate and
trigger increasingly severe consequences.

### How Points Work

- Points are assigned per violation, with values varying by severity.
- Points accumulate over a rolling period (typically 12 weeks, varies by market).
- Points expire after the rolling period ends.
- Accumulating points beyond specific thresholds triggers automatic penalties.

### Violation Categories and Point Values

| Violation Type | Points | Examples |
|---------------|--------|---------|
| **Prohibited listing** | 2-4 | Listing counterfeit goods, prohibited items, or regulated products without approval |
| **Counterfeit goods** | 4-6 | Selling fake branded products; severe cases result in immediate ban |
| **Spam listing** | 1-2 | Duplicate listings, keyword stuffing in irrelevant categories, misleading product titles |
| **Pre-order violation** | 1-2 | Exceeding pre-order shipping timeframes, not marking items as pre-order |
| **Late shipment** | 1 | Failing to ship within the required timeframe |
| **Non-fulfillment** | 2 | Cancelling orders after buyer payment, failing to ship entirely |
| **Chat policy violation** | 1-2 | Sharing external links, soliciting off-platform transactions |
| **Review manipulation** | 2-4 | Fake reviews, incentivized reviews, review exchanges |
| **IP infringement** | 2-4 | Using copyrighted images, trademarked names without authorization |

Note: Exact point values vary by market. Check your specific Shopee market's Seller
Education Hub for current values.

### Penalty Thresholds

| Point Threshold | Consequence |
|----------------|-------------|
| 3 points | Warning notification; no operational impact |
| 6 points | Listing visibility reduction; search ranking penalty |
| 9 points | Temporary account restriction (listing creation disabled) |
| 12 points | Account suspension (typically 7-28 days) |
| 15+ points | Permanent account ban (requires formal appeal) |

### Point Reduction

- Points expire automatically after the rolling period.
- Completing Shopee's compliance training courses may reduce points in some markets.
- Successfully appealing a violation removes the associated points.
- Maintaining a clean record accelerates point decay in some markets.

## Listing Policies

### Prohibited Items

Shopee prohibits the sale of products that are:

- Illegal in the seller's or buyer's jurisdiction.
- Counterfeit, replica, or infringing on intellectual property.
- Dangerous or hazardous without proper labeling and compliance.
- Adult or sexually explicit (policies vary by market).
- Live animals (most markets).
- Weapons, ammunition, and explosives.
- Drugs, drug paraphernalia, and unapproved pharmaceuticals.
- Stolen goods or goods obtained through illegal means.
- Human organs, blood, or bodily fluids.

Each market maintains its own specific restricted and prohibited products list. Always
check the local Shopee Seller Education Hub.

### Listing Quality Requirements

**Title**:
- Must accurately describe the product.
- No keyword spam or irrelevant keywords.
- No special characters used for decoration (stars, arrows, emojis used as separators are
  market-dependent).
- No promotional claims in the title (e.g., "CHEAPEST," "BEST SELLER").
- Recommended length: 25-100 characters depending on market.

**Images**:
- Minimum 3 images; recommended 5-9.
- Main image: clear product photo, clean background preferred.
- No watermarks, contact information, or promotional text on images (enforcement varies
  by market).
- Minimum resolution: 800 x 800 pixels.
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 recommended; 2:3 accepted in some markets.
- No stock photos that do not represent the actual product.

**Description**:
- Must accurately describe the product specifications, materials, dimensions, and
  contents.
- No external links or contact information.
- No requests for reviews or ratings.
- No disparaging comments about competitors.
- Formatting with line breaks and simple bullet points improves readability (rich HTML
  support varies by market).

**Pricing**:
- Price must reflect the actual selling price.
- Inflating the original price to create a fake discount is prohibited.
- Minimum price rules apply in some categories and markets.
- Prices must include any mandatory fees or charges.

**Category**:
- Products must be listed in the correct category.
- Miscategorization to gain visibility in less competitive categories is a violation.
- Category-specific attributes must be filled in accurately.

## Shipping Policies

### Shipping Timeframes

- **Standard orders**: Must be shipped within 2 days of order confirmation (market
  default; some markets allow 3 days).
- **Pre-order items**: Must be clearly marked as pre-order; extended shipping timeframes
  up to 7-30 days depending on market and category.
- **DTS (Days to Ship)**: Sellers can set custom DTS per listing within platform limits.

### Late Shipment Penalties

- Late shipments are tracked as a percentage of total orders.
- Each late shipment may incur 1 penalty point.
- Exceeding the late shipment rate threshold (varies by market, typically 5-10%) triggers
  listing visibility reduction.
- Persistent late shipping leads to loss of Preferred Seller status or account restriction.

### Non-Fulfillment Rate

- Seller-initiated cancellations count toward the non-fulfillment rate.
- The threshold is typically 5% (varies by market).
- Exceeding the threshold triggers penalty points and search ranking reduction.
- Cancellations due to stock-outs are particularly damaging to account health.

### Pre-Order Rules

- Pre-order items must be explicitly marked using the platform's pre-order feature.
- Not all categories permit pre-orders.
- Maximum pre-order shipping time varies by market (typically 7-15 days for standard
  categories, up to 30 days for customized items).
- Failing to fulfill pre-orders within the stated timeframe results in penalty points
  and mandatory refunds.

### Shipping Label and Tracking

- All orders must include a valid tracking number from a supported logistics partner.
- Sellers using Shopee's integrated logistics must follow the designated carrier's
  pickup or drop-off schedule.
- Self-arranged shipping must still meet Shopee's tracking and delivery confirmation
  requirements.

## Chat Response Requirements

### Response Rate

- Shopee tracks the percentage of new chats responded to within 12 hours.
- Target response rate: 80% or higher (varies by market; some require 90%+).
- Response rate is calculated over a rolling period (typically 7 days or 28 days
  depending on market).
- Below-target response rates reduce search visibility and disqualify sellers from
  Preferred Seller status.

### Response Time

- Average response time is tracked and displayed on your shop profile.
- Target: respond within 1 hour during business hours.
- Automated responses (using Shopee's auto-reply feature) count toward response rate
  but do not fully substitute for human responses.
- After-hours messages should be responded to at the start of the next business day.

### Chat Content Rules

- Do not share external links (competing platforms, personal websites, social media).
- Do not provide contact information for off-platform transactions (phone numbers,
  email addresses, social media handles).
- Do not use abusive, threatening, or discriminatory language.
- Do not send spam messages or unsolicited promotions.
- Do not attempt to redirect buyers to other platforms or payment methods.
- Product recommendations within Shopee (linking to your other listings) are allowed.

## Shopee Preferred Seller Requirements

Shopee Preferred Seller (sometimes called Star Seller or Preferred Shop depending on the
market) is a status tier that provides enhanced visibility and buyer trust signals.

### Qualification Criteria

Requirements vary by market but typically include:

| Metric | Typical Requirement |
|--------|-------------------|
| Chat response rate | Above 80-90% |
| Late shipment rate | Below 5-10% |
| Non-fulfillment rate | Below 2-5% |
| Minimum orders | 30-100 orders in the qualifying period |
| Minimum days active | 60-90 days |
| Penalty points | Below threshold (typically under 3) |
| Shop rating | 4.0 stars or higher |
| Account verification | Completed |

### Benefits

- Preferred Seller badge displayed on listings and shop profile.
- Higher search ranking for listings.
- Access to exclusive promotional campaigns and vouchers.
- Priority customer support in some markets.
- Eligibility for Shopee's advertising subsidies.

### Maintaining Status

- Status is re-evaluated periodically (monthly or quarterly depending on market).
- Failing to maintain any qualification criteria results in status removal.
- Regaining status requires meeting all criteria again in the next evaluation period.
- Serious violations (penalty points above threshold) cause immediate status removal.

## Shopee Mall Requirements

Shopee Mall is the premium tier for official brand stores and authorized distributors,
offering enhanced trust and visibility.

### Entry Requirements

- Must be a registered brand owner or authorized distributor with documentation.
- Brand authorization letter or trademark registration certificate required.
- Minimum sales history and performance record on Shopee (varies by market).
- Business registration documents and tax compliance verification.
- Some markets require invitation from Shopee's business development team.

### Operational Requirements

- **Shipping**: Faster shipping SLAs (typically 1-2 day shipping requirement).
- **Returns**: Must offer 15-day returns (standard is 7 days in most markets).
- **Authenticity guarantee**: All products must be 100% authentic; violations result
  in immediate ejection.
- **Customer service**: Higher response rate and response time requirements.
- **Content quality**: Listings must meet enhanced content standards (professional
  images, complete descriptions, accurate attributes).

### Benefits

- Shopee Mall badge and prominent placement in Shopee Mall section.
- Highest search ranking priority.
- Inclusion in Shopee Mall-exclusive campaigns and promotions.
- Enhanced return policy builds buyer confidence.
- Access to dedicated account manager in some markets.

### Maintaining Mall Status

- Regular performance reviews (monthly or quarterly).
- Any authenticity complaint triggers investigation.
- Multiple complaints or policy violations result in Mall status removal.
- Removal from Mall does not necessarily mean account suspension, but the seller
  reverts to standard marketplace status.

## Common Violations and How to Avoid Them

### 1. Listing Counterfeit or Infringing Products

**How it happens**: Sourcing from unreliable suppliers, using brand names without
authorization, copying product images from other sellers or brands.

**How to avoid**:
- Verify supplier legitimacy and request brand authorization documents.
- Use only your own product photos or licensed stock images.
- Remove trademarked terms from listings unless you are an authorized seller.
- When selling compatible/aftermarket products, clearly state "compatible with [brand]"
  and do not use the brand's logo.

### 2. Late Shipments and Non-Fulfillment

**How it happens**: Overselling inventory, underestimating processing time, logistics
partner delays, not syncing inventory across platforms.

**How to avoid**:
- Maintain safety stock buffers.
- Set conservative Days to Ship values.
- Use inventory management tools that sync across all platforms.
- Activate vacation mode during periods when you cannot fulfill orders.
- Communicate proactively with buyers if delays are unavoidable.

### 3. Review Manipulation

**How it happens**: Offering discounts or gifts for positive reviews, using review
exchange groups, purchasing fake reviews.

**How to avoid**:
- Only use Shopee's built-in review features.
- Focus on product quality and customer service to earn genuine reviews.
- Do not offer any incentive tied to leaving a review.
- Report competitors engaged in review manipulation rather than retaliating.

### 4. Chat Policy Violations

**How it happens**: Sharing personal contact information, directing buyers to other
platforms, responding with inappropriate language under pressure.

**How to avoid**:
- Train all team members on Shopee's chat policies.
- Use templated responses for common scenarios.
- Never share phone numbers, email addresses, or social media handles in chat.
- De-escalate difficult conversations; if a buyer becomes abusive, report rather than
  engage.

### 5. Pricing Manipulation

**How it happens**: Inflating the "original price" to make discounts appear larger,
bait-and-switch pricing, price gouging during high-demand events.

**How to avoid**:
- Set honest reference prices based on actual historical selling prices.
- Do not change the original price immediately before creating a discount.
- Price consistently across channels within reasonable bounds.

### 6. Category Abuse

**How it happens**: Listing products in incorrect but less competitive categories to gain
visibility, using trending category tags on unrelated products.

**How to avoid**:
- List products in the most specific accurate category.
- If unsure about the correct category, check where top competitors in your product type
  are listed.
- Report competitors engaged in category abuse.

## Appeal Process

### When to Appeal

- You receive penalty points for a violation you believe was applied in error.
- Your listing is removed and you have evidence of compliance.
- Your account is restricted or suspended and you have corrective measures to present.

### How to Appeal

1. **Go to Seller Centre** > **My Account** > **Account Health** (or **Penalty Points**
   section, depending on market).
2. **Identify the specific violation** you want to appeal.
3. **Click the appeal button** associated with that violation.
4. **Prepare your appeal** with the following:
   - Clear explanation of why the violation was incorrectly applied, or
   - Acknowledgment of the violation with root cause analysis and corrective actions
     (follow the Appeal Letter Framework in SKILL.md).
   - Supporting documentation (invoices, authorization letters, screenshots, test reports).
5. **Submit the appeal** through the platform's appeal form.
6. **Wait for the response** (typically 3-7 business days; can be longer during
   peak periods).

### Appeal Tips

- Appeal promptly; some markets have appeal windows (e.g., 7 days from violation notice).
- Provide concrete evidence, not just explanations.
- Be professional and factual; do not use emotional language.
- If the first appeal is rejected, review the rejection reason carefully before submitting
  a revised appeal.
- Do not submit identical appeals multiple times.
- For account-level suspensions, consider reaching out to your Shopee account manager
  (if you have one) in parallel with the formal appeal.

## Market-Specific Differences

Shopee operates across multiple Southeast Asian markets, and policies differ in meaningful
ways.

### Singapore (SG)

- **Regulatory focus**: Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act; strict labeling for food,
  cosmetics, and health products.
- **Shipping SLA**: Standard 2 days.
- **Language**: English primary.
- **Preferred Seller threshold**: Relatively high performance requirements due to smaller
  market and higher buyer expectations.
- **Payment**: SGD settlement; GST registration may be required for sellers exceeding
  revenue thresholds.

### Malaysia (MY)

- **Regulatory focus**: Product labeling in Bahasa Malaysia required for many categories;
  Halal certification important for food and cosmetics.
- **Shipping SLA**: Standard 2-3 days.
- **Language**: Bahasa Malaysia and English.
- **Market size**: Largest Shopee market by GMV; high competition.
- **Cross-border**: Active cross-border program for Chinese sellers; specific policies
  for cross-border fulfillment.

### Thailand (TH)

- **Regulatory focus**: FDA Thailand registration required for food, cosmetics, and
  supplements; strict import regulations.
- **Shipping SLA**: Standard 2-3 days (can be longer for remote areas).
- **Language**: Thai primary; English proficiency in chat is limited.
- **Market behavior**: High emphasis on livestream selling; flash sale culture.
- **Payment**: THB settlement with specific bank requirements.

### Philippines (PH)

- **Regulatory focus**: FDA Philippines for food and health products; Bureau of Customs
  requirements for imports.
- **Shipping SLA**: Standard 3-5 days (logistics infrastructure challenges).
- **Language**: Filipino and English.
- **Market behavior**: Price-sensitive market; COD (Cash on Delivery) widely used,
  leading to higher return rates.
- **Logistics**: Delivery to provincial areas may have extended timeframes; plan inventory
  positioning accordingly.

### Indonesia (ID)

- **Regulatory focus**: SNI (Indonesian National Standard) certification for many product
  categories; BPOM for food and cosmetics; Halal certification increasingly required.
- **Shipping SLA**: Standard 2-3 days (Java); 3-7 days (outer islands).
- **Language**: Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Market size**: Largest population in SEA; massive market with intense competition.
- **Regulatory complexity**: Importing regulations are the most complex in the region;
  product registration requirements are extensive.
- **Payment**: IDR settlement; specific tax requirements for cross-border sellers.

### Vietnam (VN)

- **Regulatory focus**: Product quality declarations; Vietnamese labeling requirements
  for imported products; Ministry of Health approval for health-related products.
- **Shipping SLA**: Standard 2-3 days (major cities); up to 7 days for remote areas.
- **Language**: Vietnamese.
- **Market behavior**: Rapidly growing market; heavy social commerce integration.
- **Cross-border**: Growing cross-border program; customs clearance can be unpredictable.
- **Payment**: VND settlement; foreign seller registration requirements.

### Cross-Market Compliance Tips

- Never assume a policy in one market applies identically to another.
- Product restrictions vary significantly between markets (e.g., a product legal in
  Singapore may be restricted in Indonesia).
- Labeling and language requirements are market-specific and legally enforceable.
- Shipping SLA expectations differ; set DTS values appropriate to each market.
- Cultural sensitivity matters; product descriptions and images should be adapted for
  local norms.
- Maintain separate compliance checklists for each market you sell in.
- When in doubt about a market-specific policy, check the local Shopee Seller Education
  Hub or contact your local Shopee account manager.
