## Seller Code of Conduct

### Core Principles

TikTok Shop expects sellers to uphold the following standards:

- **Authenticity**: All products must be genuine and accurately represented. Counterfeit
  or misleading products are strictly prohibited.
- **Safety**: Products must meet applicable safety standards and regulations in each
  market. No unsafe, recalled, or hazardous products.
- **Honesty**: All claims, descriptions, pricing, and promotions must be truthful and
  verifiable. No misleading or deceptive practices.
- **Respect**: Professional and respectful interactions with buyers, creators, and
  TikTok Shop support staff.
- **Legal compliance**: All products and business practices must comply with applicable
  laws and regulations.

### Account Requirements

- One seller account per business entity (unless multiple accounts are approved by
  TikTok Shop).
- Account must be registered with valid business documentation.
- Identity verification must be completed and kept current.
- Seller must be authorized to conduct business in the target market.
- Account sharing or transfer is prohibited without platform approval.

### Prohibited Seller Behaviors

- Operating multiple accounts without approval.
- Manipulating sales data, traffic, or any platform metric.
- Using bots, scripts, or automated tools to interact with the platform in unauthorized
  ways.
- Engaging in off-platform transactions to circumvent TikTok Shop fees.
- Misrepresenting business information, location, or capabilities.

## Product Listing Policies

### Restricted and Prohibited Categories

TikTok Shop maintains a comprehensive prohibited and restricted products list. Key
categories include:

**Prohibited (cannot be listed under any circumstances)**:
- Illegal substances, drugs, and drug paraphernalia.
- Weapons, ammunition, and explosives.
- Counterfeit and trademark-infringing products.
- Products promoting hate, violence, or discrimination.
- Live animals.
- Human body parts or fluids.
- Recalled products.
- Products facilitating illegal activity.
- Tobacco products, e-cigarettes, and vaping supplies (in most markets).

**Restricted (require approval or compliance documentation)**:
- Health and wellness products (supplements, vitamins).
- Beauty and skincare products (ingredient compliance required).
- Electronics with wireless capabilities (regulatory certification required).
- Children's products (safety testing required).
- Food and beverages (health and safety certifications required).
- Products making health or medical claims.

### Content Guidelines for Listings

**Title**:
- Must accurately describe the product.
- No misleading keywords, irrelevant hashtags, or keyword spam.
- No promotional claims ("BEST," "CHEAPEST," "#1") unless verifiable.
- Keep within platform character limits.
- Must be in the primary language of the target market.

**Images and Video**:
- Main image must clearly show the product.
- No misleading photo editing that misrepresents the product's appearance, size, or color.
- No watermarks, promotional overlays, or third-party branding on product images.
- Minimum image resolution: 600 x 600 pixels; recommended: 1000 x 1000 or higher.
- Product video is highly recommended (and required for some categories).
- Video content must comply with TikTok's community guidelines.

**Description**:
- Must accurately describe the product's features, specifications, materials, and
  dimensions.
- No false or unsubstantiated claims (especially health, safety, or performance claims).
- No external links or contact information.
- No competitor disparagement.
- Must include all legally required disclosures (ingredients, warnings, certifications).

**Pricing**:
- Listed price must be the genuine selling price.
- No inflated "original price" to create fake discounts.
- Prices must include mandatory taxes and fees where required by local law.
- Flash sale and promotional pricing must reflect genuine price reductions.

### Category-Specific Requirements

Many product categories have additional requirements:

| Category | Additional Requirements |
|----------|----------------------|
| Beauty & Personal Care | Ingredient list, compliance certificates, no unsubstantiated claims |
| Health & Wellness | Regulatory approval, no medical claims, proper labeling |
| Electronics | Regulatory certification (FCC, CE, etc.), warranty information |
| Children's Products | Safety testing reports, age-appropriate labeling, compliance certificates |
| Food & Beverages | Health certificates, ingredient lists, allergen warnings, shelf life |
| Clothing & Accessories | Material composition, care instructions, accurate sizing |

## Violation Points System

TikTok Shop uses a violation points system to track and enforce policy compliance.

### How Points Are Assigned

Points are assigned based on the type and severity of the violation:

| Violation Tier | Points | Examples |
|---------------|--------|---------|
| **Tier 1 (Minor)** | 1-2 | Incomplete listing information, minor policy non-compliance, late response to buyer inquiry |
| **Tier 2 (Moderate)** | 3-6 | Misleading product claims, late shipment, inaccurate product description, listing in wrong category |
| **Tier 3 (Serious)** | 6-12 | Counterfeit products, intellectual property infringement, selling restricted products without approval, review manipulation |
| **Tier 4 (Severe)** | 12-48 | Selling prohibited products, safety violations, fraud, repeated serious violations |

### Consequences by Point Threshold

| Point Threshold | Consequence |
|----------------|-------------|
| 6 points | Warning; may affect search visibility |
| 12 points | Temporary restriction on listing new products (1-7 days) |
| 24 points | Account suspension (7-14 days); existing listings deactivated |
| 36 points | Extended account suspension (14-28 days) |
| 48 points | Permanent account termination |

### Point Expiration

- Points accumulate over a rolling period (typically 180 days).
- After the rolling period, expired points are removed from the total.
- Serious violations (Tier 3-4) may result in points with longer or no expiration.
- Successfully appealing a violation removes the associated points immediately.

### Expedited Enforcement

Some violations bypass the points system and trigger immediate action:

- Selling counterfeit products: immediate listing removal and potential account ban.
- Selling prohibited products (illegal items): immediate account termination.
- Safety incidents (product causes injury): immediate listing removal and investigation.
- Fraud or deceptive practices: immediate account suspension pending investigation.

## Content and Livestream Policies

TikTok Shop is unique among e-commerce platforms in its deep integration with content
creation. Content policies are a critical compliance area.

### Product Videos

- Must accurately represent the product (size, color, functionality, performance).
- No misleading demonstrations or before/after claims without substantiation.
- No use of copyrighted music, images, or video clips without rights.
- Must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines.
- No hate speech, violence, harassment, or dangerous behavior.
- No misleading health or safety claims.
- Product claims made in video must be substantiated.

### Livestream Selling Rules

Livestream commerce is a major sales channel on TikTok Shop and subject to specific rules:

**Content requirements**:
- Products demonstrated must match the listed products exactly.
- Claims made during livestreams are subject to the same verification requirements as
  written listings.
- Presenters must not make exaggerated, false, or unsubstantiated claims.
- Pricing announced during livestreams must match the actual listed price.
- All promotions, discounts, and giveaways must be genuine and honored.

**Prohibited livestream behaviors**:
- Selling products not listed in TikTok Shop (off-platform transaction solicitation).
- Displaying competitor products for disparagement.
- Using pre-recorded footage presented as live content.
- Engaging in countdown pressure tactics with misleading stock claims.
- Showing explicit, violent, or offensive content.
- Directing viewers to external platforms for purchases.
- Making health or medical claims without proper certification.

**Technical requirements**:
- Livestream must be of adequate audio and video quality.
- Product must be clearly visible and demonstrable.
- Pricing must be displayed clearly when discussing product features.
- Livestream recordings are retained by the platform for compliance review.

### Short Video Content for Products

- Product tagging in short videos must match the actual product in the video.
- "Clickbait" content that drives clicks but does not accurately represent the product
  leads to violations.
- Video reviews and testimonials must be genuine; paid testimonials must be disclosed.
- Copyright compliance applies to all audio, visual, and text elements.

## Affiliate and Creator Policies

TikTok Shop's creator/affiliate program allows influencers to promote and sell products
on behalf of sellers. Both sellers and creators have compliance obligations.

### Seller Responsibilities in Affiliate Programs

- **Product accuracy**: Products provided to creators must match listings exactly.
- **Commission honesty**: Commission rates and terms must be honored as agreed.
- **Sample management**: Free samples provided for review must be tracked; reviews must
  be genuine and unscripted.
- **Claims responsibility**: Sellers are responsible for claims creators make about their
  products. If a creator makes a false claim, the seller may also face enforcement.
- **Creator selection**: Sellers should verify that creators they work with comply with
  TikTok's guidelines before entering partnerships.

### Prohibited Affiliate Practices

- Offering additional compensation (beyond agreed commission) for positive reviews.
- Scripting creator content to include specific claims or exaggerations.
- Requiring creators to make guarantees about product performance.
- Using fake creator accounts to promote products.
- Manipulating affiliate metrics (fake clicks, orders, or referrals).
- Using creators to circumvent listing content restrictions (having creators make claims
  that would be prohibited in a listing).

### Creator Content Compliance

Sellers should monitor content that creators produce about their products:

- If a creator makes false or dangerous claims, the seller should request correction
  immediately and report to TikTok Shop if the creator does not comply.
- Sellers should provide creators with accurate product information sheets to reduce
  the risk of inaccurate claims.
- FTC/ASA disclosure requirements apply: sponsored content must be clearly marked as
  such (market-specific disclosure rules vary).

## Shipping and Fulfillment Requirements

### Shipping Timeframes

| Market | Standard Shipping SLA | Extended (Pre-order) |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------|
| US | 3-5 business days (domestic); 7-15 days (cross-border) | Up to 15-30 days with pre-order marking |
| UK | 2-5 business days (domestic); 7-15 days (cross-border) | Up to 15-30 days with pre-order marking |
| SEA markets | 2-7 business days depending on market | Varies by market |

### Order Processing Requirements

- Orders must be processed (shipped with tracking) within the platform's required
  timeframe (typically 2-3 business days from order confirmation).
- Tracking information must be valid and from a supported carrier.
- Tracking must show movement (carrier scans) within the expected timeframe.
- Sellers must update order status promptly as shipment progresses.

### Fulfillment Performance Metrics

| Metric | Target | Penalty Threshold |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| Ship-on-time rate | > 95% | Below 90% triggers warnings |
| Valid tracking rate | > 95% | Below 90% triggers penalties |
| Cancellation rate (seller-initiated) | < 2% | Above 5% triggers penalties |
| Return/refund rate | Varies by category | Significant outliers trigger review |

### Fulfilled by TikTok Shop (FBT)

TikTok Shop offers fulfillment services in select markets:

- Sellers ship inventory to TikTok's fulfillment centers.
- TikTok handles storage, packing, shipping, and returns.
- FBT listings may receive search ranking benefits.
- Sellers must comply with FBT inventory requirements (labeling, packaging, quantity
  limits).
- FBT does not eliminate the seller's responsibility for product quality, compliance,
  and accuracy.

## Intellectual Property Policy

### TikTok Shop's IP Framework

TikTok Shop takes intellectual property rights seriously and provides tools for both
rights holders and sellers.

**For sellers**:
- Do not use trademarks, copyrighted material, or patented designs without authorization.
- "Compatible with [brand]" language must be used carefully and accurately; it does not
  authorize use of the brand's logo or imagery.
- Product images must be original or properly licensed.
- Listing descriptions must not copy text from other listings or copyrighted sources.
- Music used in product videos must be properly licensed or from TikTok's royalty-free
  library.

**Responding to IP complaints**:
- TikTok Shop will notify sellers when an IP complaint is filed.
- Sellers typically have 3-5 business days to respond.
- Valid responses include: authorization documentation, proof of legitimacy, or a
  counter-notice if the claim is invalid.
- Failure to respond within the deadline results in listing removal and penalty points.
- Repeated IP violations escalate to account-level action.

### Brand Protection for Sellers

- Register your brand through TikTok Shop's Brand Protection program.
- Use the IP Infringement Reporting tool to report unauthorized use of your brand
  by other sellers.
- Document your trademarks, copyrights, and design patents for quick reference when
  filing or defending claims.
- Monitor marketplace listings regularly for unauthorized use of your brand assets.

## Account Suspension and Appeal Process

### Types of Account Actions

| Action | Severity | Duration | Trigger |
|--------|----------|----------|---------|
| Warning | Low | N/A | First minor violation |
| Listing removal | Medium | Until fixed | Product policy violation |
| Listing restriction | Medium | 1-7 days | Moderate violations; cannot create new listings |
| Account suspension | High | 7-28 days | Serious violations or accumulated points |
| Permanent ban | Severe | Permanent | Prohibited products, fraud, safety incidents, or 48+ points |

### Appeal Process

1. **Access the appeal form** through Seller Center > Account Health > Violations.
2. **Identify the specific violation** and understand the reason for the action.
3. **Prepare your appeal** following the framework in SKILL.md:
   - Acknowledge the violation.
   - Explain the root cause.
   - Detail corrective actions already taken.
   - Describe preventive measures implemented.
4. **Attach supporting evidence**:
   - Product authenticity documentation (invoices, certificates).
   - Compliance test reports from accredited labs.
   - Photos or videos demonstrating compliance.
   - Brand authorization letters.
   - Screenshots of corrective actions taken.
5. **Submit the appeal** through the official appeal channel.
6. **Response timeline**: typically 3-10 business days. Expedited review may be available
   for severe cases.

### Appeal Success Factors

- **Specificity**: Generic appeals are rejected. Reference exact violation IDs, dates,
  products, and policy sections.
- **Evidence quality**: Clear, verifiable documentation significantly increases success
  rates.
- **Timeliness**: Appeal promptly; some violation types have appeal windows.
- **Systemic changes**: Demonstrate that you have changed processes, not just fixed the
  immediate issue.
- **Professional tone**: Factual, concise, and professional. No emotional appeals,
  threats, or blame.

### What to Do During Suspension

- Do not create new accounts to circumvent the suspension (this leads to permanent bans
  for all linked accounts).
- Use the suspension period to audit all listings for compliance.
- Prepare detailed documentation for your appeal.
- Review all policies to identify and fix any other potential issues.
- Do not contact TikTok Shop support repeatedly about the same case; allow the appeal
  process to proceed.

## Market-Specific Requirements

### United States (US)

**Regulatory environment**:
- FTC regulations govern advertising claims, disclosures, and endorsements.
- CPSC requirements for consumer product safety (especially children's products).
- FDA requirements for food, supplements, cosmetics, and medical devices.
- FCC certification for electronic devices.
- California Prop 65 warnings for products containing listed chemicals.
- State-specific requirements (varying sales tax, labeling, and licensing).

**TikTok Shop US specifics**:
- Seller must have a US business entity or authorized representative.
- Products shipped to the US must comply with all federal, state, and local regulations.
- Customs and import compliance for cross-border sellers.
- FTC endorsement guidelines apply to all creator/affiliate content.
- Higher scrutiny on health, wellness, and supplement claims.
- Returns policy must comply with the platform's US-specific return requirements.

### United Kingdom (UK)

**Regulatory environment**:
- UK GDPR and data protection requirements.
- Trading Standards regulations for consumer goods.
- UKCA marking for applicable product categories (replacing CE marking post-Brexit).
- ASA (Advertising Standards Authority) rules for marketing claims.
- UK-specific labeling requirements (English language, UK importer information).

**TikTok Shop UK specifics**:
- Seller must have a UK or EU business entity or UK fiscal representative.
- Products must carry UKCA marking where applicable.
- UK importer name and address must be on product labels.
- VAT registration and compliance required for applicable sellers.
- ASA disclosure rules apply to sponsored creator content.
- Stricter enforcement on environmental and sustainability claims.

### Southeast Asia (SEA)

TikTok Shop operates in multiple SEA markets with varying requirements:

**Indonesia**:
- SNI certification for regulated product categories.
- BPOM approval for food, cosmetics, and health products.
- Halal certification increasingly required.
- Bahasa Indonesia labeling required for many product categories.
- Among the strictest import regulations in the region.
- Recent regulatory changes may affect social commerce specifically; monitor closely.

**Thailand**:
- FDA Thailand registration for food, cosmetics, and supplements.
- TISI standards for applicable products.
- Thai language labeling required.
- Strong consumer protection laws with active enforcement.

**Vietnam**:
- Product quality declarations required.
- Vietnamese labeling for imported products.
- Ministry of Health approval for health-related products.
- Growing but rapidly evolving regulatory framework for e-commerce.

**Malaysia**:
- SIRIM certification for applicable products.
- Halal certification important for food and cosmetics.
- Bahasa Malaysia labeling requirements.
- MDTCA (Ministry of Domestic Trade and Consumer Affairs) oversight.

**Philippines**:
- FDA Philippines for food and health products.
- DTI (Department of Trade and Industry) consumer protection.
- Bureau of Customs import requirements.
- Filipino/English bilingual labeling for many categories.

**Singapore**:
- Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act.
- HSA (Health Sciences Authority) for health products.
- Strict advertising standards enforcement.
- English as primary language; no translation requirements.

## Emerging Policy Areas

TikTok Shop's policy landscape evolves rapidly. These areas are subject to significant
and frequent changes.

### AI-Generated Content

- Policies around AI-generated product images are tightening. Listings using AI-generated
  images that misrepresent the actual product face removal.
- AI-generated reviews and testimonials are prohibited.
- AI-generated video content for product promotion is an evolving area; current guidance
  suggests disclosure requirements are being developed.
- Deepfakes and AI-generated likenesses of real people in product promotion are
  prohibited.
- AI tools used for listing optimization (titles, descriptions) are currently permitted
  but the output must be accurate and compliant.

### Sustainability Claims

- "Eco-friendly," "sustainable," "green," and similar environmental claims must be
  substantiated with evidence.
- Greenwashing enforcement is increasing across all markets.
- Specific certifications (organic, fair trade, recycled content) require documentation.
- Vague or broad environmental claims without specific evidence are being targeted by
  regulators and platforms alike.
- Carbon neutrality and offset claims face particular scrutiny.

### Data Privacy and Consumer Protection

- Seller handling of buyer personal data must comply with local privacy regulations
  (GDPR, CCPA, PDPA, etc.).
- New requirements around transparent data collection and use are emerging.
- Cookie consent and tracking disclosure requirements apply to sellers with standalone
  web presences linked from TikTok Shop.
- Cross-border data transfer restrictions may affect how seller tools and analytics
  operate.

### Social Commerce Regulation

- Several markets are introducing or updating regulations specifically targeting social
  commerce platforms.
- Indonesia has introduced regulations affecting social commerce and marketplace
  integration; monitor for compliance updates.
- The US and EU are evaluating regulatory frameworks for social commerce that may affect
  TikTok Shop operations.
- Disclosure and transparency requirements for the blend of content and commerce are
  tightening globally.

### Live Commerce Evolution

- Livestream selling regulations are becoming more specific in multiple markets.
- Requirements around real-time claim verification during livestreams are emerging.
- Recording and retention requirements for livestream content are being standardized.
- Creator liability for claims made during livestreams is being clarified in multiple
  jurisdictions.
- Return policies specific to livestream purchases may differ from standard e-commerce
  returns.

### Platform Algorithm and Policy Coupling

TikTok Shop's algorithm increasingly incorporates compliance signals:

- Listings with compliance issues receive reduced visibility before formal enforcement.
- Seller compliance history affects search ranking and recommendation eligibility.
- Return rates, buyer complaints, and content quality scores feed into algorithmic
  visibility decisions.
- Maintaining strong compliance is becoming a competitive advantage in algorithm-driven
  discovery, not just a risk avoidance measure.

Always verify policy information directly in TikTok Shop Seller Center, as this reference
document may not reflect the most recent changes. TikTok Shop's policies evolve faster
than most traditional e-commerce platforms; quarterly policy reviews are strongly
recommended.
