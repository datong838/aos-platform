# Platform Fee Schedules Reference

Complete fee structure reference for major cross-border e-commerce platforms. Use this
reference when calculating landed cost and setting prices.

**Disclaimer:** Platform fees change frequently. Always verify current rates on the
platform's official seller dashboard or fee schedule page before finalizing pricing
decisions. The figures in this document are representative ranges for planning purposes
and were compiled as of early 2026.

### Amazon EU Specifics

- Referral fees: generally similar percentages to US with some category variations
- FBA fulfillment fees: vary by fulfillment center country
- Pan-European FBA: enables inventory distribution across EU warehouses; additional
  per-unit placement fees may apply
- VAT: seller must be VAT-registered in countries where inventory is stored
- EFN (European Fulfillment Network): cross-border fulfillment fee EUR 1.00-3.00
  additional per unit versus local fulfillment

### Amazon JP Specifics

- Referral fees: 8-15% (similar structure to US)
- FBA fulfillment fees: JPY 282-603 for standard size
- Storage fees: JPY 5,160 per CBM (Jan-Sep), JPY 9,170 per CBM (Oct-Dec)
- Consumption tax: 10% (similar to VAT; included in displayed price)

### Brand Registry Benefits (Cost Impact)
- A+ Content: free with Brand Registry; improves conversion 3-10%
- Vine program: $200 per parent ASIN for up to 30 units
- Brand Analytics: free data on search terms, demographics, repeat purchase
- Net impact: improved conversion rate means lower effective TACOS

### Transaction Fee
- 2.0% on all transactions (in addition to commission)
- Applied to the total order amount including shipping paid by buyer

### Shipping Structure
- Shopee provides shipping subsidies and integrated logistics
- Seller shipping fee varies by market and logistics partner
- Free shipping vouchers: Shopee often subsidizes; seller co-funds in some campaigns
- Cross-border logistics (from China): Shopee Logistics Service (SLS) or third-party;
  costs $2-5 per package depending on weight and destination

| Ad Type | Cost Model | Typical CPM/CPC |
|---------|-----------|----------------:|
| In-Feed Ads | CPC / CPM | CPM $5-15; CPC $0.10-0.50 |

### Affiliate Commission
- Sellers can set affiliate commission for creators: typically 5-20%
- This is an additional cost on top of platform commission
- Higher affiliate rates attract more creator content but reduce margin
- Recommended: 10-15% for product launch, 5-10% for established products

### Payment
- Settlement cycle: typically 7-15 days after delivery confirmation
- Payment processing: included in commission rate
- Currency conversion: 1-2% if cross-border settlement

## Mercado Libre

### Commission Structure

Mercado Libre uses a tiered commission system based on product category, listing type,
and seller reputation.

| Listing Type | Commission Range | Notes |
|-------------|------------------:|-------|
| Classic (free listing) | 11-16% | Lower visibility; no interest-free installments |
| Premium | 16-21.5% | Higher visibility; interest-free installments included |

Category-specific ranges within each tier:
| Category | Classic | Premium |
|----------|--------:|--------:|
| Electronics | 11-13% | 16-18% |
| Fashion | 13-16% | 18-21.5% |
| Home & Garden | 12-14% | 17-19% |
| Auto Parts | 11-13% | 16-18% |
| Health & Beauty | 13-16% | 18-21% |

### Mercado Envios (Shipping)
- Integrated shipping with negotiated carrier rates
- Free shipping threshold varies by market (e.g., BRL 79+ in Brazil)
- Seller pays shipping fee, offset by buyer payment in some cases
- Cross-border (from China): Mercado Libre has direct import programs in some markets;
  shipping $4-12 per package

### Mercado Pago (Payment)
- Included in commission rate for marketplace sales
- Additional processing fee for off-platform use: 3.49% + local currency fixed fee
- Installment plans: commission tier (Premium) includes cost of interest-free installments

### Total Fee Burden Estimate (All-In Platform Cost as % of Selling Price)

|----------|------------:|-------------:|-------|
| Amazon US (FBA) | 30% | 50% | Highly category-dependent; FBA adds significant cost |
| Amazon EU (FBA) | 32% | 52% | Add VAT complexity |
| Shopee | 15% | 30% | Lower platform fees; shipping costs vary |
| TikTok Shop | 15% | 35% | Affiliate commissions can add 5-20% |
| Lazada (FBL) | 18% | 32% | Competitive fulfillment fees |
| Mercado Libre | 20% | 35% | High commission offset by installment inclusion |

### Key Takeaways for Pricing Decisions

1. **Amazon** has the highest total fee burden but also the highest traffic and
   conversion rates. Price must account for 30-50% platform costs.

2. **Shopee** has the lowest commission rates but requires separate shipping cost
   calculation. Best margins on lightweight items with low return rates.

3. **TikTok Shop** commission is moderate, but affiliate commissions can double the
   effective platform cost. Factor in creator payouts when pricing.

4. **Lazada FBL** offers competitive fulfillment fees for Southeast Asian markets.
   Good for sellers wanting Amazon-like fulfillment without Amazon-level fees.

5. **Mercado Libre** Premium listing commission is high (16-21.5%) but includes
   interest-free installment costs. Essential for Latin American markets where
   installment purchasing dominates.

6. **Walmart** fee structure mirrors Amazon but with lower storage fees and no
   long-term storage surcharge (as aggressive). Requires US entity.

Always calculate total platform cost per unit (not just commission) when comparing
platforms. A lower commission rate can be misleading if fulfillment, storage, or
advertising costs are higher.
