## Product Card Optimization

The product card is the listing page that buyers see when they tap on a product link
from a video, livestream, or the Shop tab. It must convert quickly because TikTok
users have short attention spans.

### Title

- **Maximum length**: 255 characters (but keep effective title under 80 characters).
- **Front-load the primary keyword**: The first 34 characters are visible in the
  product card preview without tapping.
- **Include key specs**: Size, quantity, color, material.
- **Use natural language**: TikTok search is more conversational than Amazon. Phrases
  like "for small spaces" or "travel-friendly" work well.
- **Avoid spam signals**: No ALL CAPS, no excessive symbols, no promotional language
  like "VIRAL" or "#1 SELLER".

**Title template**:
```
[Product Type] [Key Feature] [For Use Case/Audience] - [Spec/Size] [Material/Style]
```

**Examples**:
```
Portable Blender Cup USB Rechargeable for Smoothies and Shakes - 20oz BPA Free
LED Desk Lamp with Wireless Charger for Home Office - 3 Color Modes Dimmable
Oversized Graphic Tee Vintage Washed Cotton for Streetwear - Unisex Relaxed Fit
```

### Description

- Keep descriptions concise: 200-500 characters is optimal.
- Structure with short paragraphs or bullet-style lines.
- Include keywords naturally (the description is searchable).
- Address the top 2-3 buyer questions or concerns.
- Include a size/spec note if relevant.
- End with a prompt to watch the product video for a demo.

### Images

| Specification | Requirement |
|--------------|-------------|
| Aspect ratio | 1:1 (square) |
| Minimum resolution | 600 x 600 px |
| Recommended resolution | 1200 x 1200 px |
| Maximum images | 9 |
| Format | JPEG, PNG |
| Main image | Clean product shot; white or solid background recommended |

**Image strategy for TikTok Shop**:

| Slot | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Main product shot | Clean, clear product image for the search grid |
| 2 | In-use / lifestyle | Show the product being used (mirrors TikTok video content) |
| 3 | Key feature close-up | Highlight the standout feature |
| 4 | Size/scale reference | Dimensions or comparison to common object |
| 5 | Package contents | What arrives in the box |
| 6-9 | Additional angles, infographics, or user-generated content | Fill remaining slots |

**Important**: On TikTok Shop, images matter less than video. The product card images
are a fallback. Most buyers arrive via video content, so video quality is the primary
conversion lever.

### Pricing Display

- TikTok Shop shows the lowest variation price prominently.
- Strikethrough pricing (original vs sale price) is a strong conversion signal.
- Free shipping badge increases CTR significantly.
- Consider an introductory low price to build initial reviews and sales velocity.

## Video-First Listing Strategy

TikTok Shop is fundamentally different from traditional e-commerce because product
discovery happens through video content, not search browse. Video is not optional; it
is the primary listing element.

### Product Showcase Video Types

| Video Type | Duration | Purpose | Best For |
|-----------|----------|---------|----------|
| Quick demo | 15-30s | Show the product in action | Gadgets, tools, beauty |
| Unboxing | 30-60s | Build anticipation, show packaging | All categories |
| Before/after | 15-30s | Show transformation | Beauty, cleaning, home improvement |
| How-to / tutorial | 60-180s | Teach usage while showcasing product | Beauty, tech, cooking |
| Comparison | 30-60s | Position against alternatives | Electronics, tools |
| Review-style | 60-120s | Authentic user perspective | All categories |

### Video Production Guidelines

- **Hook in first 3 seconds**: Grab attention immediately. Start with the result,
  the problem, or a surprising claim.
- **Vertical format**: 9:16 aspect ratio (1080 x 1920 px). This is non-negotiable
  on TikTok.
- **Natural lighting**: Well-lit shots outperform dark or overly produced content.
- **Show the product in use**: Demonstrations convert better than static product shots.
- **Include text overlays**: Many viewers watch without sound. Use on-screen text to
  convey key points.
- **Call to action**: "Link in bio", "Tap the cart", or "Check the link" to direct
  viewers to the product card.
- **Trending audio**: Using trending sounds increases the probability of algorithmic
  distribution. Check TikTok's Creative Center for trending audio.
- **Authentic feel**: Overly polished, commercial-style videos underperform on TikTok.
  Content should feel native to the platform.

### Video-to-Product Card Flow

```
Video appears in For You feed or Search results
  -> Viewer watches (algorithm tracks watch time, replays, shares)
  -> Product tag or cart icon visible on video
  -> Viewer taps product tag
  -> Product card opens (images, title, price, description, reviews)
  -> Add to cart / Buy now
```

Optimize every step: the video must hook, the product tag must be visible, and the
product card must convert.

### Seller-Created vs Creator-Made Videos

- **Seller videos**: You control the message and can produce at scale. Post 3-5
  product videos per week minimum.
- **Creator videos**: Affiliates and influencers create content featuring your product.
  These often convert better due to perceived authenticity.
- **Best strategy**: Combine both. Use seller videos for consistent baseline content
  and creator videos for reach and social proof.

## TikTok SEO

TikTok Shop has its own search engine. As TikTok increasingly becomes a search
platform (especially for Gen Z), search optimization matters.

### How TikTok Shop Search Works

TikTok Shop search indexes:
1. **Product title** (highest weight).
2. **Product description**.
3. **Product category and attributes**.
4. **Video captions and text overlays** associated with the product.
5. **Hashtags** on product-tagged videos.

### Search Ranking Factors

| Factor | Weight | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Keyword relevance in title | Very High | Primary ranking signal |
| Sales velocity | High | Recent sales and conversion rate |
| Video performance | High | Watch time, engagement on product-tagged videos |
| Product rating | Medium | Star rating and review count |
| Price competitiveness | Medium | Relative to similar products |
| Seller score | Medium | Order fulfillment, response rate |
| Recency | Low-Medium | New listings get temporary boost |

### Keyword Strategy for TikTok

TikTok search behavior is more conversational and trend-driven than Amazon or Shopee.

**Keyword sources**:
- TikTok search bar autocomplete (type product terms and note suggestions).
- TikTok Creative Center trending keywords.
- Trending video captions and hashtags in your product niche.
- "TikTok made me buy it" community trends.
- Comment sections on competitor product videos (buyers describe products in their
  own words).

**Keyword placement priority**:
1. Title (first 34 characters for preview visibility).
2. Video captions on product-tagged videos.
3. Product description.
4. Hashtags on product-tagged videos.

## Hashtag Strategy for Product Discovery

Hashtags on TikTok serve as both a categorization and discovery mechanism. While less
important than on Instagram, they still influence which audiences see your content.

### Hashtag Framework

Use a layered hashtag strategy on product-tagged videos:

| Layer | Hashtags | Volume | Purpose |
|-------|----------|--------|---------|
| Broad | 1-2 | Very high (100M+ views) | Category awareness |
| Niche | 2-3 | Medium (1M-100M views) | Target audience reach |
| Specific | 2-3 | Lower (100K-1M views) | High purchase intent |
| Branded | 1 | Variable | Brand awareness, UGC aggregation |

### Examples by Category

**Beauty product**:
```
#Beauty #SkincareRoutine #GlowUp #VitaminCSerum #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt #YourBrandName
```

**Home gadget**:
```
#HomeHacks #KitchenGadgets #CleaningTok #SmartHome #OrganizationTips #YourBrandName
```

**Fashion**:
```
#OOTD #StreetStyle #FashionFinds #SummerOutfit #AffashionablePrice #YourBrandName
```

### Hashtag Rules

- Use 4-8 hashtags per video (TikTok's algorithm does not penalize reasonable hashtag use).
- Mix broad and specific hashtags in every post.
- Monitor trending hashtags daily and incorporate relevant ones.
- Create a branded hashtag and encourage customers to use it.
- Avoid banned or flagged hashtags (check by searching the hashtag first).
- Do not use hashtags that are irrelevant to the product; algorithmic mismatch
  hurts performance.

## Affiliate and Creator Integration

TikTok Shop's affiliate program connects sellers with creators who promote products
in exchange for commission. This is one of the most powerful growth channels on the
platform.

### Setting Up Affiliate Commission

Access through TikTok Seller Center under "Affiliate Marketing":

| Commission Type | Description | Recommended Rate |
|----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Open plan | Any creator can promote; flat commission rate | 10-20% |
| Targeted plan | Invite specific creators; custom commission | 15-30% |
| Shop plan | Default commission for all products | 5-15% |

### Commission Strategy

- **New products**: Start with higher commission (20-30%) to attract creators and
  build initial sales velocity. Reduce after establishing organic ranking.
- **Established products**: Maintain 10-15% for ongoing creator promotion.
- **High-margin products**: Offer 20%+ to encourage aggressive creator promotion.
- **Low-margin products**: Minimum viable commission (5-10%); rely more on seller-created
  content.

### Finding and Recruiting Creators

1. **TikTok Creator Marketplace**: Browse creators by niche, follower count,
   engagement rate, and demographics.
2. **Affiliate contact**: Send product samples with a personalized message through
   TikTok's affiliate invitation system.
3. **Comment outreach**: Engage with creators already making content in your niche.
4. **Creator agency partnerships**: Work with MCN (Multi-Channel Network) agencies
   that manage multiple creators.

### Creator Brief Template

When sending product samples to creators, include:

- Product name and key selling points (2-3 bullets).
- Target audience description.
- Key messages to convey (without being overly prescriptive; authenticity matters).
- Hashtags to include.
- Commission rate and any bonus incentives.
- Content guidelines (what to avoid, any compliance requirements).
- Do not script the video; let the creator maintain their authentic voice.

### Affiliate Performance Tracking

Monitor these metrics in TikTok Seller Center:

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|------------------|
| Creator count | Number of active affiliates promoting your products |
| Video count | Total product-tagged videos created |
| Video views | Reach of affiliate content |
| Orders from affiliates | Direct attribution to creator content |
| Commission paid | Cost of affiliate channel |
| ROI | Revenue vs commission cost |

## Livestream Selling Integration

Livestream is a major sales channel on TikTok Shop, especially in SEA markets.

### Livestream Setup for Product Listings

- **Product showcase**: Pin products to the livestream so viewers can tap to buy.
- **Pricing strategy**: Offer livestream-exclusive pricing or bundles to create urgency.
- **Stream schedule**: Consistency matters. Stream at least 2-3 times per week during
  peak hours.
- **Peak hours by market**: Generally 7-11 PM local time on weekdays, earlier on
  weekends.

### Livestream Selling Techniques

1. **Open with energy**: First 5 minutes determine whether viewers stay. Start with
   the best-selling or most visually interesting product.
2. **Demonstrate every product**: Show the product in use, not just holding it up.
3. **Create urgency**: Limited stock callouts, flash pricing ("next 50 buyers get...").
4. **Engage the chat**: Answer questions by name, conduct polls, offer rewards for
   engagement.
5. **Pin and rotate products**: Keep the current product pinned. Rotate through the
   catalog every 10-15 minutes.
6. **Recap bestsellers**: Revisit top performers at the end of the stream for
   latecomers.

### Livestream Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | Why It Matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Average concurrent viewers | Growing week over week | Algorithmic boost threshold |
| Viewer-to-buyer conversion | 1-5% (varies by category) | Revenue efficiency |
| Average watch time | 5+ minutes | Indicates engaging content |
| Chat interaction rate | High | Drives algorithmic distribution |
| Products sold per hour | Increasing over time | Operational efficiency |
| GMV per stream | Growing trend | Overall revenue health |

## TikTok Shop Policies and Restrictions

### Prohibited Products

TikTok Shop restricts or prohibits certain product categories. Key restrictions include:

- Weapons and ammunition.
- Drugs and drug paraphernalia.
- Tobacco and e-cigarettes (policies vary by market).
- Counterfeit or trademarked goods.
- Adult content and products.
- Live animals.
- Hazardous materials.
- Products making unverified health claims.

Always check the latest TikTok Shop Seller Policies for your specific market, as
restrictions are updated frequently.

### Listing Content Restrictions

- No false or misleading claims.
- No before/after images for health or beauty products that imply guaranteed results
  (market-dependent).
- No competitor brand names or trademarks in titles or descriptions.
- No external links or contact information directing buyers off-platform.
- No pricing manipulation (inflating the "original" price to show a fake discount).

### Intellectual Property

- Must have authorization to sell branded products; brand registry or authorization
  letter required.
- User-generated content (UGC) used in listings must have creator permission.
- Product images must be original or licensed; no watermarked stock photos.

### Account Health

TikTok Shop monitors seller performance. Maintain these thresholds:

| Metric | Minimum Threshold |
|--------|------------------|
| Order fulfillment rate | 95%+ |
| Late shipment rate | Under 4% |
| Cancellation rate | Under 2% |
| Product quality return rate | Under 1% (market-dependent) |
| Response rate | 90%+ within 24 hours |
| Customer satisfaction | 4.0+ star average |

Falling below thresholds can result in listing suppression, loss of promotional
eligibility, or account suspension.

## Market Differences

TikTok Shop operates differently across markets. Key differences affect listing
strategy, content approach, and operational requirements.

### United States (US)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Language | English |
| Content style | Authentic, UGC-style; overly polished content underperforms |
| Key categories | Beauty, fashion, home, wellness, electronics accessories |
| Buyer behavior | Discovery-driven; "TikTok made me buy it" impulse purchases |
| Average order value | Higher than SEA markets |
| Fulfillment | Fulfilled by merchant or TikTok Fulfillment (varies) |
| Creator ecosystem | Large and mature; affiliate marketing well-established |
| Compliance | FTC disclosure requirements for sponsored content |
| Notes | Focus on viral potential; trending products move fast; review quality matters |

### United Kingdom (UK)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Language | English (UK spelling and terminology) |
| Content style | Similar to US; slightly more polished aesthetic accepted |
| Key categories | Fashion, beauty, home, food and drink |
| Buyer behavior | Price-conscious but brand-aware |
| Fulfillment | Domestic fulfillment expected; cross-border shipping acceptable with fast transit |
| Creator ecosystem | Growing; smaller than US but highly engaged |
| Notes | Smaller market but high AOV; localize product descriptions for UK audience |

### Southeast Asia (SEA) -- Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Language | Local language required (Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino, Bahasa Malaysia) |
| Content style | High-energy, entertainment-focused; livestream dominant |
| Key categories | Fashion, beauty, FMCG, mobile accessories, home |
| Buyer behavior | Very price-sensitive; heavy promotion and voucher culture |
| Average order value | Lower; volume-driven |
| Fulfillment | Local warehouse or cross-border (longer delivery accepted) |
| Creator ecosystem | Massive; micro-creators (1K-50K followers) drive significant volume |
| Livestream importance | Critical; can be the primary sales channel |
| Notes | Livestream selling is a much larger share of GMV in SEA than in US/UK; invest heavily in livestream |

### Cross-Market Strategy

| Factor | US/UK | SEA |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Primary discovery | Short-form video + search | Video + livestream |
| Content cadence | 3-5 videos per week | Daily videos + 2-3 livestreams per week |
| Creator strategy | Focus on mid-tier creators (50K-500K) | Micro-creators at volume (1K-50K) |
| Pricing | Premium positioning possible | Must be competitive; voucher expected |
| Language | English | Local language required for each market |
| Listing copy | Benefits-focused, conversational | Feature-list style, price-value emphasis |
| Compliance | FTC, ASA rules for claims and disclosures | Varies by country; less standardized |
