# 1688.com Sourcing Workflow

A step-by-step guide to sourcing products on 1688.com (Alibaba's domestic Chinese
wholesale marketplace). This guide is written in English but describes a Chinese-language
interface. 1688 consistently offers the lowest prices for manufactured goods because
it targets domestic Chinese buyers, bypassing the export markup built into Alibaba.com.

## 1. Account Setup and Navigation

### Registration

1. Navigate to 1688.com
2. Click the registration button (top right corner, labeled "mianfei zhuce" / free registration)
3. You will need a Chinese mobile number or Alipay account to register
4. If you do not have a Chinese mobile number, options include:
   - Use a sourcing agent who has an account
   - Use a Chinese partner or employee's account
   - Some virtual Chinese number services work, but reliability varies
5. Complete SMS verification
6. Set your account type to "buyer" (caigou shang)

### Navigation Overview

The 1688 homepage is organized into:

- **Top navigation bar**: Search box, category menu, user account
- **Category sidebar** (left): Major product categories (gongye pin = industrial,
  richangyongpin = daily necessities, fushi = apparel, etc.)
- **Main content area**: Promoted products, trending items, seasonal recommendations
- **Supplier types filter**: Factory (gongchang), Supplier (gongyingshang), Power
  Supplier (shili gongyingshang)

### Key Interface Elements (Chinese to English)

## 2. Search Strategies

### Keyword Translation

Searching effectively on 1688 requires Chinese keywords. Use this approach:

1. Start with your product name in English
2. Translate using Google Translate or DeepL (prefer DeepL for product terms)
3. Cross-reference the translation on 1688 to verify it returns relevant results
4. Collect alternative Chinese terms from the product listings you find
5. Search using multiple keyword variations

**Example -- Silicone Kitchen Spatula**:
- Direct translation: "guijiao chuchan" (silicone spatula)
- Alternative terms found on 1688: "guijiao guadao" (silicone scraper), "naigao guijiao chan" (heat-resistant silicone spatula)
- Each variation surfaces different suppliers

### Search Filters

After entering your search query, use these filters (listed left-to-right on the
results page):

| Filter | Chinese Label | How to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sort by sales | an xiaoliang paixu | Reveals which products actually sell in volume; good proxy for quality |
| Sort by price | an jiage paixu | Low to high or high to low |
| Supplier type | gongyingshang leixing | Filter for "gongchang" (factory) to bypass trading companies |
| Location | suozaidi | Filter by manufacturing hub (e.g., Yiwu for small commodities, Shenzhen for electronics, Guangzhou for apparel) |
| Price range | jiage qu jian | Set min-max to exclude obvious junk or premium items |
| MOQ | qipi | Filter by minimum order quantity |
| Verified supplier | yan chang | Suppliers with verified factory inspections |

### Advanced Search Techniques

- **Image search**: Click the camera icon in the search bar. Upload a photo of the
  product you want to source. 1688 will return visually similar products. This is
  extremely useful when you cannot find the right Chinese keyword.
- **Competitor reverse-search**: If you have a competitor's product, photograph it
  and use image search to find the original manufacturer.
- **Category browsing**: For product discovery (rather than specific sourcing), browse
  category pages. Sort by "new arrivals" (xin pin) to find trending products before
  they appear on cross-border marketplaces.

## 3. Reading Supplier Profiles

Click on any supplier name to view their store profile. Key data points:

### Business Information Panel

| Field | Chinese | What It Tells You |
|---|---|---|
| Business type | jingying moshi | "shengchan jiagong" = manufacturer; "jingxiao pipei" = trading/wholesale |
| Years on 1688 | kaitian nianxian | Longer is generally better; 5+ years indicates stability |
| Location | suozaidi | Should match the known manufacturing hub for your product category |
| Main products | zhuyao chanpin | Verify it includes your product category |
| Annual revenue | nian yingye e | Indicates scale; cross-reference with factory capacity claims |

### Transaction Data

| Metric | Chinese | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction volume | chengjiao e | Total value of completed transactions; higher is better |
| Repeat purchase rate | fugoulu | Percentage of buyers who order again; above 30% is strong; above 50% is excellent |
| Buyer count | maijiashu | Number of distinct buyers; indicates breadth of customer base |
| On-time delivery | anshi fayunlu | Should be above 95% |
| Dispute rate | jiufen lv | Should be below 1% |

### Badges and Certifications

- **shili shangjia** (Power Merchant): Indicates higher sales volume and platform trust score
- **yan chang** (Verified Factory): 1688 staff or third party visited the factory
- **chengxintong** (Integrity Pass): Multi-year membership indicating platform investment
- **niupi gongchang** (Super Factory): Top-tier factory designation with high transaction volume

### What to Watch For

- A supplier claiming to be a factory but with business type listed as "jingxiao pipei"
  (trading) is a red flag
- Very new stores (under 1 year) with extremely high transaction volumes may have
  manipulated data
- Check whether the listed location matches the factory claim -- a "factory" in a
  commercial district of a tier-1 city is likely an office/trading company

## 4. Product Page Analysis

### Price Tier Structure

1688 product pages typically show tiered pricing:

```
Quantity:  1-49    50-199   200-999   1000+
Price:     $3.50   $2.80    $2.40     $2.10
```

- First tier is essentially the sample/small order price
- The price you negotiate will typically be between the second and third tiers
- Fourth tier+ prices are achievable with consistent reorders

### Key Product Page Fields

| Section | What to Look For |
|---|---|
| **Product title** | Should match your search terms; watch for keyword stuffing |
| **Price tiers** | Compare across suppliers for the same quantity bracket |
| **MOQ (qipi)** | Listed minimum; often negotiable, especially for samples |
| **Colors/variants (yanse/guige)** | Check which variants are in stock vs. made to order |
| **Shipping** | "baoyou" = free shipping (within China); note the shipping method |
| **Lead time (fahuo qixian)** | "xianhuo" = in stock; "7 tian" = 7 days production |
| **Product details** | Material, dimensions, weight, certifications; verify against your specs |
| **Buyer reviews (pingjia)** | Read negative reviews carefully -- they reveal real quality issues |
| **Transaction history** | Recent orders tab shows actual order quantities and dates |

### Photos and Videos

- 1688 product photos are often shared across multiple sellers for the same product
- Look for photos with the supplier's own watermark as evidence they handle the product
- Videos showing the factory production line are a strong positive signal
- Request your own photos from the supplier to verify they physically have the product

## 5. Using Aliwangwang for Initial Contact

Aliwangwang (also called Wangwang or the 1688 chat tool) is the built-in messaging
system. Using it is required for serious sourcing -- suppliers expect and prefer it
over email.

### Setup

1. Download Aliwangwang desktop client (link on 1688.com, labeled "wangwang")
2. Log in with your 1688 account
3. The chat interface supports text, images, files, and voice messages

### First Contact Template

Send a message covering these points (in Chinese or English -- many suppliers on 1688
have basic English, and translation tools handle the rest):

1. Introduce yourself: "I am a [country] buyer looking for [product category]"
2. State your purpose: "I am evaluating suppliers for [product] and would like to
   discuss pricing and samples"
3. Ask specific questions:
   - "What is the MOQ for [product/variant]?"
   - "Can you provide samples? What is the sample cost and shipping time?"
   - "Are you the manufacturer or a trading company?"
   - "What certifications do you hold for this product?"
   - "What is the production lead time for an order of [quantity]?"
4. Request a price list or catalog if they handle multiple products you need

### Communication Tips

- Respond promptly. Chinese suppliers evaluate buyer seriousness based on response speed.
- Use specific numbers and product references, not vague inquiries
- Send photos of exactly what you want if you have a reference product
- Be direct about your timeline and intended order volume
- Do not negotiate price in the first message -- build rapport first
- Use voice messages if your Chinese is functional; suppliers prefer it

## 6. Price Comparison Across Suppliers

### Building a Comparison Matrix

For every product you source, compare at least 5-10 suppliers across these columns:

| Column | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Supplier name and link | Track who is who |
| Unit price at your target MOQ | Apples-to-apples comparison |
| MOQ | Some "cheap" suppliers have prohibitively high MOQs |
| Sample cost | Some charge $0, some charge 5x unit price |
| Sample shipping cost and method | Can add $20-80 depending on weight and speed |
| Domestic shipping to port | Factor into landed cost |
| Lead time (production) | Affects your inventory planning |
| Lead time (samples) | Affects your evaluation timeline |
| Factory vs. trader | Traders add margin |
| Location | Proximity to export ports reduces domestic freight |
| Repeat purchase rate | Higher rate = more consistent quality |
| Notes | Qualitative impressions from chat and profile review |

### Price Red Lines

- If the lowest price is more than 40% below the median, investigate -- it likely
  indicates inferior materials or bait pricing
- If a supplier's quoted price is significantly above the 1688 listed price, they
  are adding hidden margin or quoting for a different specification
- Always convert prices to a consistent currency and unit basis for comparison

## 7. Placing Sample Orders

### Sample Order Process on 1688

1. Agree on sample terms via Aliwangwang (price, quantity, shipping method)
2. The supplier may create a custom listing (called "zhuanpin lianjie" or dedicated
   link) for your sample order at the agreed price
3. Alternatively, they may ask you to order the minimum quantity from the regular
   listing and note "yangpin" (sample) in the order notes
4. Complete payment (see payment methods below)
5. The supplier ships the sample within China first
6. If you are outside China, you will need a forwarding agent or consolidator to
   receive and re-ship internationally

### Sample Order Tips

- Order 2-3 pieces minimum (one to test, one to keep as reference, one as backup)
- For multiple variants, order at least one of each
- Ask the supplier to pack samples as they would pack a production order -- this
  tests their packaging quality
- Request that the supplier include a packing list and any relevant certificates
  inside the sample shipment
- Pay attention to how the supplier handles the sample order process: disorganized
  sample handling predicts disorganized production

### Forwarding and Consolidation

If you are not in China, you need to get samples out of the country:

- **Forwarding agents**: Services that receive packages at a Chinese warehouse and
  re-ship internationally. Popular options include 4PX, Yanwen, and various Taobao
  forwarding agents.
- **Consolidation**: If you are ordering samples from multiple suppliers, have them
  all ship to a single forwarding warehouse. This saves on international shipping.
- **Shipping methods**: Express (DHL/FedEx/UPS) for speed (3-7 days), ePacket/Yanwen
  for economy (10-20 days), sea freight is not practical for samples.

## 8. Payment Methods

### Alipay (zhifubao)

- The default payment method on 1688
- Requires a verified Chinese Alipay account
- International Alipay accounts have limited functionality on 1688
- If you have a Chinese bank card linked to Alipay, this is the most straightforward
  method

### Cross-Border Payment Options

For international buyers without Chinese bank accounts:

| Method | How It Works | Fees |
|---|---|---|
| **Sourcing agent** | Agent pays with their Alipay; you pay the agent via bank transfer | Agent markup (3-8%) |
| **1688 cross-border channel** | 1688's official international buyer program supports some foreign payment methods | Varies, typically 1-3% |
| **PayPal to supplier** | Some suppliers accept PayPal for sample orders (not common for production) | PayPal fees (3-4%) |
| **Western Union / bank transfer** | For larger orders, suppliers may accept direct bank transfer | Bank wire fees ($15-40) |
| **Alibaba Trade Assurance** | If the supplier also has an Alibaba.com store, negotiate on 1688 but pay via Alibaba Trade Assurance for escrow protection | Alibaba fees vary |

### Payment Safety

- Never pay via personal WeChat transfer for business orders -- there is zero buyer
  protection
- For first orders, prefer methods with escrow or dispute resolution
- Keep all payment receipts and transaction records
- For orders over $5000, consider Trade Assurance or Letter of Credit regardless of
  convenience

## 9. Quality Indicators on 1688

### Platform-Provided Signals

| Indicator | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction volume (chengjiao e) | Total historical sales value | High -- real demand signal |
| Repeat rate (fugoulu) | % of buyers who order again | Highest -- best quality proxy |
| On-time rate (anshi fahuo lv) | % of orders shipped on schedule | High -- operational reliability |
| Dispute rate (jiufen lv) | % of orders with disputes | High (inverse) -- below 1% is good |
| Response time (huifu sudu) | Average message response time | Medium -- operational signal |
| Factory verification (yan chang) | Third-party factory inspection | Medium-High |
| Years active (kaitian nianxian) | Account age on 1688 | Medium |

### Buyer Review Analysis

1688 reviews are structured differently from Amazon reviews:

- **Star ratings**: 1-5 stars for product quality, logistics, and service separately
- **Photo reviews (daishou pingjia)**: Most valuable -- show actual received products
- **Repeat buyer indicators**: Reviews from buyers with multiple orders from the same
  supplier carry more weight
- **Negative review patterns**: Look for recurring complaints (sizing inconsistency,
  color mismatch, packaging damage). One complaint is anecdotal; three similar
  complaints indicate a systemic issue.

### Independent Quality Verification

Do not rely solely on 1688 platform data:

- Cross-reference supplier claims on Tianyancha or Qichacha (business verification)
- If the supplier claims certifications, request copies and verify with issuing bodies
- Use Baidu Maps satellite view to verify factory addresses
- Check if the supplier has presence on other platforms (Alibaba, Made-in-China) and
  compare consistency of their claims

## 10. Common Pitfalls and Workarounds

### Pitfall: Bait-and-Switch Pricing

**Problem**: The listed price on 1688 is for a stripped-down version of the product
(basic material, no packaging, no labeling). When you specify your requirements,
the price increases significantly.

**Workaround**: Always get a written quote for your exact specifications before
comparing prices. Ask: "This price is for which material/specification/packaging?"

### Pitfall: Photo Mismatch

**Problem**: Product photos on 1688 are often professional shots that do not represent
actual product quality. Multiple sellers may use the same photos.

**Workaround**: Always request the supplier to send real photos (shi pai tu) of the
actual product they will ship. Insist on photos with their business card or a
handwritten note with your order reference for proof.

### Pitfall: MOQ Manipulation

**Problem**: Listed MOQ says 10 pieces, but when you order 10 pieces the supplier
says "sorry, the real MOQ is 500 for this variant."

**Workaround**: Confirm MOQ for your specific variant, color, and specification via
chat before ordering. Screenshot the confirmation.

### Pitfall: Domestic vs. Export Quality

**Problem**: Products sold on 1688 are made for the Chinese domestic market and may
not meet export standards (safety certifications, labeling requirements, material
restrictions like CPSIA for children's products).

**Workaround**: Explicitly ask about export certifications. Provide the specific
standard requirements for your target market (e.g., CE marking for EU, FCC for US
electronics, CPSIA for US children's products). Budget for third-party testing.

### Pitfall: Communication Breakdown

**Problem**: Language barrier causes misunderstandings about specifications, and
the wrong product gets manufactured.

**Workaround**: Always use written specifications with diagrams and reference images.
Confirm understanding by asking the supplier to restate your requirements in their
own words. Use a professional translator for critical specification documents rather
than relying on machine translation alone.

### Pitfall: Shipping Surprises

**Problem**: Domestic shipping is included ("baoyou") but the supplier ships via the
slowest method, or the forwarding cost from the domestic warehouse to your country
is much higher than expected.

**Workaround**: Clarify the exact shipping method before ordering. Get weight and
volumetric dimensions from the supplier to estimate forwarding costs independently.
Use a forwarding cost calculator before committing to sample orders.
